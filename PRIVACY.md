# Privacy

## The website

The website at https://www.husbandoutworld.com/ has no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Everything on it runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) sees your IP address when it sends you the page.

**Settings.** The Settings menu (the gear, top right) saves your choices (Public or Behind The Scenes, Language, Theme, Text size and Vraki mode) in your browser's local storage, under the key `hoow`, so they are remembered next time. The webcomic reader remembers the episode you were reading, and the ones you finished, under `hoow-webcomic`, so the Webcomic page can offer "Continue". None of this leaves your device; clearing the site's data removes it.

**Offline copies.** So the site (and the app, if you install it) opens without a connection, a service worker saves copies of the home page and of every page you open, with their styles, scripts and fonts, in your browser's Cache Storage. Pictures and anything from other websites are never saved. The copies stay on your device and are never sent anywhere; clearing the site's data removes them.

**Install app.** The Install app button (Settings › App) only asks your browser to install the site. It saves nothing.

**Stats for nerds.** The debug page (Settings › DEBUG, `/debug/`) shows what your browser tells the page about itself: browser and device, screen, storage use, connection, page timings. It reads this only to show it to you on that page. Nothing is saved and nothing is sent anywhere; the page only asks this website for its build file (`stats.json`) and times three small requests to it.

The website is also offered in other languages. Those versions were translated ahead of time by AI (Claude) from the English original and say so in a banner at the top.
