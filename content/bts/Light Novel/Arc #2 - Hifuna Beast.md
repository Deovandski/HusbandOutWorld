# Book 5 - Wild Side › Prologue

#### Neferteri and Nina, back at Sanbi Castle

- [Neferteri](/lore/characters/#neferteri-sanbi-royal) tells [Nina](/lore/characters/#nina-sanbi-royal) that [Eori](/lore/characters/#eori-sanbi-royal) has been behaving badly, and she needs all the support she can get. Nina says she'll help, then asks why they're hiding behind nari gel.
- Neferteri says it's super secret research that other kingdoms would try to steal, so it has to stay super secret, even from Father. Nina giggles: "Okay!"
- Neferteri is trying to break her own patterns by getting even more involved with Nina. She knows her narcissistic tendencies, and realizes she's repeating the same controlling bullshit her own parents did, but her conviction that she can do things differently speaks louder: that somehow she can steer the ship back from how far it has drifted from the reality she designed.

# Book 5 - Wild Side › Chapter 1 - Prey

#### Harry #7-A runs through the forest

- Autumn colors, the leaves starting to fall. Harry runs, and runs, and runs some more; he doesn't even remember for how long. His legs grow heavy, then his whole body, until he loses his balance, crashes into a tree and falls.
- Breathing hard, he feels the pain the adrenaline had been holding back dequeue into his mind all at once. His feet are the worst: they've been leaking blood for a while, leaving a trail that quickly blends into the sea of leaves on the chilly wind.
- Sticks and stones are encrusted in his soles like jewels, painful, macabre ones. Pulling one out feels like a needle through the bone, followed by a warm, pounding tickle as his accelerated healing kicks in: he laughs, cries and agonizes all at once. He cleans his feet one stone at a time, but the healing gets slower with each one.

#### Harry #7-A eats a fruit the Bagarisas scream at him about

- Hand on his stomach, he finds a fruit that looks too delicious to be true, and eats his fill. He feels amazing, like never before.
- Forest all around. *What should I do? Where do I go?* He sees the sisters again in his mind, and remembers the moment Katsumi kicked him out.
- A sinking feeling sets in, getting worse with every step: thick saliva, a clenched throat, a turning stomach. He holds onto a tree and pukes his guts out, weaker than before, then wanders on, aimless, under graying skies.

#### Harry #7-A shelters at the foot of a big tree as night and rain set in

![][image1]

- The cold rain feels like ice cubes trying to pierce his skin. The fire on his fingertips is his only salvation, until a gust of wind breaks his concentration and puts it out. He's shaking too much to light it again; the bitter cold takes over his mind every time. Wozen eyes watch from the background.
- Something moves on his left. "AGRHGHRHHRHGR!" A toothy mouth flashes in front of him and takes him to the ground, and he feels warmth again: his own blood, squirting from his neck. *I don't wanna die! I don't wanna die!* He flails and hits the beast right in the eyes.
- The beast jumps back, then claws open his gut. His intestines spill out onto his skin; the throbbing pain fades, and his whole body warms up. With his last strength, Harry puts his hand on the beast and screams, or rather gurgles, as the flame takes over his fingers and flashes up his forearm before going out.
- His arm slumps to the ground, and so does his head. Harry Sanbi dies in the middle of butt-fuck nowhere.

# Book 5 - Wild Side › Chapter 2 - Penitence

#### The sisters walk the road at sunrise, Katsumi pulling Katsuki along

- [Katsuki](/lore/characters/#katsuki-flatland-long-blue) pulls away and starts walking back. [Katsumi](/lore/characters/#katsumi-flatland-long-blue) grabs her. "Too late now! Harry's dead meat! He's not gonna survive the night."
- Katsuki throws weak punches; Katsumi takes them like nothing. "Don't you realize we had to leave him behind? He became luggage the moment he hit the ground hard!"
- "One. No! Two. Harry. Savior."
- Katsumi holds her hands. "All right, cut it out! Harry is not a savior. He's a nobody like us, and what do nobodies do? They save themselves!"
- Silent tears pour down Katsuki's face as her hands calm down. "One. Trust broken. Two. No sister."
- "What are you talking about? I am your sister! Listen here! I just want us to be free! [Emelia](/lore/characters/#emelia-lakeeye-rich-flower), [Arukia](/lore/characters/#arukia-long-bridge) and all the other Red Winga people are full of shit!" Katsuki hangs her head and goes silent. "There's no going back now. C'mon. Let's go."

#### Shady Black Magina riders on Eguas

- Katsumi mouths off and draws the bandits' ire. When she refuses to come work at their farm and they realize the two are Red Winga whores, they draw their swords.

#### The sisters flee the bandits in the rain

- Katsumi drags Katsuki along until Katsuki pulls free and stops dead. "ONE! ENOUGH! TWO! KILL—ME!" Katsumi slaps her and pulls, but she won't budge, and walks toward the oncoming bandits.
- "All right! FINE! SUIT YOURSELF!" Katsumi runs, biting her lips in anger, but her sister's face keeps haunting her. She trips into the mud, too weak to get up, and feels something sharp poke her back.
- A hoarse voice: "I like 'em tight!" Thunder roars across the sky.
- "Hey, fuckface! Can't you see they're Red Winga property?" "So what? They're whores anyway!" "Red Winga whores! Can't you imagine how much info they have?" "And?" "And we can get information out of them, you retarded Brunto. Let's go! I knew I'd spotted gold."

# Book 5 - Wild Side › Chapter 3 - Payback's a bitch and a half.

#### The Bagarisas heal Harry #8-A

- Harry wakes to a Bagarisa kissing him and pushes it away, screaming: they kind of stink. It oinks at him, as if complaining about the bad treatment.
- He's against the same tree, his shirt in tatters. Everything is back where it belongs, but he's even thinner now, and so are two of the Bagarisas. *They healed me!* He hugs the two underweight ones. "I'm sorry! I'm sorry! I didn't know!" Understanding oinks, if such a thing can be inferred.
- The others, a female among them, cast a spell at the foliage to curse the fruit down. One crashes onto Harry's hand and lands by his groin. Remembering the last fruit, he watches the Bagarisas eat first, only the white inside, then follows suit, and almost cums from how good it is. Together, they strip the tree bare.

#### Harry and the Bagarisas settle in to sleep, full

- They still stink, but not so bad anymore; maybe his nose stopped working, but who cares. He feels amazing comfort and protection: the cold wind now feels like an A/C breeze keeping a cozy blanket just right.
- One Bagarisa stands up, its back lit on fire. The others notice too, and get defensive.

#### Two Wozens attack the Bagarisas

- In a flash, the Bagarisas and Wozens are at each other. Harry watches the Bagarisa that healed him agonize over its death, just like he did, and anger and hatred brew inside him.
- He screams, tackles a Wozen from behind, and his hand lights up, burning it alive; he kills it by burning its face and mouth.

#### Harry #8-A cooks by the fire

- Chunks of meat and fur on a makeshift spit, a bloodied stone on the side. Disgusted, he eats it anyway, spitting out fur. The Bagarisas want some, so he gives them all of it, and they clean their plate.
- "Can you guys help me out of here?" Oinks. *Wait. Where would I even go?* More oinks: they point in a direction, and Harry follows.

# Book 5 - Wild Side › Chapter 4 - Unwanted job

#### Kibe and his guard captains go through their daily work

- [Kibe](/lore/characters/#kibe-sanbi-royal): "That's it. You can all go now." The captains stand up, refusing to leave. "What? We're done with the city safety debriefing."
- One of them steps forward. "We want you to retire." "Sure. How do I do that?"
- The room goes silent, then fills with murmurs about what needs to happen. One confident voice in the back cuts through with a sharp clearing of the throat: "According to Sanbi law, only an apprentice can replace a courtroom scientist." It's [Huneo](/lore/characters/#huneo-sanbi-royal).
- "All right then, you're now my apprentice." Kibe glances at the clouds, then back at the young man. "Now you're the courtroom scientist."
- Huneo shakes his head. "You're missing a few steps. First, the three others need to approve a new apprentice. Second, a public hair paint must happen. Third, you need to train me up to par. Fourth, you resign publicly, and I take over the post at a public event, too. Those last two can be the same event."
- "Let's do that, then. Let's go get the approval. Everyone else, just go already." "Not so easy. You need to write a letter summarizing all my qualities, and why I'm the one." Kibe groans. "How do I write this letter?" "I can show you."

#### Kibe and Huneo meet with Neferteri, Itagiba and Eiri

- Everyone approves except Neferteri, who drills Huneo with questions. [Eiri](/lore/characters/#eiri-sanbi-royal) asks if she suspects him; Neferteri says it's all happening too fast.
- She clears her throat. "The law was followed to the letter, albeit hastily. My vote has no bearing on the matter. Shall we continue?"
- Huneo: "Arranging the ceremony. I'd like it done in two days." [Itagiba](/lore/characters/#itagiba-sanbi-royal): "That soon? There won't be enough time to advertise it." Neferteri stays silent, still suspicious. Huneo points at his massive list of reforms. "There's no time to be jerking around."
- Eiri asks for a day off to see [Coema](/lore/characters/#coema-sanbi-half-cruro) off. Neferteri can't stand hearing the name; she grips her badge and tells Eiri not to bring Coema here.

#### Kibe and Huneo at the ceremony

- Kibe: "I'm out." Someone in the crowd: "Man! You suck!" A tall, strong Darfaries: "Yeah! I'll give you an Amethyst if you can suck me as much as you sucked at this job! Hahaha!" Kibe pauses, almost as if taking a mental picture of him, before leaving the stage.
- Huneo launches into a long speech, until the crowd cuts him off: "Sanbi shit! Are you gonna talk all day or what? I got a fish to gut!" He skips several pages. "Thank you all, and—and I hope to earn your trust." The crowd claps, erratically but energetically, not quite sure what to make of this uptight Forway lookalike.
- "Just—just one more thing. I'm nominating Kibe as my apprentice." Kibe, walking off in the distance, thinks his ears just stopped working. "Kibe is exceptional at following specific directions, so he'd be my perfect right hand. On which note, I'll also officially introduce a vote to rename the position." The crowd stares at Kibe. "What?" Huneo smiles: "Pay will be the same as an apprentice's." Kibe shudders.

# Book 5 - Wild Side › Chapter 5 - Home sweet home, kinda.

#### Harry #8-B fights wildlife alongside the Bagarisas

- Harry and the Bagarisas are getting each other's rhythm. Two Bagarisas die in the fight, and the others revive them. Harry uses the Bagarisas' exploding forward power.
- A Bagarisa heals Harry, and he cuddles it. "Thank you! I love you guys!" Happy oinks. Then he gets sad again: he's still stuck in the forest.

#### Harry #8-B stumbles onto an abandoned shack

- Near sundown, the Bagarisas find someone's remains not far from the shack and start chewing on them. Harry realizes it was a person and tells them to stop. The clothes are an older woman's. He calls inside, but no one answers.

#### Inside the shack

- Harry lights the fost stone, and the Bagarisas all flee outside, spooked; he tries to play with them, but they won't come back.
- The books and things inside are deteriorated. The only map he finds doesn't show where he is. He sighs, spots a fishing rod, and goes to sleep.

# Book 5 - Wild Side › Chapter 6 - Contract Nullification

#### Eori, alone in a room, tries to free herself

- [Morika](/lore/characters/#morika-tolerideme) comes in to feed her; she gets aggressive, and it turns violent. He knocks her out. Nobody can really deal with her, so they pour Black Honey down her throat.

#### Eori, Neferteri and Morika

![][image2]

- Neferteri sits across from Eori, adventurers standing by her, an empty seat at her side. Eori stares at her with some of [Yacamim](/lore/characters/#yacamim-sanbi-royal) and [Maiara](/lore/characters/#maiara-sanbi-royal)'s look. "So what now?"
- "Princess—daughter Eori. My daughter Eori. When I said Harry would be under your care, I meant using him as a tool to further our scientific knowledge, and our kingdom into the future."
- "I gave you a fuckload of new nari gel! What about it?"
- "Going by the contract alone, you're delivering double the minimum required. But this is no longer about our contract; it's about your relationship with Harry."
- Eori jumps up. "I NEVER SAID I LOVED HIM!" The guards step forward; she clenches her fists and sits back down, holding back a sea of emotions. Neferteri raises a hand, the guards step back, and she lets the awkward silence thicken.
- "I am the Queen of Sanbi, but above all, I'm the mother of four princesses. I trusted you with an important task despite my doubts about your ability to care for others. But your inability to control your emotions turned out just as unacceptable as Maiara's… and Yacamim's." They stare each other down, until Neferteri closes her eyes. "Princess Nina, please come in."

#### Nina reads the contract to Eori, with Neferteri's help

- Nina comes in holding a contract, smiling ear to ear, climbs onto a chair and, chest puffed out, unrolls it, trying to sound like a guard reading a sentence: "With the afore—mentioned clause—sules, Queen Neferteri shall terminate the contract, and the research funding."
- Neferteri calmly applauds. "Thank you, Princess Nina. Based on y—" Eori feels the end of the line coming; she bursts into tears, crying like a child caught stealing candy.
- Neferteri stands and hugs her. "As Queen of Sanbi, I hereby relieve you of your research duties." Tears run down her face as the memory of a dead fetus creeps back. "I'm sorry, Eori. I should have known."
- "Harry? I love him. I want him!" "The search quest has been created." "Thank you, Mom. What will happen to the contracts I signed?" "I'll deal with them. Nina, shall we all put our disguises on and go back to the castle?" "Yeahhh!" A crying Nina joins in a group hug.

# Book 5 - Wild Side › Epilogue

#### Neferteri with Morika, Namia and Posaqui

- "Are we in the clear?" [Namia](/lore/characters/#namia-sundeath): "I don't hear anyone. Go ahead, my queen."
- Morika has finished burying [Gabiro](/lore/characters/#gabiro-sanbi-royal)'s research. [Posaqui](/lore/characters/#posaqui-sanbi): "Yessu! I laid a total of thirty-five misdirections for the adventurers." Namia: three adventurers stumbled onto where [Sinoi](/lore/characters/#sinoi-sanbi-east) was held captive, but found nothing new.
- Neferteri: from now on, they'll let the quest go stale and give her daughter's heart time to heal. Their next orders: find Harry and Sinoi. All three are to go fully back to their adventurer roles and cast a wide net across the kingdom. She hands out the only badge they have, which is Harry's, and cuts their stipend in half: the new courtroom scientist is relentless about probing Eiri for spending records.
- She also wants them to look into Arukia's disappearance: the more she thinks about it, the more intertwined Arukia's actions seem. But they're to put their own safety above hers, since nobody knows what Arukia, or whoever controls her, really is.
- Namia apologizes: she was so sure of her findings when she first observed Arukia. Neferteri: "I don't doubt your expertise, Namia. Arukia was simply too clever at disguising who she truly was."
