# WIP Items

Ideas for the arc, not yet in order.

#### Sora washes Harry's back

- Harry has fun. [Eiri](/lore/characters/#eiri-sanbi-royal) watches them while she fills him in on everything that happened, Huneo included.
- They probe his relationship with [Eori](/lore/characters/#eori-sanbi-royal). He lies and says it didn't work out. They probe even more, but Harry won't say what actually happened.

#### Harry becomes a Pearl adventurer

- A small ceremony, then drinks with Eiri, [Sora](/lore/characters/#sora-sanbi-blue-glass), [Itagiba](/lore/characters/#itagiba-sanbi-royal), the other maids and [Morika](/lore/characters/#morika-tolerideme). [Neferteri](/lore/characters/#neferteri-sanbi-royal) is nowhere to be seen.
- Itagiba gets drunk and asks Harry a favor: find out whether he had any children under the name Juno Triangle. He also tells him to enjoy the adventurer life a bit before meeting up with… (the note stops here).

#### Morika gets promoted to Amethyst (or Aquamarine) by claiming Harry's search quest

- He gets paid for Harry's quest and spends it all on his debts, which brings him closer to ending his contract.

#### Huneo and a Sanbi captain talk about Neferteri's odd behavior

- [Huneo](/lore/characters/#huneo-sanbi-royal) is torn: he was invading her privacy, but he also found a hint of [Gabiro](/lore/characters/#gabiro-sanbi-royal)'s research, and that she was trying to hide it.
- He ends up confronting Neferteri about it, and she sees a chance to get rid of him.

#### Juno Triangle, Amethyst adventurer

- Long ago, before he met Neferteri, Itagiba may have fathered children under a fake name. He wants to find out who they are, and how they're doing. (One of them is [Kaiana](/lore/characters/#kaiana-forway-cliff-jumper).)

#### A Cruro quest

- A Cruro attacks Harry. Another Cruro falls in love with him, because he smells like nothing it has ever smelled before.
- Harry has to get out of marrying the tribe leader's daughter. Then another tribe invades, looking for the smelly man.
- Harry escapes in the confusion with the bag of pearls, as the quest asked, and gets a bit of money for it.

#### Neferteri gets rid of Huneo

- Huneo is removed from his position. He comes to despise Neferteri, and leaves for Forway to join another kingdom.

#### Katsumi becomes a celebrity by fucking like a legend

- Word of her spreads all over the world.

#### Passing through Rebula

- Harry says hi to everyone, and thanks them for their help.

#### Harry and the Embassy guy drink way too much

- The Embassy guy orders "the special", and Harry follows along. It's clearly something strong, and Harry's memory lapses.
- He wakes up with memories trickling back, the Embassy guy on the bed next to his, in Long-Bridge, halfway to Forway.
- The prostitute wakes up and asks who's paying for the night of leisure. Harry asks how much it all cost.
  - "13 Amethysts."
  - "Ohhh. What if I don't have all that money?"
  - "Then you'll work for us until it's paid off. Fun isn't free."
- A girl wakes the Embassy guy: "That Pearl loser doesn't have enough money. Are you gonna pay or not?"
  - "I have 3 Amethysts and 5 Pearls. Wait, where are we again?" "Long-Bridge." "Then I have 2 Amethysts to give."
  - The courtesan grabs him by the balls. "I said 13 Amethysts."
  - "And I said I work at the Forway Embassy. Do you want to stand in front of me, and my job?"
  - Another girl takes the whole bag and walks out. "Your money stays. You two, get out, right now."
- Waiting in town for Forway guards to pass by, the Embassy guy teaches Harry the power of Forway. The guards take them on horseback toward Sanbi, but Harry still has to find his own way back.
- On the road, Wozens attack the guards, drawn by Harry's body odor. They beat the Wozens and cook them for dinner to save money: "limited budget and all", though really, the guards gambled away most of their budget.
- Harry learns about fallen adventurers and how the revival system works, and that some think it's basically slavery. Another guard cuts that one off, since there's an actual Embassy man with them; the Embassy guy says he didn't hear anything, and that the problem is above his pay grade.
- Not all Forway guards are ex-adventurers, but a good portion are.

#### Nina brings a request from Neferteri to take her to Tolerideme

- The stamp is fake. Guards hunt Harry down, until Neferteri shows up to sort it out.
- Harry ends the ordeal by slapping her butt in front of everyone.

#### A pile of Winga mail waits for Harry at the embassy

- All from Eori, all love letters. Harry isn't sure what to do with them.
- The Embassy guy: "Don't stick your dick in crazy. It may feel good, but that's what you get." Harry laughs.

#### Harry finds the sisters through a poster of Katsumi in Forway

- He'll meet them in Flatland, and help take down the [Red Winga](/lore/factions/#red-winga).
- [Katsumi](/lore/characters/#katsumi-flatland-long-blue) is pregnant and wants a peaceful life. She apologizes to Harry for her sister's actions, and Harry is just glad they found a new life.
- Harry meets [Ryo](/lore/characters/#ryo-flatland-long-blue), who connects the dots: "So you were the flame that made Eori pump out nari gel like crazy! Are you going back to her? If you do, I want to make a deal right now. Her nari gel was awesome!"

#### Harry finds Arukia on Endlessfog

- [Arukia](/lore/characters/#arukia-long-bridge) is hiding out with her Bruntos. She decides to take revenge on the Red Winga.

#### The Blue Wozens attack Harry

- [Kozaku](/lore/characters/#kozaku-forway)'s Blue Wozens were hired by the Red Winga to bring Harry to them, dead.

#### A man tries to kill Harry for sleeping with his wife

- [Sinoi](/lore/characters/#sinoi-sanbi-east), a.k.a. Fake Harry, did it.

#### Harry joins an offensive against Bahyum Castle, and his bond with Magi deepens

- Morika comes in full body armor.
- They head to [Bahyum Castle](/lore/lands/): its mood maxed out a few days ago, and the adventurers need all the help they can get.
- Harry asks around, and a nerd in the group explains the ancient species that built the castle to study souls and Walking Heart stones.
- Harry and a bunch of adventurers gather in the half-destroyed adventurer town, pair up and follow the orders of more experienced adventurers.
- A rush of undead pours out of the castle toward the village. A horn sounds, and everyone attacks the incoming wave, but the castle's mood doesn't drop. [Magi](/lore/characters/#magi-blood-blue-flower-red-bone) starts dozing off.
- The adventurers lick their wounds and brace for the worst, since the mood still isn't dropping. A few hours later, another horn: a wave of undead led by five commanders. Many adventurers die.
- When Magi suddenly collapses, Harry reaches out to her, and a Soul Energy strike blows his arm off instead.
- Morika's armor is damaged, revealing a bit of him.
- Two Sapphire adventurers, nearly dying themselves, manage to kill the aberrations. The castle's mood starts to drop, and people celebrate.

#### A forest where people disappear overnight

- People who spend the night in it tend to vanish forever, without a trace.

#### The Red Winga take Dalila

- Because [Dalila](/lore/characters/#dalila-broken-tree) spilled Harry's secrets.

#### Morika marries the second princess

- A world event happens, and the main character is powerless. A hero rises, because he wants the princess. The main character loves her, but realizes he's a nobody next to a true hero.
- The king says he'll give the princess to the hero if the hero completes the world event, but he doesn't say which princess. Magi plays along too, since she knows that way the princess can't try to marry the main character anymore.
- It turns out to be the second princess, not the third: the king never said which. The king laughs; everyone is in shock. The second princess grabs the guy and leaves the room, and minutes later, the whole castle is shaking.
- Later that day, Magi, the main character and the third princess are eating, and the princess chews Magi out for her scheming. Meanwhile, the hero and the second princess come in for dinner too, the hero in a state of bliss from everything the second princess did to him.

- Haley uses Vine Enhance to call for a volunteer. Only one can be chosen, so they all compete to see who's the most cat-like.
- They go somewhere that mixes a cat with a human. Harry: "Why not a Wozefaries?" The Wozefaries becomes a catgirl, but her intellect drops to a cat's, and now Harry has to take care of her. Maybe someone else in the house gets really close to the cat, and they bond?
