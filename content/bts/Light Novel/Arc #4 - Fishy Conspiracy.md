# Book 12 - “Missing” People › Chapter 1 - Pulling Arukia’s Heartstrings

- [Arukia](/lore/characters/#arukia-long-bridge), hiding somewhere in [Endlessfog](/lore/cities/#endlessfog), hoes in the sun. She stops for a second to watch a passing boat and wonders how long she's been here. Maybe it's for the better: no more Red Winga, no more Harry, no more anyone. Just her.
- She can't even face her own parents, if they're even still alive after what she did to the Red Winga. She knew where they were and what the Red Winga could do, but she still went through with it, and fled it all. She hoes even harder, and then hears a cry.

#### Arukia finds an injured Bruntos

- She thinks about what to do with it, fights with herself, and decides to keep it. It hugs her and climbs up onto her neck. Arukia isn't sure what to do, and stays kind of cold toward it. ([Bruntos](/lore/beasts/#bruntos) are in Beasts.)

#### In her quarters, Arukia puts the Bruntos to sleep on the floor

- The Bruntos climbs onto the bed; she puts it back on the floor. It cries, and the people around her get pissed, so she puts it on the bed to shut it up.

#### Arukia sells goods at the market

- A man shouting advertisements in the background suddenly calls out, "Arukia, we miss you!", and moves on. A wave of emotions hits Arukia as she remembers how life used to be. She cries, and the baby Bruntos hugs her. She hugs it back.

#### Arukia gets ready for bed, holding the baby Bruntos

- She knows that if she shows up, she'll be discovered and bring harm to her family. Who could she turn to? She fucked over everyone she knew, burning all her bridges. She hugs her baby Bruntos tight.

# Book 12 - “Missing” People › Chapter 3 - Magi Blood Blue-Flower-Red-Bone

#### Magi wakes up against a wall; an old Cruro shows her a handwritten note: "You okay?" (her green bag isn't as full as before)

- People are staring at this pink adventurer. [Magi](/lore/characters/#magi-blood-blue-flower-red-bone) jumps up—"I'M SORRY!"—and takes off running, clutching her green bag as she hears them murmur, baffled. "HEY! YOU LEFT YOUR BOW AND BAG!" She stops: she's holding her green bag, but she left everything else behind. She walks back in shame.
- An older lady scolds her as she hands over her things. "Lady! You need to be more careful! There are bandits around!" The girl in pink takes her bow, attaches it to her backpack and puts it on. "I'm sorry!"
- She bows and starts off, then weighs her green bag, opens it and peeks inside: several small Fost stones, about the size of a piece of gum. She turns around. "Did I drop some, by any chance?" The older lady looks away. "I'm sure they're all there, sweetie." Then she looks back. "Unless… are you saying we're bandits?" Magi feels an ominous presence from them. Someone at the back: "What do you say? Let's call it a rescue fee?"
- She backs away quickly and keeps walking down the main road. As she walks, her eyes grow heavy again. She slaps herself, drawing everyone's attention; thinking she's crazy, they all leave her be. The girl in pink wakes up a bit and speed-walks on.

#### Magi turns in her quest at the Embassy

- Magi anxiously waits while the Embassy clerk counts the Fost stones. She watches his hand move each small stone from one pile to the other, her eyes following the repetitive motion, growing wearier with each one. She tries not to blink at all costs, but her eyelids fight her tooth and nail. She gives in to one quick blink.

#### The clerk hands her about 2 Pearls; Magi asleep in a seat with her backpack and bow

- A poke on her cheek wakes her: the same clerk, handing her a measly two Pearls. "Hey, miss. You didn't return all the stones." She holds out her hands and he places them there, then shows her the contract: she got more Fost stones from them than she brought back. Magi clutches the two Pearls to her chest, holding back her tears. The clerk waves the contract; she signs it.
- "Going to sign another? I have a few." Magi: "Whatever's safest, for the most money." "More roadwork, I presume?" Magi nods, holding the two Pearls. She pulls out her badge, makes it light up, and hands the two Pearls back. "Can you please add these to the Blue-Flower-Red-Bone guild?" The clerk doesn't take them. Instead, he smiles. "I'll get the contracts arranged. Please excuse me."
- Magi sits down again and looks at her tattered clothes. "I'm sorry, [Jonem](/lore/characters/#jonem-blood-blue-flower-red-bone)." (Jonem is her little brother.)

#### Magi in front of Yosuke's restaurant; Harry smiles at her

- "Sorry, adventurer, but we're about to close." A white-haired stranger smiles at her. She holds her cold hands against her chest. "Do you have any [Breadplates](/lore/flora/#carbey--economic-value)?" The white-haired guy's smile disappears. He looks inside at some patrons finishing their meals. "We might have some soon. We've got plenty of empty tables too, so come in and warm up."
- He leads the pink adventurer to a table in the corner, looks around suspiciously, then speaks loudly: "I'm Desmond! How can I serve you today?" Then, quieter: "You're supposed to at least buy something, but don't tell anybody, or I'll be in trouble."
- Magi smiles, but her sad expression blurs the smile. She watches Desmond, the white-haired guy, help someone with their bill, grab something to drink, then bring all the leftover Breadplates over to her. *He looks familiar—* Desmond starts chowing down on them too. The pink adventurer looks at him, confused, then angry. "Are you mocking me, like everyone else?"

#### Harry, confused, and Magi sit across from each other, eating Breadplates together

- "What do you mean?" She lifts the free bread. "This! You're eating this too!" "There are no leftovers, so this is what I'm eating." "Don't you make, like, fifteen Pearls a day?" "Yeah, but I live here too, so I pay ten a day, and I'm using the rest to pay off this uniform." She chows down on more bread. *And I made 2 Pearls in the past two days.*
- Desmond sees her chewing slow down and her head dip forward. With the next blink, she's out cold, falling forward onto the table and spilling her drink. "Hey? Are you okay?" No response. He touches her soft shoulder; nothing. *That doesn't look comfortable.* He grabs a pillow, and as he tilts her back onto it, she startles awake and slaps his hand away.
- Desmond leans back. "You okay? You just passed out cold." Magi glares at him. "Don't touch me!" "DESMOND! STOP FLIRTING AND CLEAN THIS SHIT UP!" Rimir's scream from the kitchen door breaks the awkwardness filling the room. "Sorry, pink adventurer. I'll grab you another drink. You can stay here until I finish cleaning up the floor."
- Magi takes another bite. She watches Desmond as she eats, and something about him seems familiar, and it isn't the Breadplate she's always eating because it's free.

#### Magi, alone, at an end table by the wall in a loud tavern

- Older lady: "Not ordering anything again, pink adventurer?" Magi shakes her head. "Poor you." Magi ends up falling asleep, and suddenly remembers the Gandew duel at the end of Vol 1, which she watched from the background (she has been in the background of the illustrations): she saw Harry's old, fatter form. He has changed a lot, but he still holds some semblance. "HARRY!" The outburst draws several people's attention. A drunk guy: "Hey, baby, toss Harry aside. Come spend the night with me instead!" Magi, disgusted, pushes him aside and leaves the tavern.
- She heads for Yosuke's restaurant, but her steps grow weaker. *What is he running from? Why wouldn't he change himself more?* She realizes he was eating with her. *Does he work for food and a place to live?*

#### Magi, nervous, at the corner, watching the building; the door is closed, but there's still light inside

- Magi feels the tension. She wants to capture Harry, but what if he's just a look-alike? She takes too long to decide, and the restaurant's light goes out.
- Her heart races thinking of all the money she'd get. Then she remembers something from back in [Blood](/lore/cities/#blood), when she applied to the guild to become a Pearl: she can borrow a suspect's badge for up to a week, but if she loses it, she pays ten times the price or is forced to work until the debt is paid off, plus other fees if she takes more than a week. (The [Missing Person](/lore/embassies-and-guilds/#embassies--quests) rule: only for adventurers investigating with a good lead.) She decides she'll do it.

# Book 13 - A Fateful Day › Chapter 3 - A Brewing Conspiracy

It sounds strange, almost foreign, but somehow a relief, even with a pointy arrow ready to tear his flesh. "Tell Dalila and everyone I'm sorry."

"An older guy in the same uniform as you gave me the key to the restaurant and asked me to take you there." Harry looks at his uniform and touches it in disbelief. "Oh no. He's gonna punish me!" Magi feels like an asshole. "I'm sorry about the arrow, but Princess Eori did most of it."
