## Questions for the author

Status: **open** until answered. An open question means the abridged text leaves that point out rather than guessing, or keeps the raw wording. Asked as one batch on 2026-11-21 and answered the same day.

### LA1. Woodland: "Tree Trunks" or Trunk People? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Woodland: "Loads of Tree Trunks are found here. They seem to grow more active the farther southwest you go, almost as if they were trained to keep humans at bay."
- **Why:** Beasts.md has "Trunk People" (four-armed, native to heavy forests around the Freeish Kingdom). Are the "Tree Trunks" the Trunk People?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Yes.
- **Change to the abridged text:** "Loads of Trunk People (see Beasts) are found here".
