## Questions for the author

Status: **answered** 2026-11-22. Asked as one batch on 2026-11-22.

### T1. Who went through the desert in ~2,650 B.G.?
- **Legacy text:** Finaies › Hifuna Conquer Period and Fuhraies › Red Snow Period: "~2,650 B.G. The Buhraies mounted a secret assault through the desert, but the desert killed off most of them." Buhraies › Fake Battle Period: "~2,650 B.G. The Buhraies did not care about the failed Finaies invasion of the Fuhraies."
- **Why:** The Finaies entry is about the Finaies' army crossing Hifuna, and the Buhraies entry calls it a Finaies invasion. Should both lines say the **Finaies** mounted it? (The abridged text says "a secret assault" for now.)
- **Answered:** 2026-11-22
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > T1) yes. From memory, they tried to massacre the Fuhraies but the Fuhraies were able to hole up themselves inside their newly created Gandew mountain. I believe I mentioned about it on the world lore.
- **Change to the abridged text:** both lines say the Finaies mounted the assault to massacre the Fuhraies, and that the Fuhraies holed up inside their newly created mountain. **Judgment call:** the mountain is written as Fuhrair, not Gandew: in ~2,650 B.G. the Fuhraies had just founded Fuhrair (Red Snow Period, ~3,000 to ~2,100 B.G.), while the Gandew mountain was only built in ~495 B.G. (Mountain Pact Period). The other mention the author remembers wasn't found: no other Legacy doc covers the ~2,650 B.G. assault (searched World Lore, Public and the novels for the date, the desert assault and Fuhrair).

### T2. Buhraies › Fuhraies Subjugation Period
- **Legacy text:** "Frustrated that the subjugation of the Fuhraies had gone nowhere, the Buhraies began targeting the Fuhraies for subjugation".
- **Why:** The period before is the failed Lishies Subjugation. Was it "Frustrated that the subjugation of the **Lishies** had gone nowhere"? (The abridged text says "their subjugation efforts" for now.)
- **Answered:** 2026-11-22
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > T2) yes! Good catch!
- **Change to the abridged text:** "Frustrated that the subjugation of the Lishies had gone nowhere".

### T3. When does the Darfaries' Transformation Time start?
- **Legacy text:** Darfaries › "Transformation Time (~200 B.G. - ~270 A.G.)", but its first period is "Lishies & Buhraies Shift Period (~805 B.G - ~0 A.G.)"; Buhraies › New Home Period says the first Darfaries, Wozefaries and Sorefaries were born after ~805 B.G. (the same for Wozefaries & Sorefaries).
- **Why:** Should the Transformation Time start ~805 B.G.?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-22
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > T3) yes, with smaller changes starting in the ~805 but really starting to form and shape strongly around ~300 into ~200 B.G
- **Change to the abridged text:** Darfaries and Wozefaries & Sorefaries: Transformation Time ~805 B.G. to ~270 A.G., with a line that small changes started around ~805 B.G. and the species really took shape around ~300 to ~200 B.G.

### T4. Two versions of the overthrow
- **Legacy text:** World Timeline: "The greed of the heroes was controlling the people"; the King of Gandew: "Some people don't believe he was that kind and selfless." Novels › Prosperity Treaty's Heroes - Part II: "The stones were controlling the people"; "Only a few people know of his true actions."
- **Why:** Which version is right, or are both on purpose (the in-world book telling it one way, history another)? Each page keeps its own version for now.
- **Answered:** 2026-11-22
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > T4) difference in purposes/perspectives.
- **Change to the abridged text:** each page keeps its own version, and each now points to the other as telling the events from another perspective.
