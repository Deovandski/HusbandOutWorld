## Questions for the author

Status: **open** until answered. An open question means the abridged text leaves that point out rather than guessing. Asked as one batch on 2026-11-21 and answered the same day.

### F1. What does "General Power Effects" measure? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Every elemental spell: "Tiers: See General Power Effects \*Cm". The Darfaries and Buhraies Body spells say "\*Kg of mass affected".
- **Why:** In `Formulas - Soul Energy.xlsx`, General Power Effects is 0.1 at Tier I and doubles each tier (0.2, 0.4 … 51.2 at Tier X … 6,553.6 at Tier XVII). Does that mean the ball's size in cm (0.1 cm at Tier I, about half a meter at Tier X), and the mass affected in kg for body spells? If so, I'll add a short table to these docs.
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > yes.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Cat. Elemental now gives the ball size by tier: 0.1 cm at Tier I, doubling to 51.2 cm at Tier X and 6,553.6 cm at Tier XVII (table from the spreadsheet). The same reading (kg of mass) is used in the Buhraies {BodyPart} VERB.

### F2. Which hand and fingers does a Finaies cast with? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Soul Energy to Spell Conversion: "the movement of the left hand while both hands are tied together". Step 1: "Hold the two fingers with the other hand for the element to appear".
- **Why:** Is it always the left hand that draws? And which two fingers does the other hand hold (the middle and ring fingers, like the Buhraies veins)?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > yes also finaies only have two fingers, branch like. You can try to parse their illustration to confirm.
- **Change to the abridged text:** The text now says the left hand draws, Finaies hands have only two fingers, branch-like, and the other hand holds the left hand's two fingers. Confirmed against `Illustrations/Races/Finaies.png`: each hand splits into two branch-like fingers.
