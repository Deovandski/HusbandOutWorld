### Q2. How much soul energy comes back per day? (open)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-20
- **Legacy text:** Soul Energy Recharge: "On average, a single Nari's worth of soul energy is replenished a day"
- **Why:** `Formulas - Soul Energy.xlsx` gives recharge rates per race in soul units per day, and they're much higher than one a day (for example 30–60 for Finaies, and 139 for the character calculator's default). At Tier I a spell costs 1 soul unit, or 1 nari stone, so "one nari's worth a day" reads as about 1 unit a day. Which is right, or is "one nari's worth" a bigger amount than one stone?
- **Answer:**
- **Change to the abridged text:** for now, no number is given ("more or less quickly depending on…").

### Q3. Does the badge gauge show current soul energy? (open)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-20
- **Legacy text:** Soul Energy Quantity Detection: "See the Embassy badge for more information on how to find out how much Soul Energy they currently have."
- **Why:** Embassies & Guilds.md doesn't describe it. Vol 1 (Chapter III, subchapter 2, Eiri showing Harry her badge) describes two thermometer-like gauges (thin 1–5, thick 6–10, logarithmic) that light up red under the thumb. Is that the soul energy reading? Do the numbers 1–10 mean anything more specific (tiers, or a share of your capacity)?
- **Answer:**
- **Change to the abridged text:** the abridged text describes the gauge from Vol 1 as showing current soul energy (the published novel wins over the World Lore's silence). It will be corrected if your answer differs.

### Q4. How long does a soul take to decay? (open)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-20
- **Legacy text:** Soul Death: "See Power Time. Replace Hours with Years for an average decay time."
- **Why:** "Power Time" is a column in `Formulas - Soul Energy.xlsx`: 1 hour at Tier I, doubling each tier up to 65,536 hours at Tier XVII. Read as years, a soul would take about 1 year to decay at Tier I, 16 at Tier V, 512 at Tier X and 65,536 at Tier XVII. Is the tier the person's soul energy tier at death? Can the abridged text say so with those numbers?
- **Answer:**
- **Change to the abridged text:** for now, only "the more soul energy capacity… the longer" is kept.
