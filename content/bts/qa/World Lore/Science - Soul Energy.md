## Questions for the author

Status: **open** until answered. An open question means the abridged text leaves that point out rather than guessing. Q1–Q6 were asked as one batch on 2026-11-20 and answered on 2026-11-21; Q7–Q8 are follow-ups.

### Q1. What is "Soul Bind"? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-20
- **Legacy text:** Soul Life Cycle & Soul Death: "See Soul Bind for additional info, including side effects of resuscitation"
- **Why:** There is no "Soul Bind" section anywhere in Legacy (World Lore, story notes, novels or spreadsheets). What are the side effects of resuscitation?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Normal revival side effects includes grogginess, some memory loss leading up to moment of death, disorientation, if you're been dead for longer you might feel like you're weeks behind calendar-wise until you stabilize mentally. If you're the type that fully remembered everything, you might be prone to an ego death, or even depression if you saw and felt your life being taken and now you're back.... But for how long? Some feel dread as they don't want to feel the strong feeling of death, while others get high off feeling immortal (and often that's when adventures becomes too much of risk takers and end up in the red, owning too much revival debt to Forway, or a private lender.) some experience true freedom after experiencing the feeling of death and explode in grown, self-realization, sometimes completely changing personalities.
  >
  > It doesn't mean that you can revive that death becomes any easier.
- **Change to the abridged text:** New subsection "After a revival" with the side effects, the three ways people react, and revival debt. The "See Soul Bind" pointer stays out (no Soul Bind section exists).

### Q2. How much soul energy comes back per day? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-20
- **Legacy text:** Soul Energy Recharge: "On average, a single Nari's worth of soul energy is replenished a day"
- **Why:** `Formulas - Soul Energy.xlsx` gives recharge rates per race in soul units per day, and they're much higher than one a day (for example 30–60 for Finaies, and 139 for the character calculator's default). At Tier I a spell costs 1 soul unit, or 1 nari stone, so "one nari's worth a day" reads as about 1 unit a day. Which is right, or is "one nari's worth" a bigger amount than one stone?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > 1 nari stone = 1 soul unit roughly. Humans on average recover 1 soul unit a day (confirm from word lore for me.) different races recovers at different rate, and within a given species humans recovers at different rates, partly because nature, partly because of nurture.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Recharging now says one nari stone holds roughly one soul unit, and that the rate depends on the race and differs between people (nature and nurture). The human average is held back: see the discrepancy below and follow-up Q7.

### Q4. How long does a soul take to decay? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-20
- **Legacy text:** Soul Death: "See Power Time. Replace Hours with Years for an average decay time."
- **Why:** "Power Time" is a column in `Formulas - Soul Energy.xlsx`: 1 hour at Tier I, doubling each tier up to 65,536 hours at Tier XVII. Read as years, a soul would take about 1 year to decay at Tier I, 16 at Tier V, 512 at Tier X and 65,536 at Tier XVII. Is the tier the person's soul energy tier at death? Can the abridged text say so with those numbers?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > yes. You can show it but do state this is basically something that scientists haven't been able to nail down the exacts in the Vraki world but it is commonly accepted "law of the soul" that the bigger the reservoir is, and the more you had energy wise there when you kicked the bucket, the longer is your revival window (if there's still physical flesh to rebound your soul into) and the longer you're expected to decay after "soul death". In the lore soul death is when you cannot revive someone.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Soul death is now defined as the point when someone can no longer be revived, followed by decay. Added the "law of the soul" paragraph (bigger reservoir and more energy at death mean a longer revival window and a longer decay), stated as not pinned down by scientists, and a table of average decay time per tier (Tier I about 1 year, doubling to 65,536 years at Tier XVII).
