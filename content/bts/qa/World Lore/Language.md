## Questions for the author

Status: **open** until answered. Asked as one batch on 2026-11-21 and answered the same day.

### L1. How did the Buhraies shape Vraki? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Vraki Language: "Because of Buhraies bringing English-speaking humans into the mix, but removing the language aspect. However, not everything was removed, so over time, when humans took over, the fundamental basis of creating an alphabet stuck around…"
- **Why:** I wrote "The Buhraies brought English-speaking humans into the mix but took away their language. Not everything was removed, though: when humans took over, the basic idea of building an alphabet stuck around." Is that what you meant?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Basically. Memory/mind manipulation, but language is such an embedded part of the soul that some of it will keep on.
- **Change to the abridged text:** "The Buhraies brought English-speaking humans into the mix and used memory and mind manipulation to take away their language. But language is such an embedded part of the soul that some of it kept on…"

### L2. Which interfaces couldn't Darfaries use? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Darfaries Interface Language: "A Logographic Language created by the ancient species, as Darfaries could not use their interfaces"
- **Why:** "Their interfaces" could mean the ancient species' interfaces (Finaies and Buhraies), or the Darfaries' own. Which one?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Darfaries cannot use Ancient species interfaces.
- **Change to the abridged text:** "…because Darfaries can't use the ancient species' interfaces."

### L3. Two glyphs in Misc look copied (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Misc table: "-" uses the same picture as "+" (image48), and "%" the same as "\*" (image49).
