## Questions for the author

Status: **open** until answered. An open question means the abridged text leaves that point out rather than guessing, or keeps the raw wording. Asked as one batch on 2026-11-21 and answered the same day.

### V1. Cliffbay and Cocona Island read the same (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Both: "Small village off the Gynorami water that meets the ocean. Hosts a Lighthouse"
- **Why:** Is one of them a copy/paste? Where is Cocona Island (an island village)?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Both are similar, you can even reference each other as in people that go into one spot might honestly be confused as how it looks almost errily identifical to the other spot...
- **Change to the abridged text:** Each now says it looks almost eerily identical to the other, so visitors are often confused.

### V2. New Freeish reads like Minala (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Minala: "Small village by two rivers. It floods every spring, so all houses are built off the ground." New Freeish: "It floods every spring, so all houses are built off the ground."
- **Why:** Is the New Freeish line a copy, or does it also flood? Anything else about it?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Floods as well because of the change in river flow and snow melt
- **Change to the abridged text:** "It floods every spring, as the river flow changes and the snow melts".

### V3. Sanbi East: smiths, or the Smith family? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** "The Smith family (both father and son) sucks at their job."
- **Why:** Is "Smith" their name, or are they the village's (black)smiths?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Village blacksmiths
- **Change to the abridged text:** "Its blacksmiths, a father and son, suck at their job."
