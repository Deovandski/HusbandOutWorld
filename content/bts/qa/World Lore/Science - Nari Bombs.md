## Questions for the author

Status: **open** until answered. An open question means the abridged text leaves that point out rather than guessing. Asked as one batch on 2026-11-21 and answered the same day (NB3, a follow-up, too).

### NB1. What does "Distance Cost Tier" mean? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Distance Cost Tier: "Tier I (Cheap Goods) None · Tier II (Common Goods) None · Tier III (Expensive Goods) Nari Gels · Tier IV (Nobility Goods) Banned Nari Gels"
- **Why:** The same section opens six docs (Natural and Crafted Resources, Fauna, Flora, Beasts, Nari Gels, Nari Bombs), sorting goods into four tiers, but nothing in Legacy explains what the tiers mean (how far goods can travel for the price? a markup per distance?). Also, this doc lists nari gels, not nari bombs: is that copied from the Nari Gels doc, or are nari bombs priced like nari gels?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > The four tiers basically attempts to separate from a society perspective of a common person what they would see. A common person might be er buy in their lifetime something as expensive as 1 sapphire, which would be something that a really rich person can. The boundaries between cheap and common, or expensive and nobolity tiers are a bit blurry and it's okay as it's more of a spectrum, those categories just help better visualize. And for the nari bombs, it is likely a copy/paste errors. If the costs are not the same as nati gels then we are good to use it. If they are true copy/paste, then I will need to come up with the numbers.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Distance Cost Tier is now abridged: a short explanation of the four tiers as a spectrum seen from a common person's point of view (the one-sapphire example). The nari gels listed under Tiers III and IV are dropped as a copy/paste error. The costs are not a copy: nari bombs cost 10–30 Pearls for ~100 g, while the nari gels range from 30–60 to 145–205 Pearls for ~200 g, so the bomb cost stays. Which tier nari bombs belong to is asked in NB3.

### NB2. How is a signal bomb set off? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Signal/Smoke Bombs: "Used alongside CREATE MIST and firing an upward shoot. Or submerged in water."
- **Why:** I read it as "set off alongside a Mist Create spell and fired upward, or dropped in water". Is that right?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Yes. Water is the catalyst to start the smoke.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Added: water is the catalyst that starts the smoke.

### NB3. Which cost tier are nari bombs? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Signal/Smoke Bombs › Cost: "10\~30 Pearls for \~100g". Distance Cost Tier listed nari gels (copy/paste).
- **Why:** With the nari gels dropped, the doc no longer says which tier nari bombs belong to. Which one (or ones) do signal/smoke bombs fall in?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Common goods
- **Change to the abridged text:** The Distance Cost Tier section now lists nari bombs by tier: Tier II (Common Goods) has Signal/Smoke Bombs; Tiers I, III and IV have none. (The tiers are bullets inside that section in the raw doc, not entries, so they are written there instead of as separate sections.)
