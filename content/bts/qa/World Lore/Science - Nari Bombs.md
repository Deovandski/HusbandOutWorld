- **Legacy text:** Distance Cost Tier: "Tier I (Cheap Goods) None · Tier II (Common Goods) None · Tier III (Expensive Goods) Nari Gels · Tier IV (Nobility Goods) Banned Nari Gels"

Also, this doc lists nari gels, not nari bombs: is that copied from the Nari Gels doc, or are nari bombs priced like nari gels?

- **Change to the abridged text:** Distance Cost Tier is left raw (not abridged) until this is answered.
