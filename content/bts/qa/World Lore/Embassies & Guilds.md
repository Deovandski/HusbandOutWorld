## Questions for the author

Status: **open** until answered. An open question means the abridged text leaves that point out rather than guessing, or keeps the raw wording. Asked as one batch on 2026-11-21; all answered the same day.

### E1. Sanbi guards in every embassy? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Location › Floor 5: "where the sanbi guards get and release wingas."
- **Why:** This describes every embassy. Did you mean "the guards" (Sanbi guards only in Sanbi)?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Embassy Floor 5 is where Forway guards get and release wingas. And it is in every embassy. Good catch
- **Change to the abridged text:** "Forway guards receive and release wingas here."

### E2. Embassy Captain and Forway general: the same role? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Embassy Captain: "you will only see Aquamarine or Sapphire Forway captains in those positions" … "Forway enforces all the generals' armor and social clothing to be the same as soldiers". Forway generals: "City embassies usually have a Pearl or Amethyst-General, kingdom embassies an Aquamarine-General".
- **Why:** Is the captain the same person as the embassy's general? If so, which ranks hold the job (Pearl and Amethyst at city embassies, or only Aquamarine and Sapphire)? The News section also has "the local Forway captain" shouting the daily news.
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Yes. And the note about Aquamarine and Sapphire generals is correct for Forway Embassies. I did further specify that lower rank generals take care of the city embassies, partly because of their size, but also partly because cities are under Forway's rule to begin with, so diplomacy and relationship handling with a reigning king/queen or elected official is not really much of a thing, and Embassies in smaller city serve more of a functional purpose like Winga mail, badge handling, quests for adventurers, revival center, banking, etc.
- **Change to the abridged text:** "Who runs it" merges the two: the embassy captain is its Forway general; kingdom embassies have Aquamarine or Sapphire generals (diplomacy with kings, queens or elected officials); city embassies usually get lower-rank Pearl or Amethyst generals, because the cities are smaller and already under Forway's rule, so their embassies are mostly functional (Winga mail, badges, quests, revivals, banking).

### E3. What do the badge balls look like? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Badge: "All badges are made of wood, with balls shaped to represent the adventurer tier, and iron-shaped balls represent a normal citizen."
- **Why:** I wrote "a ball for the adventurer's tier, or an iron ball for a regular citizen". Right?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > No. Lookup prices. I believe it is 1 amethyst for a badge.. perhaps regardless of the type.
- **Change to the abridged text:** The badge paragraph now follows the published novel and the public page: credit-card size, soul wood (greenish), name carved in, Forway logo on the back, smells of burnt wood when fresh; four corner balls, iron for a regular citizen (Eiri's, Vol 1) or matched to the rank for Amethyst, Aquamarine and Sapphire adventurers; Itagiba's black badge has red, blue, green and yellow balls (Vol 1). Price stays "1 amethyst", as this doc says.
