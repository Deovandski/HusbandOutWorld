## Questions for the author

Status: **open** until answered. An open question means the abridged text leaves that point out rather than guessing, or keeps the raw wording. Asked as one batch on 2026-11-21 and answered the same day (S5 needs a follow-up).

### S1. What are Fast Fish and Fast Bird? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Fast Fish: "people compete to see who travels the farthest … Everything and anything is allowed, except for flight/gravity Soul Energy interfaces." Fast Bird: "Certain Soul Energy interfaces/Nari gels are allowed."
- **Why:** Is Fast Fish a swimming race (who swims the farthest), and Fast Bird a flying race?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Yes. Nari Gel powered. Is akin to olympiads, but to see who has the best body, mind and nari gel.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Fast Fish is a nari gel-powered swimming race and Fast Bird a nari gel-powered flying race; both are like an olympiad showing who has the best body, mind and nari gel.

### S2. What is Fish Catch Fish? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** "Fish, while being able to swim, is the Fish Catch Fish."
- **Why:** Does it mean catching fish by hand while swimming?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Yes. Basically a literally way to fish by fishing with your hands and skills, with the power of nari gel.
- **Change to the abridged text:** "Fish Catch Fish is literally fishing with your hands and skills, swimming with the power of nari gels."

### S3. Explain Hammerschlagen? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Wood Hammering › Rules: "Hammerschlagen"
- **Why:** Hammerschlagen is a real German game (players take turns driving nails into a wooden stump; the first to sink theirs wins). OK to explain it like that, or keep the word only?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Explain it as not many people know it. Also add difficulty by using different type of woods like soulwood, rakuja tree etc.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Explains Hammerschlagen (players take turns driving a nail into a stump; first to sink theirs wins) and adds that different woods, like Soul Wood or a Rakuja tree, make it harder. (Flora.md describes Rakuja as a vine living on the trees it infests; the abridged text uses the author's words, "a Rakuja tree".)
