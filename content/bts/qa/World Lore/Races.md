## Questions for the author

Status: **open** until answered. An open question means the abridged text leaves that point out rather than guessing, or keeps the raw wording. Asked as one batch on 2026-11-21 and answered the same day.

### R1. Lishies: "Buhraies + Humans = Nothing"? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Lishies › Reproduction: "Buhraies + Humans = Nothing." Buhraies › Reproduction: "Buhraies + Humans = Baby that perishes within a few months of being born."
- **Why:** The Lishies line looks copied from the Buhraies one. Should it say "Lishies + Humans = Nothing"?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > That is correct if under Lishies I did not spell it out Lishies + Human = something else.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Lishies › Reproduction now says "Lishies + humans = nothing" (Legacy names no other outcome for Lishies).

### R2. Can Darfaries dampen a fall? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Darfaries › Skills: "due to their size, they can't fly, nor dampen their fall, so it's pretty useless." Later: "Due to their size, Darfaries cannot fly, but they can somewhat dampen their fall from approximately three-story buildings."
- **Why:** Which one is right?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > The truth is that they are too big for the Darfaries interface to do much, but it also doesn't do nothing. So it helps Dafaries dampen their fall a bit if they are not too heavy. So instead of getting 100% of the fall damage, it would be 80% or so.
- **Change to the abridged text:** "Darfaries are too big for the interface to do much, so they can't fly, but it isn't nothing: if they're not too heavy, it softens a fall a bit, so they take about 80% of the fall damage instead of 100%." (The "about three stories" line is dropped.)

### R3. Sorefaries: red flame or black flame? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Sorefaries › Isekai History: "When flying, a red flame appears on their Darfaries tattoo." Skills: "When flying, a black flame appears on their Darfaries' veins".
- **Why:** Which color is it when they fly? (Darfaries and Wozefaries get a black flame when dampening a fall.)
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Vol 1 has officially recorded a illustration with Sorefaries having a black flame when flying so it would be the same black flame when flying for Sorefaries and Darfaries. For the red flame, if there no other mentions of it anywhere else, then it was a brainfart and should be ignored, with the darfaries veins showing a white fire on it when casting spells. Color might be different if the person developed their own interface, or really went on to mess with their own darfaries interface, and somehow did not die in the dangerous process.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Checked: the red flame is mentioned nowhere else in Legacy (only in this line and the Vol 4 Draft 2 Q&A that quotes it), so it is dropped. Sorefaries show a black flame when flying, like Darfaries; the veins show white fire when casting; the color can differ for someone with their own interface or who messed with theirs and survived.

### R5. Are Cruros deaf? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Cruros › Intelligence: "They still can't speak or listen, but can draw pictures to converse."
- **Why:** Does "can't listen" mean they can't hear (or can't understand speech)? They do understand each other's grunts.
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > They are not deaf, at least most of them are not.. In the novel I believe Vol 1 there is an official description of the Cruros as in the smarter ones can hear Vraki just fine, but strugle to enunciate anything so they resort to scribbling it down or using head movements to communicate. Some can communicate with them with different noises representing different things, but depends how much of a friendship and history you have with them.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Most Cruros aren't deaf: the smarter ones hear Vraki fine but struggle to enunciate, so they scribble or use head movements; some people talk to them with noises, depending on friendship and history. In the novels, the closest scene found is in Vol 2 (a group of Cruros writing in their journals and showing each other), cited for the writing.
