## Questions for the author

Status: **open** until answered. An open question means the abridged text leaves that point out rather than guessing, or keeps the raw wording. Asked as one batch on 2026-11-21.

### R1. Lishies: "Buhraies + Humans = Nothing"? (open)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Lishies › Reproduction: "Buhraies + Humans = Nothing." Buhraies › Reproduction: "Buhraies + Humans = Baby that perishes within a few months of being born."
- **Why:** The Lishies line looks copied from the Buhraies one. Should it say "Lishies + Humans = Nothing"?
- **Answer:**
- **Change to the abridged text:** The crossbreeding line is left out of Lishies until answered.

### R2. Can Darfaries dampen a fall? (open)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Darfaries › Skills: "due to their size, they can't fly, nor dampen their fall, so it's pretty useless." Later: "Due to their size, Darfaries cannot fly, but they can somewhat dampen their fall from approximately three-story buildings."
- **Why:** Which one is right?
- **Answer:**
- **Change to the abridged text:** Uses the more specific one (they can somewhat dampen a fall from about three stories) for now.

### R3. Sorefaries: red flame or black flame? (open)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Sorefaries › Isekai History: "When flying, a red flame appears on their Darfaries tattoo." Skills: "When flying, a black flame appears on their Darfaries' veins".
- **Why:** Which color is it when they fly? (Darfaries and Wozefaries get a black flame when dampening a fall.)
- **Answer:**
- **Change to the abridged text:** Keeps the red flame in the description and points to the Darfaries veins for the rest, for now.

### R4. "Control their attitude"? (open)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Sorefaries › Skills: "While Sorefaries can control their attitude at will."
- **Why:** Did you mean altitude (they can fly)?
- **Answer:**
- **Change to the abridged text:** Keeps "attitude" for now.

### R5. Are Cruros deaf? (open)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Cruros › Intelligence: "They still can't speak or listen, but can draw pictures to converse."
- **Why:** Does "can't listen" mean they can't hear (or can't understand speech)? They do understand each other's grunts.
- **Answer:**
- **Change to the abridged text:** Keeps "can't speak or listen" for now.

## Left out on purpose

- The Darfaries-vein abilities (flames, senses, saliva), repeated word for word under Darfaries, Wozefaries and Sorefaries, are written once under Darfaries; the other two point there and keep their differences.
- Judgment call: "loli-looking" (Wozefaries) and "Small Kid-like loli" (Sorefaries) are written as "small and youthful-looking" and "small, childlike", and the Sorefaries entry doesn't repeat the sensing details next to the childlike description.
- "Scientific History" is written as "Inspired by …" (it names the real-world inspiration: trees, orcs, vampires, Homo sapiens).
- "Bunhraies" and "Polinizing" (typos, in the public text and the raw note).
