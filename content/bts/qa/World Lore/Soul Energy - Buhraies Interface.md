### B1. How do the interface shells work? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Interface Shells: "Each tier holds a limited number of Soul Energy tiers." Shell I 2, II 8, III 18, IV 32, V 50.
- **Why:** Is each number per shell, or the running total? And how do they relate to the 17 soul energy tiers (the five shells add up to 110)?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > per shell. And nobody ever maxed it out on Darfaries interface to know what would happen. Current theory is that if you exceed the theoretical max, the whole thing would explode, possibly a white hole event.
- **Change to the abridged text:** The shells sentence now says each shell, on its own, holds that many tiers. Added: nobody has ever maxed it out; the current theory is that going past the theoretical maximum would explode, possibly in a White Hole event. (The answer says "on Darfaries interface". The shells are described in the Buhraies doc, so the abridged text says "it" without naming an interface. Ask if that should change.)
