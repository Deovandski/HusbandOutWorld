# Characters: abridgement Q&A (proof of work)

- **Raw (Legacy):** `Legacy/Behind The Scenes/World Lore/Characters.md` (unchanged)
- **Abridged (website):** `content/bts/World Lore/Characters.md`
- **Issue:** #103

Every question asked of the author and his answer (word for word), every gap filled from elsewhere in Legacy (with its source), every discrepancy and how it was settled, and what was left out. Source order: (1) the author's decision, (2) the published novels, (3) the rest of Legacy.

**Polish (the author's standing instruction, 2026-11-22: "Polish grammar, typos always."):** every entry is polished for grammar and typos.

**How this one is abridged:** in batches, by group. Each character gets their backstory ("Isekai History (Prior to Story)") as short paragraphs, then a list of facts (Race, Birthday, "Attracted to" for Sexuality, Myers-Briggs, Intelligence, Habits for Action tags, Speech for Verbal tags, Strengths, Flaws, Likes and dislikes, Interface and Skills), then their stats by book. The Website Description is the public text, shown above the notes. Empty values ("-", "None") are left out, and so is a stats table with no numbers. Characters not yet abridged fall back to the raw notes.

## Batch 1: Adventurers and Blood (asked 2026-11-22)

Harry, Magi, Fernan, Hiromi, Morika, Takeo, July, Kaiana, Posaqui, Akemi, Jonem.

Status: **answered** 2026-11-22.

### CH1. Takeo: Darfaries or human?
- **Legacy text:** Race "Darfaries"; Interface "Darfaries". Isekai History: "Takeo is technically human as he lacks the Darfaries' veins. He is conflicted as to whether or not that fact is true."
- **Why:** Should Race say human (looks like a Darfaries), or Darfaries as written?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-22
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Ch1: yes.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Race: "human, though he looks like a Darfaries."

### CH2. Akemi: born in Blood City or Flatland?
- **Legacy text:** "Akemi's parents were from flatland, but found themselves in Blood city later where she was born. Grew up in Flatland city." Later: "many years later ended up in blood city."
- **Why:** Born in Blood City and raised in Flatland, then back to Blood City? Or born and raised in Flatland?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-22
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Ch2: anything else mentioned about this anywhere else? If not, born in Blood, raised in flatland and young adult she found her way back there. Make fun of it like some people are just "destined" for something and doesn't matter what happens it seems that they keep getting grabbed back to a specific place.
- **Change to the abridged text:** checked first: no other Legacy doc says where Akemi was born or raised (City.md only notes the Blue-Flower-Red-Bone guild was created when she and Fernan married; `Legacy/Public/characters.md` doesn't say). The abridged text: born in Blood City, raised in Flatland City, back in Blood City as a young adult, with "Some people are just \"destined\" for a place: no matter what happens, it keeps grabbing them back."

### CH3. Akemi's birth year
- **Legacy text:** Akemi "1004 A.G. Asa 64th"; Magi "1020 A.G. Asa 3rd"; Fernan "994 A.G. Adrian 6th".
- **Why:** That makes Akemi 16 when Magi was born, after a first quest and "many years" as a historian. Is 1004 right?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-22
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Ch3: tweak Akemis Birthday so that she had Magi at 26. Make it official.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Birthday 993 A.G. Asa 64th (Magi was born on 1020 A.G. Asa 3rd, when Akemi was 26). **Made official:** `content/fixes.json` corrects "1004 A.G. Asa 64th" in the raw Characters notes and in `Legacy/Public/characters.md`, and the translated public paragraph is re-keyed with 993 in all fourteen languages.

### CH4. Posaqui's age
- **Legacy text:** Birthday "1023 A.G. Asa 33"; Book 3 age "?"; an Amethyst adventurer and one of Neferteri's agents.
- **Why:** 1023 makes him 16 in 1039. Is that right?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-22
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Ch4: yes. He is an young starter. Make reference that despite being precocial (or whatever the wording is) he is incredibly talented and serious with his contracts, making him look older for basically everyone.
- **Change to the abridged text:** "He's a young starter, only 16 in 1039, but despite being so precocious, he's incredibly talented and serious about his contracts, which makes almost everyone think he's older." Book 3 age: 16.

### Judgment calls in batch 1

- **"(See World Lore Q&A › Vol 4 Draft 2, Qn.)"** pointers are dropped: their answers are already in the notes, and the Q&A drafts aren't on the website.

- Magi, Fernan, Takeo, July, Akemi and Jonem have no stats table (the raw ones are empty).

### CH10. Names on the public page vs the notes
- **Legacy text:** `Legacy/Public/characters.md` vs Characters.md:
  - Arukia **Sanbi** vs Arukia **Long-Bridge**
  - Maicon **Crossroads** Black-Dove vs Maicon **Forway** Black-Dove
  - **Binaata** Forway Cliff-Jumper vs **Biny** Forway Cliff-Jumper
  - Coema **Half-Cruro** vs Coema **Sanbi Half-Cruro** (the same for Kiko and Zico)
  - Huneo Sanbi **Sandour** vs Huneo Sanbi **Royal**
- **Why:** The Name Schema puts where someone lives in their name, so both could be right at different times (Arukia was born in Long-Bridge and now works in Sanbi; Maicon was born in Crossroads). Which names are current? Should the notes and the public page match?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-22
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Ch10: use the novel and world lore. Someone can have a birth name so to speak, and then move and update their badge. Arukia name remained the same from when she was born as Red Winga also wanted to have a solid backstory for her if they needed that framing for a big job.... In this case the Sanbi Kingdom job. That's why her badge got updated to Sanbi. Maicon updated to Forway Black-Dove when he was an adult. Binaata is the full name, Biny is a nickname. For the Sanbi Half-Cruro study the name structure. That's because they are in the same guild.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Arukia: her badge kept her birth name (Long-Bridge) for the Red Winga's backstory, and was updated to Arukia Sanbi for the Sanbi Kingdom job. Maicon: born Maicon Crossroads Black-Dove, updated to Forway Black-Dove as an adult. Biny: Binaata is his full name, Biny a nickname. Coema, Kiko and Zico: by the Name Schema (NAME PLACE GUILD), "Sanbi Half-Cruro" is Sanbi (their place) plus the Half-Cruro guild they share; it goes in their entries in the Sanbi batch. Huneo (Sandour or Royal) wasn't answered; asked again in the Sanbi batch. No novel gives these surnames (searched the Light Novel folder).
