## Questions for the author

Status: **open** until answered. An open question means the abridged text leaves that point out rather than guessing, or keeps the raw wording. Asked as one batch on 2026-11-21 and answered the same day.

### F1. Pussy Power: dog tags or right arm? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Isekai History: "Pussy Power members are easy to spot, as they wear their insignia on dog tags." Identification: "They wear their badge proudly on their right arm."
- **Why:** Both? (Dog tags worn on the right arm?) Or one of the two?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > Both.
- **Change to the abridged text:** "they wear their insignia on dog tags, and their badge proudly on their right arm."

### F2. Pussy Power: 273 or 50+? (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** Website Description: "Their latest self-reported size was 273". Members: "50+, and they all seem to have equal voices."
- **Why:** Is the real number 50+ and 273 just what they claim, or should one of them change?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > 50+ as Forway has investigated, but Pussy Power publicly claims a very specific number, 273 as of Asa 1039 A.G... Forway has not done an in-depth research either, but it believes it is likely an inflated number.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Members: 50+ by Forway's investigation; they publicly claim a very specific 273 (as of Asa 1039 A.G.); Forway hasn't researched in depth but believes it's likely inflated.

### F3. Black Magina: Magina, Mind Enabler and "Nagina" (answered)
- **Asked:** 2026-11-21
- **Legacy text:** "The most well-known faction behind Mind Enabler was banned by Forway." … "using Nagina to put everyone to sleep." … "to prevent other gangs from supplying Magina." Identification: "a Magina petal". Elsewhere Magina is smoked ("smoking too much Magina").
- **Why:** Is Magina their own drug (a smoked flower), separate from the Mind Enabler nari gel, or the same thing? And is "Nagina" a typo for Magina, or something else (like the Sleep Inducer gel)?
- **Answered:** 2026-11-21
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > They are. Black Magina is how they got into the black market business, but then they expanded naturally into banned nari gels to of course diversify their business... They are criminals but they are not stupid. And I meant to say Magina instead of Nagina.
- **Change to the abridged text:** Magina is how they got into the black market; they then expanded into banned nari gels (Mind Enabler) to diversify. "Nagina" reads "Magina".
