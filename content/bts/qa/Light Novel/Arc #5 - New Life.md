### A5-2. Morika marries the second princess: who's who?
- **Legacy text:** "Morika marries Princess #2", told with "the main character", "a hero", "Princess #2" and "princess #3".
- **Why:** Is the hero Morika, the main character Harry, the second princess Maiara and the third Eori (the order of Itagiba's daughters)? The abridged text keeps the generic roles for now.
- **Answered:** 2026-11-22
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > A5-2: Yep. Morika will marry Maiara.
- **Change to the abridged text:** the idea is retitled "Morika marries Maiara", with the names in: Harry, Morika, Itagiba, Maiara (second princess) and Eori (third princess).

### A5-3. Whose butt does Harry slap?
- **Legacy text:** "The stamp was a fake. Harry is hunted down by guards, then Neferteri shows up to resolve the situation. Harry ends the ordeal by slapping her butt in front of everyone."
- **Why:** Neferteri's? The abridged text keeps "her", as written.
- **Answered:** 2026-11-22
- **Answer (verbatim):**
  > A5-3: Nina's butt. I remember now. Basically the plot here surrounds the idea that Nina will act up, like the previous princeses, but Nina's "wildness" is tied to her just wanting to walk around town without Neferteri breathing down her neck and just running around like a child, and eat and drink whatever she wants. She fakes a stamp to make Harry do her binding as if it was Neferteri that had approved it.
- **Change to the abridged text:** the idea now explains Nina's "wildness" (walking around town without Neferteri breathing down her neck, running around like a child, eating and drinking whatever she wants) and that she fakes Neferteri's stamp to make Harry do her bidding ("binding" read as bidding); it ends with "Harry ends the ordeal by spanking Nina in front of everyone."
