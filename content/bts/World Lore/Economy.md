# Economy › Isekai History

Economic activity in Vraki comes from fauna, flora and beasts, and the goods and services made from them. It's divided into four economic regions, and comparative advantage decides which goods are worth exporting from one region to another.

# Comparative Advantage

A region has a comparative advantage when its members can carry out a particular economic activity more efficiently than another region's. The Distance Cost Tier (D.C.T, the cost of moving goods) must be accounted for before claiming one either way.

**Example:** fanchaki farms are spread out everywhere, but Freeish and Blood have the comparative advantage for large-scale farming. Raising larger fanchakis (like Sanbi's experimental farm) costs more than their meat sells for.

**Moving goods.** Shipping costs about 1 Pearl per kg for every 120 km for Tier I (cheap) goods, 84 km for Tier II (common), 58.8 km for Tier III (expensive) and 29.4 km for Tier IV (nobility), assuming a full egua cart. An egua pulls up to 150 kg of cargo and covers about 30 km a day pulling it (about 65 km a day when ridden).

| Between | Distance |
|---|---|
| Sanbi and Tolerideme regions | 1,000 km |
| Freeish and Sanbi regions | 2,000 km |
| Freeish and Tolerideme regions | 3,000 km |
| Gandew and Sanbi regions | 3,000 km |
| Freeish and Gandew regions | 3,293 km |
| Gandew and Tolerideme regions | 4,000 km |

The full numbers are in the [Economy spreadsheet](Legacy/Behind%20The%20Scenes/Spreadsheets/Formulas%20-%20Economy.xlsx) (Distance Cost Calculator).

# Economical Regions

Forway is part of all four regions, and so is any village within a region's shared boundaries. A few cities sit in two.

| Region | Members | Mainly exports |
|---|---|---|
| Freeish | Forway, Triangle, Deltacross, Freeish, Blood, Sweetlake, Sundeath | A ton of flora and fauna |
| Gandew | Forway, Flatland, Gandew, Sundeath | Minerals, Wooly and chitin |
| Sanbi | Forway, Sanbi, Waterfall, Triangle, Tastyleaf, Endlessfog | Sanbi, fish-related products, etc. |
| Tolerideme | Forway, Tolerideme, Waterfall, Tastyleaf, Wingarest | Domar Carbey, Wood People and Wingas |

# Balls Currency › Isekai History

Balls are the common currency of the four kingdoms (Tolerideme, the Sanbi Kingdom, the Freeish Kingdom and the Gandew Kingdom) and all the participating cities around them. The Prosperity Treaty introduced them in Section #3, since bartering is forbidden between the treaty's parties. Balls are made throughout the kingdom, and the city or kingdom's embassy oversees their manufacturing.

There are four balls. From the most valuable:

| 1 ball | Aquamarine | Amethyst | Pearls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sapphire | 10 | 100 | 5,000 |
| Aquamarine | | 10 | 500 |
| Amethyst | | | 50 |

To get a feel for the value, the author's spreadsheet counts 1 Pearl as about 1 US dollar.

# Daily Salary

The average daily pay, for an average work day of 12 hours. Work time varies by job, and this doesn't account for jobs that depend on luck, like fishing.

Pay in Pearls, by Profession Tier:

| | Tier I | Tier II | Tier III | Tier IV | Tier V | Tier VI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Apprentice, per hour | 1 | 2 | 3 | 5 | 7 | 10 |
| Master, per hour | 1 | 3 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 15 |
| Apprentice, per day | 12 | 24 | 36 | 60 | 84 | 120 |
| Master, per day | 12 | 36 | 48 | 72 | 96 | 180 |

Expenses take about 75% of earnings, so a Tier I worker keeps about 3 Pearls a day. See the [Economy spreadsheet](Legacy/Behind%20The%20Scenes/Spreadsheets/Formulas%20-%20Economy.xlsx) (Salary Calculator).

# Weekly Salary

The average weekly pay assumes pay for every work day: about 4 a week. Some professions don't accrue pay on days off.

| Pearls per week | Tier I | Tier II | Tier III | Tier IV | Tier V | Tier VI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Apprentice | 48 | 96 | 144 | 240 | 336 | 480 |
| Master | 48 | 144 | 192 | 288 | 384 | 720 |

Over a year (73 weeks), that's 3,504 Pearls at Tier I, up to 35,040 for a Tier VI apprentice or 52,560 for a Tier VI master. See the [Economy spreadsheet](Legacy/Behind%20The%20Scenes/Spreadsheets/Formulas%20-%20Economy.xlsx) (Salary Calculator).

# Sapphire

- **Hardness:** 9
- **Where it comes from:** mined from Flatland City to Blood City, deep inside the mountains (about 1 km down).
- **Size:** slightly bigger than a fingernail, about 2 cm across.
- **Worth:** 10 Aquamarine, 100 Amethyst or 5,000 Pearls.
- **What it buys:** a fully grown tamed egua, properties, slavery contracts, guilds, specialized education, Tier IX+ Soul Energy.

# Aquamarine

- **Hardness:** 7.5 to 8
- **Where it comes from:** mined from Flatland City to Blood City, deep inside the mountains (about 1 km down).
- **Size:** slightly bigger than a fingernail, about 2 cm across.
- **Worth:** 10 Amethyst or 500 Pearls.
- **What it buys:** custom-crafted armor and weapons, or good gear in general; a young tamed egua; an adult tamed Big Winga; well-protected, guarded travel; fancier hotels; fundamental education; fancy clothing, usually with gold or precious-metal details; Tier VI to VIII Soul Energy. For some people, it's their entire life savings.

# Amethyst

- **Hardness:** 7
- **Where it comes from:** mined from Flatland City to Blood City, deep inside the mountains (about 1 km down).
- **Size:** slightly bigger than a fingernail, about 2 cm across.
- **Worth:** 50 Pearls.
- **What it buys:** 1 nari stone; common household items like a table or fost lamps; fancier food and drinks; average armor and weapons; a better travel experience; average lodging, often with breakfast; new cheap clothing, or somewhat more refined clothing; most nari gels at higher effect tiers; Tier IV to VI Soul Energy; sigil rings; a Winga companion.

# Pearl

- **Hardness:** 2.5 to 4.5
- **Where it comes from:** fishermen from Sanbi gathered them at first, but once the Cruros showed up, it became their task, since it was close to free labor.
- **Size:** slightly bigger than a fingernail, about 2 cm across.
- **Worth:** the lowest ball; the others are counted in Pearls.
- **What it buys:** common food and beer; a day's lodging in a hostel or cheap hotel; bargain armor and weapons; hay for an egua; travel by wagon (less comfortable and less protected); utensils; used clothing (sometimes with wear and tear) or very cheap clothing; most nari gels at weak effect tiers; Tier I to III Soul Energy; paper, ink, etc.
