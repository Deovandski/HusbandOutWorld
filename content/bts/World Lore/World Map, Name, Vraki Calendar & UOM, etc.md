# Day & Night

Vraki's sun behaves much like ours. The day is split into four timehunks, each divided in two: a low and a high half (times are approximate, since they shift with the seasons). Low and high refer to the ratio of nari crystals in the mixture for each chunk.

![][image1]

The moon is like Earth's, except for its color. The night sky uses the moon's color palette (#61f878). See the Time Keeper for more.

![][image2]

If you focus moonlight on a small point, any living being glows within its shine (more in the World Timeline). The night is laid out like the day, with the same low and high halves.

# Vraki Calendar & Birth Age

Forway City created and standardized the calendar (see the World Timeline). Every Asa 1st, Forway also releases a lunar mapping, for a measly twenty pearls, for hunters, farmers, adventurers and anyone else who reads the lunar phases to gauge environmental tendencies.

**A year** has 4 months, 73 weeks and 365 days.

**The months** are named after the three courtroom scientists, plus the man who kickstarted the rebellion that led to the Prosperity Treaty (Freeish Paulin):

| Month | Season | Days | Weeks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asa | Spring | 85 | 17 |
| Paulin | Summer | 110 | 22 |
| Moacir | Fall | 85 | 17 |
| Adrian | Winter | 85 | 17 |

**A week** has 5 days, each named after a kingdom (or Forway):

| Day | | Named after |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Freeday | Work day | The Freeish Kingdom, which gets the first day of the working week because the rebellion began there |
| 2. Tollday | Work day | Tolerideme, the second kingdom to join |
| 3. Sannday | Work day | Sanbi, the third |
| 4. Gannday | Work day | The Gandew Kingdom, the last kingdom to join |
| 5. Forrday | Weekend | Forway ("Forward Day"): all embassies are closed to the public |

The whole calendar, with a day-by-day comparison to the Gregorian calendar, is in the [World Calendar & UOM spreadsheet](Legacy/Behind%20The%20Scenes/Spreadsheets/World%20Calendar%20%26%20UOM.xlsx).

**Birth age:** a person's age is the number of years they've been alive as of the current year. The day and month they were born don't matter.

# Name Schema

To make names, contracts and negotiations easier, Forway introduced a naming schema for every kingdom child born after the Prosperity Treaty came into effect in 680 A.G.

A full name is **NAME PLACE GUILD**:

1. **Name:** given by the parents at birth. How unique names are, and what they sound like, varies by region.
2. **Place:** the city of origin or main residence, often where the person's main guild is.
3. **Guild:** the guild's name; if they're in several guilds, any of them. If they're in no guild, this part is skipped. When the guild is named CITY-GUILD and the city is already the badge owner's place, the city isn't written twice: Itagiba Sanbi Royal, not Itagiba Sanbi Sanbi-Royal.
