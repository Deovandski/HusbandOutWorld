# Aldemeri

Not much is left since the fall of Aldemeri. Some bandits and Cruros live around these plains, alongside a few small villages that farm the fertile land. It used to be one of the largest livestock and farming areas around, but the land overtook what was left.

# Bahyum

See the World Timeline. Now a moody castle is basically all that remains, after Wood People and humans destroyed or ransacked most things. A nearby village of the same name exists thanks to the flow of adventurers.

#### Moody Castle

A massive castle in ruins, a favorite place for adventurers, bandits and researchers to explore. There are tons of enemies to fight, and once in a while someone finds a new room ready for ransacking. It's almost as if the castle itself is still alive. In the village, a tracker is updated daily with how moody the castle is (see Walking Heart Stone under Natural & Crafted Resources):

# Fuhrair

The largest Fuhraies civilization, now empty: too cold and too high for anyone to live in, but some still venture in for its dangers and the valuables lying there. Forway has sent search parties to gather more information, and hidden scientific stations are scattered around some areas.

#### Snow Houses

Several houses high up the mountain, with no doors, just holes for windows. Underground tunnels connect everything.

# Hifuna

# Lishin

See the World Timeline. A large city with lots of big buildings, some a few stories tall. Its central location made it a hub for travelers, then for armies, before it became Forway City.

- **The Ancient Four-Story Tall Building:** the last four-story-tall Lishies building.
- **The Ancient Plaza:** a small plaza surrounded by several buildings.
- **Fighting Pits:** a few fighting pits scattered around, some filled with water, others still usable to settle disputes the Lishin way.

**Visual reference:** like Ganyrami, but rockier in its architecture.

# Nari Caverns

In the mountains around Blood City (see Nari Worms for their history, and Blood City for more). Some humans come here to mine other minerals, but they have to be careful: too much noise irritates the worms and causes tremors.

#### Deep Hole Chamber

One of the largest chambers rumored to exist deep underground. Finding chunks of minerals, nari included, is a given there; getting there is the hardest and most dangerous part. The Queen Nari Worm is rumored to live there.

# Newland

The land of big mammals, with tons of resources to explore and things to eat. Giant sloths rule the place as the de facto kings of the land.

#### Sweet Sting Nest

# Whiteland

The planet's south pole. White Roundies live there, alongside some other mammals, most of the year. Humans don't really visit, unless they're running from everyone.

![][image17]

# Woodland

A piece of land west of Sweetlake: a densely populated forest. Loads of Tree Trunks are found here; they seem to grow more active the farther southwest you go, almost as if they'd been trained to keep humans out. Large flowers grow here too.
