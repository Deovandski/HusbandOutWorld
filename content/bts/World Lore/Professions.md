# Tier VI Professions

The richest people in the four kingdoms. Their power is immense, and their riches are vast.

**Average pay:** 10 Pearls an hour for apprentices, 15 for masters.

# King

**History:** the job came into being when great adventurers founded the kingdoms.

**Duties:** take care of the kingdom. Work with the Queen, the courtroom scientists and the other kingdoms to comply with the Prosperity Treaty, and plan city protection with the courtroom scientists and city guards.

**Training:** Fundamental School plus two specialized schools.

How much of this applies depends on how the kingdom is organized.

# Queen

**History:** the job came into being when great adventurers founded the kingdoms.

**Duties:** take care of the kingdom. Work with the King, the courtroom scientists and the other kingdoms to comply with the Prosperity Treaty.

**Training:** Fundamental School plus two specialized schools.

How much of this applies depends on how the kingdom is organized.

# Courtroom Scientists

**History:** the job came into being when great adventurers founded the kingdoms and needed more help running them.

**Duties:** help take care of the kingdom. Work with the King, the Queen and the other kingdoms to comply with the Prosperity Treaty, and plan city protection with the King and city guards. How much depends on how the kingdom is organized.

**Training:** Fundamental School, Soul Energy School, and Tier VII Soul Energy capabilities.

# Courtroom Scientists Apprentice

**History:** most kingdoms need to plan ahead for their courtroom scientist's demise.

**Duties:** take care of the kingdom, working directly under the courtroom scientists.

**Training:** Fundamental School, Soul Energy School, and Tier VI Soul Energy capabilities.

# King-Maid

**History:** the job started in the Sanbi Kingdom, when great adventurers built castles and needed someone they trusted to help run them.

**Duties:** look after the Royal Guild's needs and security, and coordinate the maids under them to keep the castle clean and the food stock full.

**Training:** Fundamental School, Accounting School and Cooking School.

# Tier V Professions

The second richest people in the world. Their power reaches a region at most.

**Average pay:** 7 Pearls an hour for apprentices, 8 for masters.

# Artisan Blacksmiths

**History:** detail-oriented blacksmiths end up becoming artisan blacksmiths.

**Duties:** create highly detailed weapons, armor, etc.

**Training:** Blacksmithing School, and a natural talent for crafting.

# Artisan Tailors

**History:** people need to cover up their private parts in fashion.

**Duties:** create and repair highly detailed armor, tunics and related items.

**Training:** Tailoring School, and a natural talent for tailoring.

# City Guard Captain

**History:** a city guard who also specializes in Soul Energy is promoted to City Guard Captain. A guard with great communication skills or innate Soul Energy talent might have that schooling paid for by the kingdom (depending on how the kingdom is organized).

**Duties:** keep the peace, assist in investigations and help medics. Also coordinate nari stone usage, work with adventurers, and coordinate with the King and courtroom scientists (depending on how the kingdom is organized).

**Training:** Fundamental School, plus Combat and Soul Energy School.

# Region Merchants

**History:** someone who takes merchanting extremely seriously.

**Duties:** own one or more general trading goods stores, often in touch with traveling and local merchants.

**Training:** Fundamental School and Accounting School.

# Large Farm Owner

**History:** large farms are usually the result of many generations of hard-working people.

**Duties:** handle people, livestock and crops, and maybe get dirty on the job.

**Training:** Fundamental School and Accounting School.

# Tier IV Professions

The third richest people in the world. Their power reaches a city at most.

**Average pay:** 5 Pearls an hour for apprentices, 6 for masters.

# Sapphire Adventurer

See [Badges, Embassies & Guilds](/lore/embassies-and-guilds/).

**Training:** Adventurer School.

# Maid

**History:** as prosperity created wealthy people, they wanted someone else to handle running the home, correspondence, etc.

**Duties:** housekeeping. How far it goes depends on the household and the maid's prestige: from more "normal" maids, up to someone like Eiri, a King-Maid, whose job extends into finances, cooking, cleaning and taxes.

**Training:** Fundamental School and Cooking School.

# Blacksmiths

**History:** people kill people, so someone needs to make the instruments.

**Duties:** create weapons, armor, etc.

**Training:** Blacksmithing School.

# Tailor

**History:** people need to cover up their private parts.

**Duties:** create and repair armor, tunics and related items.

**Training:** Tailoring School.

# Artisan Cheesemaker

**History:** because some cheese tastes better than others.

**Duties:** make cheese, store it and care for it.

**Training:** Fundamental School and Cooking School.

# Construction Manager

**History:** someone has to organize the building and repair of things.

**Duties:** plan construction, assign roles, manage people, and manage costs, material orders, etc.

**Training:** Fundamental School and Construction School.

# City Guard

**History:** as villages grew bigger, someone had to step in and keep the peace.

**Duties:** keep the peace, assist in investigations and help medics.

**Training:** Fundamental School and Combat School.

# Historians/Teachers

**History:** wherever humans live, history soon follows, filling libraries. But some history was lost during the Greed Period, when wars raged for a long time, and tons more because before Ganyrami it was mostly passed by word of mouth among humans. The Darfaries burned their own history, which makes the past kind of sketchy. And some people don't care about history, because they prefer living in the present. So people in this profession usually teach at the fundamental level to earn a livable wage while they pursue research nobody is funding.

**Duties:** write down historical events, keep libraries organized and books in top shape, and study the ruins.

**Training:** Fundamental School and Historian/Teaching School.

# Local Merchants

**History:** someone who takes merchanting very seriously. Usually passed down through the guild, and often started by a traveling merchant who settled down and created a guild.

**Duties:** own, part-own or manage a general trading goods store, often in touch with traveling and regional merchants.

**Training:** Fundamental School and Accounting School help, but aren't necessary.

# Medium-Farm-Owner

**History:** medium-sized farmers exist all over the place, usually both feeding themselves and selling crops and livestock.

**Duties:** handle people, livestock and crops, and get dirty on the job.

**Training:** Fundamental School isn't required, but it's really hard to do without.

# Famous Courtesans

**History:** why not get paid to get fucked, especially if you're handsome or pretty, and famous?

**Duties:** beauty is in the eye of the beholder.

**Training:** knowing how to please the guest goes a long way; otherwise, learn on the job. Luck and street smarts help.

**Duties:** carry the dead to the mortuary, prepare them for the ritual, and clean up bodily fluids to contain disease (depending on the local culture).

# Stable Hand Manager

**Training:** Fundamental School.

# Innkeeper & Barkeepers

# Small-Farm-Owner

**History:** small farmers exist all over the place, usually feeding themselves and selling the leftovers for other things.

**Duties:** handle livestock and crops, and get dirty on the job. Maybe handle a person or two, but usually just guild members.

**Training:** Fundamental School helps.

# Bards

**History:** wherever people go, entertainment soon follows.

**Duties:** read, play and write songs.

**Training:** Fundamental School.

# Sanbi Eye

**History:** created by the Sanbi Kingdom as a plot device to hide what actually happened to Harry. Neferteri created the whole thing so Eori would have Harry exclusively for herself. The public Sanbi Eye was Sinoi wearing Harry's fake skin; only Neferteri knew.

**Duties:** listen to people's requests and relay them to the Sanbi Kingdom as official requests. Basically, an on-the-go stand-in for the King and Queen, so everyone is heard.

**Training:** Fundamental School.

# Travelling Merchants

**History:** they buy and sell as they travel from city to city. The best place to find odd trinkets is with one of them.

**Duties:** be good at negotiating prices.

**Training:** Fundamental School helps, but this is self-employed, so you do you.

# Classy Courtesans

**History:** you can get paid to get fucked, especially if you're handsome or pretty.

**Duties:** beauty is in the eye of the beholder.

**Training:** knowing how to please the guest goes a long way; otherwise, learn on the job.

# Tier II Professions

# Boat Repair

**History:** as Sanbi became a fishing hub, boat repair became necessary.

**Duties:** fix and clean boats.

**Training:** learn on the job as an apprentice; no reading required.

# Wooly Herder

**History:** because Woolies are kind of stupid.

**Duties:** take care of Woolies and help with their births.

**Training:** knowing how to read speeds up going from apprentice to master; otherwise, learn on the job.

**History:** you can get paid to get fucked.

# Winga Tamers

**History:** as the number of cities grew, cities needed to correspond.

**Duties:** tame wingas from the egg into adulthood for courier services.

# Ore Smelter

**History:** someone has to do the smelting, but not everyone wants to.

**Duties:** smelt ores into bars and remove impurities.

**Training:** knowing how to read speeds up going from apprentice to master; otherwise, learn on the job.

# Wood/Stone Worker

**History:** one of the earliest jobs, first done by the same person who extracted the resource.

**Duties:** prepare stone and wood for buildings and artisans.

**Training:** learn on the job as an apprentice; no reading required.

# Road Maintainers

**History:** as roads were built, maintaining them became a job.

**Duties:** fix the roads and clear the forest from them. The risk of bandits makes the job better paid.

**Training:** learn on the job as an apprentice; no reading required.

# Paper/Vellum Crafter

**History:** information is necessary, so some people make vellum and paper for it.

**Duties:** make vellum from animal skin, and paper from wood pulp.

**Training:** learn on the job as an apprentice; no reading required.

# Ink/Wax Crafter

**History:** paper is half the battle, so someone needs to make the ink and the wax.

**Duties:** see Natural & Crafted Resources.

**Training:** learn on the job as an apprentice; no reading required.

# Egua Rider

**History:** one of the earliest jobs in existence.

**Duties:** drive egua carts from city to city, carrying basic goods.

**Training:** learn on the job; no reading required.

# Hunter

**History:** one of the earliest jobs in existence: hunting in the nearby woods, a quite dangerous job. Mostly replaced by adventurer hunting, since hunters on their own don't have the safety net adventurers have with the embassies.

**Duties:** hunt prey, butcher it, and bring it back somewhere to sell.

**Training:** learn on the job; no reading required.
