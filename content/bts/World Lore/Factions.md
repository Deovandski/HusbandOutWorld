# Red Winga

Laura Forway White-Bell's faction (see Characters). Red Winga has a strong sense of principles, which Laura requires:

- If anyone asks, say "We are here to please you."
- Keep track of your surroundings at all times.
- Sleep your way into becoming a proxy, and you shall no longer swallow. Otherwise, swallow like no other and rise until you become the next Madam (of a given brothel; still a proxy business under Laura).

**Upkeep:** upper-class by design. That's how Laura attracts high-interest clients and extracts information to sell on the black market or hold as blackmail. A sick worker is healed and put back to work. Hired mercenaries aren't members and don't wear the choker; they carry a metallic square badge stamped with Red Winga.

**Area of operation:** their entertainment houses are centered around Forway, serving an upscale clientele and gathering information on targets. Their proxies are often side businesses that agents deal with.

**Recruitment:** they "recruit" Wozefaries by acquiring them in the desert before the Flatland people do, or pick young humans who are hired by a proxy of their own will. They're raised inside the proxy's business (brothels, flower shops, etc.), and when they come of age, Laura gives them contracts to sign. Then the proxy and the Madam decide whether each becomes an Agent or an Entertainer, depending on how much they trust them and whether they'd fit Laura's other "business" endeavors. All agents are raised through the proxies, and anyone who disobeys the proxy or Laura is forced into "entertainment" and dirty jobs. Young people are lured in by all kinds of promises, especially a richer life. After working very loyally as an entertainer, someone might be promoted to proxy; some agents become proxies too.

**Identification:** only certain agents who work as entertainers wear the Red Winga choker, as a sign of disobedience and distrust.

# Pussy Power

Guilds of women adventurers who banded together into a super guild to help each other out. But with too many chefs in the kitchen, things quickly turn to shit. Forway hasn't put them on the wanted list like the Bagarisa Brothers; only the minority that apparently grew too empowered, disturbing the peace and acting as if they were Forway guards. They mostly help women and children they see in harm's way, but sometimes misjudge the situation and cause unnecessary grief, like beating a father to a pulp after his child said something stupid about not getting the promised candy.

**Area of operation:** decentralized.

**Recruitment:** woman to woman.

**Identification:** their insignia, worn on dog tags; they wear their badge proudly on their right arm.

**Members:** 50+, and they all seem to have equal voices.

# Black Magina

The best-known faction behind Mind Enabler, which Forway banned. They rose to "success" by never killing anyone: all their attacks are carried out with nari gels, using Nagina to put everyone to sleep. They hold firmly to never killing anyone, no matter how problematic the person is. They run on a franchise model, to keep a constant supply and stop other gangs from supplying Magina.

**Area of operation:** the Freeish outskirts.

**Recruitment:** mostly villagers looking for more money in life.

**Identification:** a Magina petal hidden in their clothes.

**Members, per franchise:** 1 Chef, 1 Herbalist, 10 Cultivators.

# Wood People

Once a famous guild of Wood People killers, it fell apart when some bad Wood Yabuca (apples) joined and steered it into banditry. Adventurers went after their heads, and they now seem disbanded, though some say otherwise. They act like the Blue Wozens but blame the Wood People instead, and their motive is simply easy money, not revenge. They often harass villagers for protection money against the Wood People, or deliberately set the Wood People on the village to attack it mindlessly.

**Area of operation:** mostly Tolerideme to Forway.

**Recruitment:** bandit-to-bandit word of mouth.

**Identification:** a guild badge hidden inside their clothing.

**Members:** 5 masters, 15+ apprentices.

# Bagarisas Brothers

Brothers who lost their families to bandits from another faction and became vigilantes. Their belief in "guilty until proven innocent" goes against Forway Law, so they're always on the run.

**Area of operation:** all over, seeking out injustices and solving them with their bare fists.

**Recruitment:** bars, and people who lost family to bandits.

**Identification:** a secret handshake: make tusks with your index and pinky fingers and touch the other person's "tusks".

**Members:** 2 Dark-Red Bagarisas, 17+ Bright-Red Bagarisas.

# Triangle Lovers

Three small bandit groups in Triangle City joined forces to control the city from the shadows, which worked, given the city's shady past. They're few compared to the city's size, but they work hand in hand with city officials to keep as tight a grip as possible on people's opinions and actions.

**Area of operation:** Triangle City.

**Recruitment:** bandit to bandit, or exceptionally well-behaved citizens.

**Identification:** a metal badge.

**Members:** 3 Trianglers, 30+ Lovers.

# Fallen Tree

Ex-adventurers who fell from grace after growing fed up with the Adventurer's guild's predatory revival fees. They turned to banditry, targeting Forway treasuries specifically. Most of their attempts so far were uncovered or fought off, and the ex-adventurers who were caught were tortured in hidden places. The rest are on the run, surviving day to day by targeting Forway guards and any small Forway fish they can sink their teeth into.

**Area of operation:** on the run.

**Recruitment:** adventurer to adventurer.

**Identification:** a small paper drawing they hide away, as a badge of their honor and intentions.

**Members:** 14+.

# Beer Balls

A group of fat men who turned to banditry for extra beer money. They were mocked and brought to justice on their first heist. Other bandit groups now use the name to move around more easily, using the joke as cover for their conversations and plans, while officials are still trying to work out how many impersonating groups there are and how they differ.

**Area of operation:** local, depending on the impersonating group.

**Recruitment:** bandit to bandit, or bars.

**Identification:** varies. Some hide a paper stamp in their fat folds.

**Members:** unknown; many other small bandit groups use the logo.

# Blue Wozens

Created by "the boss": see Kozaku Forway.

**Recruitment:** mostly bandits who know other bandits.

**Identification:** a blue square badge with a Wozen drawn in the middle.

# Hidden Sorenos

Built by a small group of Forway's richest people to do all kinds of crazy deeds in the background, especially to curb their enemies' success without leaving a paper or blood trail: a way around the bureaucracy and those in power. They're slow to plan and act, but extremely precise when they strike.

**Area of operation:** from Forway throughout the rest of the kingdoms, through their agents.

**Recruitment:** the rich aristocrats' most trusted people are enrolled as Sorenos.

**Identification:** a small Soreno badge hidden in their underwear.

**Members:** 4 leaders, 15+ Sorenos.

# Black Sapphires

![][image11]

There's no official artwork; some people assume this would be it.

A group inside Forway's administration that seeks power by unifying Forway into a single nation, then bringing every leader under its command. Their goal is to usher in a new age of prosperity by finishing what the courtroom scientists started but didn't have the balls to finish: toppling all the kingdoms. Not the same as the Hidden Sorenos, but with somewhat similar goals.

It was started by three Sapphire adventurers and has since grown to include a couple of Forway City officials. Since their plans move very slowly, they specialize in research into eternal life: whoever is left is mostly focused on keeping their souls alive as long as possible, while some try to crack the truth about the ancient species above Hifuna, make contact with them, or even dominate them (another urban legend, but based in truth).

To most people, they're an urban legend. The core members are few and far between, but they still have pull through the Forway ranks. If you're under their gaze, you can disappear without a trace and never be seen again. Some of what people blame on them is true; some is wrongly pinned on them.

**Area of operation:** all regions, since Sapphires are accepted everywhere.

**Recruitment:** only trustworthy Sapphire adventurers.

**Identification:** they're extremely careful, so they leave no paper trail.

**Members:** a handful of Forway City officials, plus some Black Sapphire adventurers.
