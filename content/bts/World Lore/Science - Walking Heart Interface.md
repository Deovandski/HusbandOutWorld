# Walking Heart Interface

Walking Hearts are stones that form in the irradiated zones of Hifuna. They usually grow to about the size of a fist and are black, but colors glow within the black, and those colors tell what type of stone it is and what powers it will likely grant whoever replaces their heart with it.

Forway banned their use because of the deaths and the corruption of character they often cause. And since the person becomes undead, they give up their rights as a citizen.

![][image1]

#### How a stone binds

A stone holds five groups of enhancement: red, yellow, green, blue and purple. The person's mind and the stone's color ratios have to become one. If they don't, the person never wakes up. If they do, the stone binds to the person's soul and they become undead, and the color of their skin shows which powers are bound to them. A stone grants one to three enhancements from a group.

As long as the person still has a soul, the stone keeps feeding off it to power the body.

#### Living with one

- Their skin, eyes included, changes to the color of their enhancement group. Kozaku Forway, the Darfaries who leads the Blue Wozens, is one: the stone melded with him perfectly, then corrupted his mind.
- Their heart no longer beats, and they no longer eat, drink, breathe or sleep. They recharge only through emotions, or by stabbing a nari stone into their heart.
- The stones are impure by nature, so one person can rarely develop two colors at once.
- When they feel a strong emotion that matches a color bound to their soul, a flame of that color rises from their chest.

# Strength (Red)

- **Emotions likely to run rampant:** revenge, anger, berserker rage.
- **Benefits:** extra strength; builds muscle easily.
- **Drawback:** agility.

# Stamina (Yellow)

- **Emotions likely to run rampant:** anxiety, jealousy, hoarding.
- **Benefits:** runs without getting tired; can fight or work for hours on end; uses soul energy more efficiently.
- **Drawback:** dexterity.

# Intelligence (Blue)

- **Emotions:** neutral and balanced, and at worst emotionless: robotic, almost catatonic, a blank slate.
- **Benefits:** memory, intellectual processing, planning, understanding knowledge.
- **Drawbacks:** loses strength, agility, dexterity and stamina; likely a shorter lifespan.

# Dexterity (Green)

- **Emotions likely to run rampant:** depression, embarrassment, sadness.
- **Benefits:** fine motor coordination; ranged weapons strike truer; harder to notice, if they train their walk, since they no longer breathe.
- **Drawback:** stamina.

# Agility (Purple)

- **Emotions likely to run rampant:** fear, terror, grief.
- **Benefits:** faster reflexes; faster healing.
- **Drawback:** strength.
