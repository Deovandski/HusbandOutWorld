# Finaies Interface

A natural evolution of the Finaies that lets them use a basic, elemental soul energy interface. A Finaies draws the spell's logogram in the air with the left hand, and soul energy shapes the spell from that movement. Finaies hands have only two fingers, branch-like.

#### Casting a spell

In short: draw the spell in the air, hold to charge it, then release.

1. **Shape the spell.** Draw its logogram in the air (see Language › Finaies Interface Language), then hold the left hand's two fingers with the other hand for the element to appear.

   ![][image1]

2. **Hold.** Keep the fingers clenched to power up the holding hand. The longer you hold, the stronger the spell, and the more soul energy it costs. Other Finaies can touch you to give you soul energy and power the spell up faster.

   ![][image2]

3. **Release.** Separate your fingers to release the spell, or cock your arm back for a ranged attack.

   ![][image3]

# Cat. Elemental
