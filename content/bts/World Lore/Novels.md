# From Blood Bag To Cattle

It all began when humans awoke on the islands of the Buhraies Plains. The green trees and peaceful nature contrasted with the pink and red trees that seemed to watch over them from all around.

A paradise of beautiful overlords who taught them and brought them all kinds of food soon turned into a brutal reality: humans were walking food rations for the Buhraies, and their blood tasted sweetest when they feared most for their lives. They ran around like wild game, until some figured out that the Wood People, as they came to call the pink and red trees, only looked frightening and did nothing (the Buhraies kept them chained with Fost stones). The Buhraies, though, seemed to attack at random, almost as if they enjoyed keeping humans on edge.

Some humans covered themselves in dirt and leaves to stay out of sight and spread out, living in the forests while they looked for a way to escape. That's how they discovered the East Dip: a cliff with faults they could use to shorten the climb down and soften its steepness. Over a few years, they gradually weakened the cliff until it collapsed. With that, humans were freed from the Buhraies' fangs, and fell into the hungry mouths of the Lishies.

- **Characters:** none.
- **Era:** ~2,100 to ~1,650 B.G.

# Fanged Love

A male human was tied down for a Buhraies to feed on, but he was more excited than afraid, for the Buhraies' beauty was a perfection of nature. As she fed, she felt his lewd aura tickling hers, so she bought him from the experimentation island and brought him home, where she kept healing and teasing him while she fed on him.

The baby they had was sent back to the experimentation island, and it soon died. The couple kept trying, but every baby died soon after. The Buhraies took her revenge on the male and killed him, and only then discovered that the problem wasn't him, but the incompatibility between their races. She brought him back to life, but their love had died too: a fruit of love would never be born.

- **Characters:** a male human; a female Buhraies.
- **Era:** ~2,050 to ~1,978 B.G.

# Avenger of Twenty

The heroic tale of a man on the coasts of Aldemeri who grew up with three girls he loved and had 20 children with them. The Lishies ate them all in a single day, while the men were out hunting.

Consumed by vengeance, he paired with a mostly red Walking Heart stone. With pure rage coursing through his blood, he killed hundreds of Lishies and was one of the first to set back the Lishies' advance. But rage fueled his heart, and he became a berserker, completely losing himself: he hunted in the forest and ripped apart anyone in his path, until other heroes had to hunt him down and destroy the stone that had replaced his heart.

- **Characters:** Jonem.
- **Era:** ~957 to ~920 B.G.

# Logical Arrow

A girl whose parents hid her the moment the Lishies struck and ate her entire village. She waited patiently for them to leave, then made her way through the forest from village to village, warning whoever she could, and saved many lives with her heroic act.

With her natural dexterity and analytical mind, she paired with a blue and green Walking Heart stone, which gave her full control of the bow and extra intelligence, and she began leading small armies against the Lishies, strategically and with great success. But she overstressed her body without feeding it enough emotions, and one day she dropped dead. (Nari stones were only discovered years later.)

- **Characters:** Elijah.
- **Era:** ~960 to ~940 B.G.

# The Smart Ladies

The Buhraies unchained the Wood People, and over the years they massacred humans across the Buhraies Plains, until humans found a way to use some Wood People as a barrier against the others. One of the villages that formed was led by three ladies, who came up with scientific ways to farm the land better. It was not an easy job, and the stress made them binge eat and gain weight quickly. As the successful villages around what is now Tolerideme grew stable, people looked up to them, and roundness became the norm.

When they died, they were cremated with Wood People wood, and their ashes were spread across the land for good luck. Tolerideme is what it is today because of what they did.

- **Characters:** Evedyn, Everyn and Evelyn.
- **Era:** ~810 to ~750 A.G. (as written; see the Q&A).

# The True Sanbi King

A legend tells of a young fisherman who claimed a massive Sanbi fish talked to him: "Humans taste good." He nearly soiled himself as the fish came closer. Then it smiled: "You look too young. Old humans taste the best! Hmmm… chewy skin! Give us chewy old people, and we give you our dumb young ones—yes!" The young fisherman faintly said, "Yes," and the massive fish disappeared into the depths.

He told everyone what had happened; some fishermen did just that, and it worked. The story spread like wildfire, but it seems only the fisherman's relatives can catch Sanbi fish with ease. That family is now the biggest family in Sanbi.

- **Characters:** a young fisherman.
- **Era:** ~60 A.G.

# Ganyrami Flanker

The heroic tale of sacrifice for the greater good: Machado, leading an army of 5,000, chose to flank Ganyrami instead of helping his brother in the frontal battle for the city. His brother died in a trap the enemy had set, along with most of his men, but in return Ganyrami fell and all the ancient species there were put to the sword, a special moment for humans. Machado never used a Walking Heart stone, since his sister died trying to use one.

- **Characters:** Machado.
- **Era:** ~0 A.G.

# Gabiro Sundeath Scientist Presents. A deeper dive into Buhraies' Soul Analysis

The first book from [Gabiro](/lore/characters/#gabiro-sanbi-royal)'s Buhraies soul research: an old, tattered book with simple bindings and a golden, handwritten title. He wrote it while working in Sundeath, one of a few books of his that nobody seemed to care for. Gabiro argues that the Buhraies, the masters of the soul, really did clone people by splitting their souls in half, and that they also did the reverse, creating life from two or more sources: the core of how they extended their lifespans and gave existing specimens new traits. Entirely new specimens, like Bruntos and Naked Bruntos, came from tapping into the soul realm and binding souls to donor flesh. [Harry](/lore/characters/#harry-sanbi) reads it in Vol 2.

A second book covers using Buhraies soul analysis to identify soul markers of different species, testing standards and so on. Gabiro also suggested using it for paternity tests or finding missing people, and maybe fingerprinting everyone for an alternate badge system. It just didn't fly.

- **Characters:** Gabiro.
- **Era:** ~986 A.G.

# Strong Walking Heart

A teenage girl from a tribe seeking greater power was cornered by a group of male Buhraies. Seeing no choice, she gave in; they took her to their house and abused her. Her mind stayed strong, and she took mental notes of everything she saw, including strange stones in their basement. They let her go, since killing her would be work, especially the cleanup.

She hid in the forest, waited for them to leave, then broke into the house and stole the stone along with their notes. Back in her village, she was the first to choose the surgery, because more than anything she wanted to stop grieving the disappearance of her parents. A mostly purple stone was implanted in her heart, which let her take revenge on the male Buhraies. But her emotions went awry, and she took her own life a few days later.

- **Characters:** a female human; male Buhraies.
- **Era:** ~939 B.G.

# Prosperity Treaty’s Heroes - Part I

In 640 A.G., an incredibly smart man named Freeish Paulin felt compelled to change things, so he began building a secret base in what is now Forway City to overthrow the kingdoms.

In 648 A.G., Paulin was betrayed and sold to the Gandew Kingdom to be assassinated. But as the King of Gandew learned what Paulin was trying to do, he came to agree with most of it, overthrowing the current kingdoms included, mainly because he realized that otherwise everyone would end up dead and nothing would be solved. He still had to have Paulin killed to save face with the people, but Paulin had given him all his connections, so the king began building a new base in what is now the Freeish Kingdom. He entrusted one of his closest allies (and mistresses) with leading the organization, and made her Courtroom Scientist under the guise of "better war preparations".

- **Characters:** Freeish Paulin.
- **Era:** ~640 to 648 A.G.

# Prosperity Treaty’s Heroes - Part II

In 650 A.G., the courtroom scientists of the Gandew, Tolerideme and Sanbi Kingdoms met at the hidden base to plan the overthrow: Asa the Powerful for Gandew, Adrian from North Tolerideme for Tolerideme, and Bijura Moacir for Sanbi. They slowly conditioned the people to accept their ideas and stirred up revolts ("the stones were controlling the people"), until the kingdoms lost their grip on their people and could no longer go to war with each other. Then the rebellion truly began, and it toppled all three kingdoms:

- The King of Gandew was executed in front of everyone, calm and relieved despite it all. Only a few people know what he truly did.
- The King of Tolerideme was starved to death.
- The King of Sanbi was humiliated in public, spanked by many people, then dropped alive into the ocean.

The plan succeeded, and the courtroom scientists became the new kings. They wrote the Prosperity Treaty and Forway law so that no more wars over greed would break out between them. They created a common currency, formalized a calendar starting from the year Ganyrami fell, and built other tools to put everyone on the same page and make trade easier. Embassies were founded soon after, and all ruins and major cities became Forway property. Sundeath tried to stay out of it all, but gave in under the sheer pressure from Forway; in exchange, it has Pharaohs as city leaders.

- **Characters:** Freeish Paulin; Asa the Powerful; Adrian from North Tolerideme; Bijura Moacir.
- **Era:** 650 A.G. on.
