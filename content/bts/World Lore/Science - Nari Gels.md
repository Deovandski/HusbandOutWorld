# Isekai History

Nari gels are the quickest and safest way to act on any living soul: they're ready to swallow or inject, and their tiered effects are easy to reproduce. Ground nari dust is infused with water into a jelly-like consistency, then mixed with other substances, and maybe catalysts, to get whatever effect you want. The coarser, cheaper grades are easier to swallow, but their transformations tend to be more painful and slower to kick in. You can pay a premium for very fine-grade gels, which are easier to inject, especially if you want to keep the pain as low as possible.

A wide range of nari gels covers most poisons, and body and mental status changes: from swapping your eyeballs for those of someone with better vision, to turning yourself completely into a Big Winga so you can soar the skies freely! Because they're tiered, the difference between smoothing your skin and melting it off (as you suffer the worst imaginable pain and scream for someone to end your life) can be just three scoops.

On the plus side, your liver will eventually process and expel the changes, so if you're on your own and put yourself in jeopardy, sit back and relax! If it doesn't kill you, you'll eventually get back up and do it again! On average, the coarser, cheaper stuff takes longer to expel, so keep that in mind. Either way, do it enough times and your body might start accepting the changes as its new reality, changing your idea of normal forever. It won't be cheap, unless you're getting high on your nari gels.

**Resistance:** flesh can grow immune to basically every nari gel. Someone who keeps changing "skin" to look like someone else will notice their body taking longer to shift, and hurting more, even with the highest-quality gels (a purer gel gives cleaner, stronger effects). Overuse can also leach into the soul: adventurers who insist on looking a certain way find that, given enough time, Nari Refresher returns their body to somewhere between how they were born and how they looked the longest. It's as if the nari gel leaves a ghost imprint on the soul, which might affect how strongly soul and body connect. (World Lore Q&A › Vol 4 Draft 2, Q16.)

Scientists, usually alongside businesspeople, try to outdo each other with the latest and most extraordinary recipes. Unfortunately, the market has a reputation for being cutthroat: sometimes the breakneck pace of research, or shady cost-cutting in testing, turns unaware buyers into "alpha testers" of new products and their claims.

Everyone buys these gels, adventurers usually more than commoners. A wise adventurer always keeps a Nari Refresher ready, to reset every affliction caused by other nari gels—unless that adventurer is using fake-body nari gels to alter their personality, which isn't unheard of, but definitely isn't recommended.

# Distance Cost Tier

Goods are sorted into four tiers by how they look to a common person in Vraki: from cheap things anyone buys, to things only the very rich can afford (a common person might buy something as expensive as one sapphire once in their lifetime; for a really rich person, that's ordinary). The boundaries between tiers are blurry on purpose: it's a spectrum, and the tiers just help picture it.

Nari gels by tier:

- **Tier I (Cheap Goods):** none.
- **Tier II (Common Goods):** none.
- **Tier III (Expensive Goods):** nari gels.
- **Tier IV (Nobility Goods):** banned nari gels: Mind Enabler, plus Skin Melt and Sleep Inducer stronger than Tier I. Forway forbids them, so they're found on black markets.

# Sustained Soul Consumption

There's no upfront soul cost: the soul energy the effect needs is contained in the gel itself. But some nari gels put a constant tax on the user's soul, like an ability in an RPG that costs a % of your total mana or slows your mana regen. While the effect lasts, the soul energy you generate each day (in soul units) is reduced by its daily consumption. If you don't generate enough, your soul energy drains; near zero, the body-to-soul connection starts to detach, you lose consciousness, and soul death soon follows. So these gels are only truly worth it for adventurers or researchers with stronger soul regeneration.

On average, the daily consumption by tier:

| Tier | Nari soul a day |
|---|---|
| I (Cosmetic) | ~1 |
| II (Localized) | ~2 |
| III (Regional) | ~4 |
| IV (Whole Body) | ~8 |

## Mind Enabler

Made from ground nari with Koalin brain matter, eyes and mustache, mixed with blood. Smells like preserved meat or bloody sausage. Tastes like grainy toothpaste and raw old meat, and fatty, since it's brain matter. (See World Lore Q&A › Vol 4 Draft 2, Q15.) This item is forbidden by Forway. Found on black markets.

- **Inventor:** Sexual predators, at some point during B.G.
- **Subversions:** Eori's Mind Eraser (#37): Koalin brains only, or Koalin brains, eyes and mustache. The goal is to wipe all memories of oneself: a blank slate.
- **Cost:** 145–205 Pearls for ~200 g.

It affects people differently. Some might even remember what happened after keywords or actions are seen again, like someone’s voice.

| Tier | Effect |
|---|---|
| I (Cosmetic) | Difficult walking, confused memories. Disrupts the connection between soul and self. Might affect some people differently. |
| II (Localized) | Memories clash, easier to extract info at times, but it could also mean wrong info if the person hallucinates. Dreams become memories and vice versa. |
| III (Regional) | Rambling, unintelligible speech; memories don't form well. |
| IV (Whole Body) | Death, coma. |

## Time Quicken

It's legal: anyone can buy it at the nari shops across towns, and it's free for kingdom back-office staff. It's like a powerful nicotine. (See World Lore Q&A › Vol 4 Draft 2, Q14.)

- **Inventor:** Hunters, at some point during B.G.
- **Subversions:** Controlling headaches, how “high” it makes the person feel, flavor, etc.
- **Cost:** 45–65 Pearls for ~200 g.

| Tier | Effect |
|---|---|
| I (Cosmetic) | Removes sleepiness; focus; quicker reflexes; anxiety. The feeling of time moving slowly can feel off to some people. In some versions, fight-or-flight is kinda on all the time, too. |
| II (Localized) | Fight-or-flight is switched on. If left untreated, it can cause the person to collapse within a few hours. |
| III (Regional) | Body and brain speed up even more; injuries start to compound from sudden movements. |
| IV (Whole Body) | Extreme reflective senses and heightened muscle responses, but will cause brain aneurysms, muscle tears, coma, and death on its own. |
