# Distance Cost Tier

Goods are sorted into four tiers by how they look to a common person in Vraki: from cheap things anyone buys, to things only the very rich can afford (a common person might buy something as expensive as one sapphire once in their lifetime; for a really rich person, that's ordinary). The boundaries between tiers are blurry on purpose: it's a spectrum, and the tiers just help picture it.

Nari bombs by tier:

- **Tier I (Cheap Goods):** none.
- **Tier II (Common Goods):** Signal/Smoke Bombs.
- **Tier III (Expensive Goods):** none.
- **Tier IV (Nobility Goods):** none.

# Signal/Smoke Bombs

Adventurers use smoke bombs to signal each other. A bomb is set off alongside a Mist Create spell and fired upward, or dropped in water: water is the catalyst that starts the smoke. Forway Law made the colors official, and misusing them is punished:

| Color | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Red | Help |
| Cyan | Conquered / complete |
| Green | Safe / go ahead |
| Yellow | Retreat |

**Invented by** early adventurers, to communicate with other groups.

**Variations:** extra colors, smoke that lasts longer, or more smoke. Gabiro enhanced a deep blue, non-explosive version that Sanbi uses at a soul sendaway: at Gabiro's own sendaway on the old wharf (Vol 3), the balls were dropped into the water and a colorful, deep blue smoke bubbled up.

**Effects by strength:**
- **Weak:** a little lingering smoke.
- **Medium:** more smoke, and a higher sizzling sound.
- **Intense:** an explosion. It can smoke out enemies, damage hearing, or even choke people, depending on how enclosed the place is. It always explodes as soon as the energy is infused, so it hurts whoever infuses it.

**Cost:** 10–30 Pearls for about 100 g.
