# Fiera’s Hut

An old lady, Fiera, took care of every passerby, whatever their origin or destination, and some like-minded people decided to stay and help strangers too.

**Food:** Fiera's baked pies, from recipes passed down through the generations. Deliciously moist.

# Jack Wood

A village not too far from Forway City, where Harry and Dalila got on a wagon. It's called Jack Wood because its early residents were famous for robbing travelers by blocking the road with trees (hijack, jack).

# Green Sea

A village east of Sanbi. The village chief, Tupinamba, is old and cranky but realistic: when he says something is shit, it's because it is. He seems about as old as Gabiro, but a bit fatter and taller.

# Likenia

A village west of Northpoint, founded by someone who was nearly the Lishies' food, though it never grew much, for whatever reason. It has a lighthouse.

# Long Bridge

A village between Sanbi and Forway. Back in the day, crossing the river was a must, so a village grew around this resting point. The village elder, Tomanius, is a caring older guy. The bridge is four times as long as the reference, a bit like the one in Skyrim.

# Rich Island

Usually one or two people in this "village", who dare to stay isolated long enough to get rich. Its lighthouse keeps boats from straying too far northeast by accident, and boats coming from Newland from going too far south.
