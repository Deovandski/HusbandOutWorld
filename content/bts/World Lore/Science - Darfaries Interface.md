# Darfaries Interface

The Buhraies created this interface as an enhancement to their own soul energy interface, in an attempt to control the Darfaries as a versatile army of foot soldiers.

The logograms are the guardrails. Every race with the Darfaries interface can try to override them by using a knife to reach the Buhraies interface underneath, as Gabiro does in the Vol 1 prologue. It's dangerous: an empty logogram forces the user into immense concentration, and the odds aren't in their favor. So for common folk, it's basically like being unable to reach the "ancient roots" of the interface.

Soul energy shapes the spell using logograms drawn from the knowledge shared by the people nearby.

#### Sigil rings

A sigil ring brings forth a spell: its logograms make soul energy mutate and do what the caster wants.

- Each ring has one spell carved into it, such as Body Restore or Shock Create.
- The best-known spells (healing, reviving, elemental and more) can be bought at any Soul Energy shop.
- Blank sigil rings are forbidden, to prevent accidents. Only courtroom scientists and Aquamarine or Sapphire adventurers may use them, to experiment with new spells.
- Nari sticks (nari stones chained together) fuel the spell. A single-use nari stick is spent in one cast, and they aren't cheap.

#### Interface shells

When a spell is cast, five rings appear around the hand holding it. Each shell holds a limited number of soul energy tiers:

| Shell | Tiers |
|---|---|
| I (smallest, closest to the hand) | 2 |
| II | 8 |
| III | 18 |
| IV | 32 |
| V (largest, farthest from the hand) | 50 |

#### Compound objects

This is where some progress has been made in extending the interface. Nobody has managed to change its OBJECT and VERB structure, but people have packed more information into the object while keeping the casting consistent. Forway hasn't approved a new compound object for sigil rings in a while: there's a single requirement, but it's incredibly tough to meet. Random passersby are asked to use the experimental sigil ring, and if any of them miscasts, the compound object isn't approved.

#### Casting a spell

In short: choose the spell, put energy into it, and release it. A spell has two parts:

- **Subject:** the target of the spell. With no subject, the soul energy falls back on the caster's own idea of how it should apply itself.
- **Verb:** the change being applied. With no verb, the soul energy falls back on the caster's own idea of what it should do.

Either way, a miscast explodes in soul flame in the caster's hands.

1. **Choose the spell** (see Language › Darfaries Interface Language). Either put on a sigil ring:

   Or draw the logograms by hand. The scientific community pushed back too hard. Opinion shifted in the early 900s A.G., after many scientific tragedies with no clear progress, and by the late 900s A.G. only fringe scientists like Gabiro Sundeath (also known as Gabiro Sanbi Royal) still seemed to be investigating it.

2. **Start the spell.** Clench the fingers of the hand holding the spell and the soul energy. The Darfaries shells appear as you do. With a sigil ring:

   Drawn logograms are forbidden inside city and kingdom perimeters, because of the risk of explosions and miscasts:

3. **Infuse soul energy.** The casting hand takes in energy when the caster "eats" with it. A spell is only stable at a soul energy tier, so energy always fills up to the next tier (after a partial charge, it adds the difference). The energy can come from:
   - **Someone's soul energy:** when the person touches their middle and ring fingers, the Darfaries veins in their hand light up with soul flame (every race that came after the ancient species has them: Darfaries, Wozefaries, Sorefaries and humans), and the caster's other hand "eats" the flames.

# Entangle

Gabiro brought Entangle back from the history books. Many had tried before him, but the side effects were always too strong; Gabiro understood how it worked in principle far better than anyone. Most people don't use it day to day, but it's a fundamental tool for the adventurers and guards who protect them.

**Uses:** mainly to entangle the sounds reaching people's ears, so they all hear the same thing. It can be overwhelming: sounds echo when people are close to each other, and it gets harder to tell where sounds other than voices are coming from.

# Transmute

The Buhraies either were more efficient with this interface or used something else that gave them a leg up. Forway made the Transmute symbol official purely to help research.

**Uses:** active research is trying to figure out how the Buhraies used it to shape the land and do all kinds of shit with it. Some researchers think it could outright make gold, which seems possible, since people have transmuted these (it just doesn't make much economic sense):

- Manganese → Iron
- Iron → Cobalt
- Cobalt → Nickel
- Nickel → Copper
- Copper → Zinc
- Gold → Mercury
- Platinum → Gold

# Soul Create (Book I Chapter I Gabiro’s version)

Among researchers, a faint rumor calls it "Uncontrolled Create".

Creates a soul with specific soul genetic information, using flesh as the base. The incantation tries to picture the target and think aloud about its characteristics.

**What happened in Vol 1, Chapter 1:** Gabiro was trying to bind a specific soul. A lack of proper concentration and not enough nari power made all the soul and body genetic information suddenly meld, triggering a White Hole event, and the soul energy of everyone involved fused into a single being. In that event, a nearly decayed soul (Harry's) was bound by pure chance. What was left of the soul tied to the bagarisa corpse used in the "summoning ritual" was wildly different from Harry's. When the explosion happened, Gabiro's own soul was fused into the mix too, and Harry's soul absorbed the capacity of all three. That's how Harry woke up able to speak Vraki, the common language, and why he later develops a custom interface (Vol 4).

A White Hole (also called White Fire) is what happens when soul energy doesn't convert properly into the desired outcome: it backfires, explodes, or becomes nothing instead of something. These events aren't quite an urban legend: scientists and researchers know about them. They were better known in the old days, before Forway really became a thing and resentment against messing with the soul became commonplace.

![][image8]

**Tiers:** the more soul energy (and flesh) put in, the higher the chance of getting a soul to your specifications. The more things you seek in a soul, the more its power is diluted across those skills: ask for someone good at singing, baking and herding, and the energy poured in will most likely be spread evenly among the three. At times the distribution comes out erratic, because the spell "captures" and summons a soul in decay that happens to match the summoner's requirements. It also de-ages the target soul.

Researchers don't fully understand the process. In reality, the spell doesn't create a soul: it tries to pull a disconnected soul into the flesh you want, where it melds with that flesh's own leftover soul connections into a final hybrid. Sex and giving birth are somewhat like that process too: giving new life is basically mixing two souls, giving part of yourself for a newborn to form.

# Cat. Body

Body manipulation spells are easier to cast, but they can be risky if the caster doesn't know the side effects. Some people may develop side effects not listed here.

The mass affected follows the General Power Effects: 0.1 kg at Tier I, doubling with every tier (51.2 kg at Tier X).

# Body VERB

- **Increase/Decrease:** the whole body grows or shrinks.
- **Repel/Attract:** the target repels or attracts other bodies. With no target, the soul flame simply evaporates.
- **Restore:** soul energy uses the target's soul genetic information to find what's out of place in their body, and corrects it using stored energy to fuel the changes. More below.
- **Disassemble:** tries to explode the body.
- **Create:** tries to recreate the target's body, born where the spell was started. It must be fed Black Honey to grow the clone, and it stays lifeless unless the original is killed, since only one being can connect to a soul. Cloning yourself shortens your lifespan and raises your cancer risk, because the soul's link is forced to thin out, so it's heavily discouraged.
- **Destroy:** tries to destroy the target's whole body. At low tiers it only causes internal or external bleeding; medium tiers can cause convulsions; high tiers cause an explosion.

![][image9]

#### Healing with Body Restore

- Put lost limbs close to the body, so regrowing them doesn't take as much energy. Severe damage makes the body use a lot of energy to heal.
- Restore and Create started out as two different spells. But the logograms are an interface to raw soul energy, whose meaning all users share as fact, and over time both came to mean the same thing: regrow what's missing from the body, be it a finger, or everything else if only a finger is left. If no genetic information remains, see Cat. Soul or other interfaces.
- It only restores the base genetic information; it doesn't deal with whatever is causing the damage, such as worms.
- If healing isn't happening, the target must be fed Black Honey or another energy-dense food. Five bottles of Black Honey are like eating tens of 2,000-calorie junk food meals. It's expensive, but there's plenty to go around.
- Healing matches the flesh to how the soul looks, so old scars don't exist in Vraki unless someone is too poor for nari gels or the Darfaries Interface. Tattoos are removed as soon as Body Restore is used.
- A piece of someone's flesh can be grown with Body Restore to see their real body (to identify a disguised intruder, for example).
- Body parts for later use are kept in jars of a viscous red jelly: finely ground nari stone mixed with blood.

**Revival, step by step** (as Morika does it in Vol 4):

1. Nari Refresher, injected with a glass syringe, undoes nari gel body mods.
2. Body Restore, through a sigil ring fueled by nari sticks.
3. When the body runs out of fuel, Black Honey finishes the healing, so the target doesn't turn skeletal from undernourishment.
4. Shock Create restarts the heart. Storm Create at Tier II or more also works (higher the fatter the person), but it creates a localized storm that harms the skin, so it's more of a "punishment revival".

**What each tier can heal:**

| Tier | Heals |
|---|---|
| I | Bruises |
| II | Minor cuts and minor blood loss |
| III | One or two deeper cuts and minor blood loss |
| IV | Minor broken bones and medium blood loss |
| V | Multiple broken bones and major blood loss |
| VI | Missing legs or arms |
| VII | A missing head |
| VIII | A missing torso |
| IX | Near-instant regeneration, if there's enough fat or Black Honey to heal with |

# {BodyPart} VERB

- **Increase/Decrease:** the body part grows or shrinks.
- **Repel/Attract:** the target repels or attracts the chosen body part. With no target, the soul flame simply evaporates.
- **Restore:** restores a specific body part (see Body VERB).
- **Disassemble:** tries to explode the body.
- **Create:** creates the chosen body part on the target as if it had always been there. Feed Black Honey to grow it.
- **Destroy:** tries to destroy the chosen body part on the target by explosion.

# Cat. Material

Material manipulation is an experimental Darfaries research branch, meant to manipulate the world around.

# World VERB

- **Increase/Decrease:** the target grows or shrinks, but its weight stays the same, since its density changes. If the density goes up too much, the item shrinks into infinity and disappears forever. It can harm an item whose function depends on its density. It can turn one metal into another through fusion, but it explodes most of the time.
- **Repel/Attract:** the target repels or attracts everything around it (gravity still affects it). With no target, the soul flame simply evaporates.
- **Restore/Disassemble:** Restore doesn't put things back together from their pieces; it just makes the object look newer. Disassemble does the reverse: it rusts an item. It's basically the reverse of chemical reactions.
- **Create/Destroy:** Create does nothing. Destroy explodes the target into a shower of extremely fine particles and gas, basically a fine dust; at lower tiers, it might only destroy the object's outer shell.

![][image10]

**Tiers:** the mass affected follows the General Power Effects (0.1 kg at Tier I, doubling with every tier).

# {Object} VERB

Among certain researchers, a faint rumor says it can also be used as "Uncontrolled Create", with bigger potential gains and risks.

- **Increase/Decrease:** the target grows or shrinks, but its weight stays the same, since its density changes. If the density goes up too much, the item shrinks into infinity and disappears forever. It can harm an item whose function depends on its density. It can turn one metal into another through fusion, but it explodes most of the time.
- **Repel/Attract:** the target repels or attracts everything around it (gravity still affects it). With no target, the soul flame simply evaporates.
- **Restore:** shapes the item into the desired object by combining elements around the caster, basically the reverse of chemical reactions. Have the elements it's made of nearby to improve the odds; otherwise the repaired object may break again if the new connections aren't strong.
- **Create:** tries to create objects from the target using all the elements around. Have the elements it's made of nearby to improve the odds.
- **Destroy:** explodes the target into a shower of extremely fine particles and gas, basically a fine dust; at lower tiers, it might only destroy the object's outer shell.

![][image10]

**Tiers:** the mass affected follows the General Power Effects (0.1 kg at Tier I, doubling with every tier).

# Cat. Elemental

Spells in this category can be released into an object, or in the air, or as a projectile if you cock your arm back and release the spell as you push it forward.

Every elemental spell works the same way:

- **Increase/Decrease:** behaves like Create/Destroy.
- **Repel/Attract:** the target repels or attracts the element. With no target, the soul flame simply evaporates.
- **Restore/Disassemble:** draws the element into the target from its surroundings, or explodes it out of the target into its surroundings.
- **Create/Destroy:** Create makes a ball of the element for a ranged attack; Destroy turns the target's element back into virtual particles. With no target, the soul flame simply evaporates.

The ball's size follows the General Power Effects: 0.1 cm at Tier I, doubling with every tier (51.2 cm at Tier X).

# Gravity VERB

- **Increase/Decrease** and **Restore/Disassemble:** behave like Create/Destroy.
- **Repel/Attract:** the target repels or attracts the element. This is the one meant for gravity control. With no target, the soul flame simply evaporates.
