# Embassies

Embassies were created right after the Prosperity Treaty, so Forway's influence could reach every city and kingdom involved. The area inside an embassy's marked boundary is officially Forway City territory. Forway guards are always present, and some even guard the embassy as Big Wingas (guards wearing the Big Winga nari gel).

#### Where and what

Every kingdom and major recognized city has an embassy, and they all report to the Forway City Palace. Every embassy smells of stamp wax and sweaty adventurers coming and going (sending and receiving Winga letters is one of their core jobs), and has a blackboard with the entire Prosperity Treaty carved into it in clear white lettering.

#### Who runs it

**The embassy captain (its Forway general)** is the highest authority of their embassy: over the whole city if Forway manages it, otherwise over the embassy grounds, while the kingdom around it belongs to whoever owns it. Generals carry the gem ranks too. Kingdom embassies have Aquamarine or Sapphire generals (the Forway embassy in Sanbi has an Aquamarine General), since Forway officials must trust their own future to them: there, the job is diplomacy and handling the relationship with a reigning king or queen, or an elected official. City embassies usually get a lower-rank Pearl or Amethyst General, partly because the cities are smaller, but also because they're under Forway's rule to begin with, so diplomacy isn't really much of a thing; a smaller city's embassy serves a more functional purpose: Winga mail, badges, adventurer quests, revivals, banking, etc. Sapphire Generals work at the Forway level, so their decisions affect everyone, and kingdoms with voting power help decide who becomes one. There are no archdukes in Vraki.

Captains have been essential to keeping stability, peace and prosperity ever since the Prosperity Treaty. Some are loved, some feared, sometimes both, depending on who you ask, but they all work toward the same goal. Why would someone of that stature dress like a guard, with only a fancy cap to set them apart? Forway makes all generals' armor and social clothing the same as the soldiers', so they remember where they came from and the impact of their actions: it's easy to get lost in the numbers and make a decision that looks good on paper but strains the relationship between the kingdoms, or the people, and Forway.

#### Birth documentation and badges

Every newborn should be registered promptly with a badge from the local embassy; it's the only way to use the embassies' services. It's common for people of Tier I and II status not to have a badge, or to get one later in life.

A badge is official identification, especially for adventurers as they're promoted between tiers. It's about the size of a credit card, normally made of soul wood (hence its greenish color), with the holder's name carved in and the Forway logo on the back; a fresh one still smells of burnt wood. Four balls decorate the corners: iron for a regular citizen (like Eiri's in Vol 1), or matched to the rank for Amethyst, Aquamarine and Sapphire adventurers. Black wood badges are for city officials, kingdom representatives and embassy representatives: in Sanbi, only Itagiba Sanbi Royal and Lofinghy Sanbi Forway General have one (Itagiba's corners have four distinctly colored shiny balls: red, blue, green and yellow). A badge costs 1 amethyst.

How do you stop someone from getting a second badge at another embassy? Forway has specialized Wingas fly samples of people's blood, mixed with ground nari stone, from every corner of the world to Forway, where the central soul repository is. Running it is such a behemoth of a cost that it would be far too much for any single kingdom. A central repository also makes it possible to identify a corpse mangled beyond recognition, found by the side of the road.

#### Adventurers

Adventurer is officially a Forway City job, created to keep order and keep tabs on the rumors spreading through the kingdoms: a kind of decentralized military. Retired adventurers and Forway City officials rank adventurers to match them to quests. Recognitions and promotions are handed out once a year, at the Adventurer feast every Paulin 100th. It's a way for people of lower stature to get a better life, though more dangerous than city life (that is, if they don't die too much).

- **Tier V, Black Sapphire (hidden):** See [Factions › Black Sapphires](/lore/factions/).
- **Tier IV, Sapphire:** Well-rounded in many parts of life, including battle and conflict management (talking with kings and queens, investigations, etc.). Most seem loyal to Forway City and to keeping its power throughout the kingdoms, and a handful are tight-lipped, especially about Forway's private matters. Requires Adventurer School and many years of loyal service.
- **Tier III, Aquamarine:** Tackles more dangerous quests: wiping out bandit camps, dangerous beasts, Hifuna control. Highly sought-after bounty hunting. Requires Fundamental School, or passing a test.
- **Tier II, Amethyst:** Often carriage protection, beast and bandit hunting, and related requests. The de facto fauna hunters, often paired with Pearls for training and a helping hand. Some bounty hunting; sets bandit traps.
- **Tier I, Pearl:** Bottom-of-the-barrel tasks: helping with lighthouse keeping, construction outside cities, etc. They often travel from city to city to find work, gain experience and grow their soul energy, and help Amethysts.

Under the Prosperity Treaty (Section #2, Sub 3), no adventurer may carry out judgment unless authorized by a Forway guard or a kingdom representative.

#### Revival services

Any adventurer can be revived at any embassy if they choose. When taking a quest, they accept a deadline, after which a search party is sent to recover their body and try to finish the quest for them. If the body is found, they're revived, and Forway takes 50% of their profit on every other quest until the debt is repaid. If they die again before repaying it, they're forced to become Forway guards; if they flee, they become wanted criminals. Some people consider this effectively slavery.

If an adventurer's body isn't found within a reasonable time for their soul to still be revived, Forway processes them as "Presumed Soul Dead". Forway keeps the badge so no one can impersonate them, and every guild they belonged to is notified: their personal inventory and services move to the top guild they chose, or are split between guilds according to the will they made when signing up. If they had no guild, the state donates their belongings to charities within one calendar year.

#### Banking, guild and storage services

- **Banking:** available at any embassy. Kingdoms manage their own money instead of keeping it all in Forway City, so it stays decentralized. Withdrawals and deposits mean submitting a request and waiting a few days while the embassy confirms the funds with Forway. Loans are available, with fees depending on whether an individual or a guild is asking. Investments are possible too, but only in Forway-backed projects; individual projects go through direct contracts.
- **Guilds:** for one sapphire, you can create an officially recognized guild. The embassy keeps the official member list by duplicating members' badges and storing them in-house. Guild services include property management for certain members, asset management, storage, etc. Adventurers and families use it widely to share resources.
- **Storage:** kingdom-wide. Whatever you deposit at one embassy can be picked up at any other. Don't expect quick turnaround, since costs are kept down by moving things whenever cargo space is available, but it's for sure the most secure way to store your stuff.

#### Winga courier

Every embassy offers Winga courier services; kingdoms tend to run their own separate Winga couriers for more private communication (see Kingdoms).

- Winga mail is the size of a postcard. The envelope comes in the destination kingdom's color, with a seal on top, and the back is the same color as the front.
- Forway holds the price at 1 Pearl to send a letter to any embassy. The envelope comes with a single white page; you can buy premium paper at the embassy or bring your own, as long as the letter weighs no more than four pearl balls. Anything heavier goes by private mail or the storage service.
- From, To and the embassy name are written in white ink (sometimes another color when needed).

#### Contracts

- Contracts need the blood signature of everyone involved, mixed with nari on signing. There are no household crests on documents: the blood signature is the seal, and a contract made on someone's behalf needs that person's blood.
- Whoever issues a contract is responsible for taking the right blood from the right person. That's why public contracts are often signed at an embassy, where a proper witness makes sure it's formal and legally binding. Kingdoms can have their own contract authority, just as legal as Forway's.
- Every contract has nullification clauses, which must be on the back.
- To verify a contract, place the person's badge on the signature field: if the blood signature matches, the badge base (where the thumb goes) lights up.
- People moving contract-bound individuals carry their contracts, plus a letter for the Forway officials inspecting cargo, stating who is contracted to deliver whom.

#### News

Relaying official news is part of an embassy's job. Every day around Monnalow, the local Forway captain ends their shift by going to the public square and shouting out the day's news from Forway. Weekly printouts covering the week are available at every embassy every Forrday; they're more of a thing in Tolerideme and Forway, where more people can read. Everyone else prefers the shouting (shoutcasting) or word of mouth.

- **1. General** (1 Pearl + rewards): The cheapest. Usually people looking for specific things, or wanting to brag; over the years it turned into a marketplace for odd things. Posted on the general board and in the weekly Forway News printout, where anyone can read it or ask a guard. Pearl adventurers sometimes check this board, but don't count on it.
- **2. Local Warning** (1 Amethyst + rewards): Missing people, personal protection, etc. Usually, all quests are Local Warnings: the usual "Pay To Be Heard". Posted on the quest board, which adventurers are far keener to check, and the captain announces every open quest and news item of this level each Monnalow. Some companies and traders advertise here too. Posting may be delayed while they investigate.
- **3. Kingdom Alert** (1 Aquamarine + rewards): Has the Buhraies castle spilled a bunch of undead garbage again? Has a kingdom official been taken hostage? Do you have a lot of money and want something done fast, or want everyone to know? On top of the quest board and the Monnalow shoutcast, the captain immediately sends shoutcasts across the kingdom's main cities and villages to gather every available adventurer (if needed), or just shouts the news as it is. Posting may be delayed while they investigate. Once shoutcast, it can't be taken back: the embassy can only soft-de-escalate a posted search, not shut it down. Whoever posted it can withdraw it, but has to announce the withdrawal publicly, and embarrassment follows, plus likely punishment from Forway depending on what they did. A kingdom that issues an emergency "for the lolz" is heavily punished, with sanctions and the withdrawal of comparative advantage rulings.
- **4. Emergency** (1 Sapphire + rewards): For when shit hits the fan, like an external invasion, or an army of Hifuna monsters marching down toward Forway. It has never happened since Forway was established, so it's a news type in theory only, and some Forway higher-ups have pushed to remove it, since keeping every embassy ready for an emergency costs resources: staffing at least a hundred adventurers with lodging, food and fighting supplies within three days, defending against a Tier X spell, etc. Posted immediately.

#### Quests

Jobs offered to adventurers. Some require a minimum rank; for local quests, see the local embassy.

| Quest | Minimum rank |
|---|---|
| Soul Energy Assist, Fauna Hunting, Construction Assistance (in the wild, such as roads), Lighthouse Assistance, Road Maintenance Assistance | Pearl |
| Area Surveillance | Pearl around cities, Amethyst out in the wild |
| Message Spreader (around cities and villages when there aren't enough Forway guards) | Pearl |
| Cargo Protection, Beast Hunt, Bounty Hunting, Bandit Traps, Training, Village Checker | Amethyst |
| Missing Person (in some places, including boat and person rescue) | Amethyst |
| Bandit Extermination, Personal Protection, Adventurer Hunter/Rescuer operations, Archeologist Assistance | Aquamarine |
| Kingdom/Guild/Business Law Compliance (secret) | Sapphire |

**Missing Person:** an adventurer can retrieve the missing person's badge to confirm them, but if they lose it, they pay 10 times the quest reward or face forced labor. They have 1 week to return the badge; after that, a fine equal to the reward is added every week (up to 10 times), and a warrant is issued for their arrest.

# Guilds

People with similar skills, trades or character band together to share ownership of their assets. Since people have no last names, a guild is also how an individual stands out.

**Family:** "relatives" and "family" are well-known words, but last names don't exist; people use the guild in place of a family name.
