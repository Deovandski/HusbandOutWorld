# Cinika

A small cyan butterfly that spreads everywhere every spring. Known for attacking Wozefaries and Sorefaries in swarms and paralyzing them to lick their sweat. One of the most popular butterflies around: when they swarm and flee, adventurers and commoners know bad weather is coming.

- **Inspired by:** Butterfly.
- **Habitat:** Lower plains.
- **Tastes like:** Hairy flowers, earthy-like.
- **Food:** Flower nectar.
- **Intelligence:** Butterfly.
- **Reproduction:** Every spring.
- **Interface:** Antenna.
- **Skills:** Detect Pressure; Detect Humidity; Detect Temperature; Paralyze.

#### Economic value

- **Cinika Butterflies**: Freshly killed nearby and stored in a wagon with ice, ready for the butcher shops or to be shipped out.

# Culuim

These worms ate soil infused with Walking Heart, and over time the constant feeding and forced use altered their souls.

- **Inspired by:** Worms (average).
- **Habitat:** Ponds. Someone dumped Walking Hearts into a pond near the Freeish Kingdom; the worms ate them, and it altered their souls.
- **Tastes like:** Dirt. Very earthy.
- **Food:** Ground.
- **Intelligence:** Worm.
- **Reproduction:** By themselves or with other Culuim.
- **Interface:** Walking Heart.
- **Skills:** Its carapace can flip between Walking Heart modes at will, activating the Walking Heart by inflating it with blood, though it can't seem to keep that up for long.

#### Economic value

- **Medallions**: Used for some nari gels, but also chopped up and grilled; part of the Freeish Region's cuisine.

- **Cuisine**: See Freeish Kingdom.

# Egua

A heavy horse with a hippopotamus-like head. Herbivores that didn't mind being around other species, they were quickly domesticated. They love being petted and understand basic instructions, but hurting them, physically or emotionally, makes them violent. Their Soul Energy interface lets them walk on water naturally, which makes them the basis of transportation across the Shared Delta. Humans respect their friendliness so much that Eguas are often given human burials. Some people do eat their meat, but mostly when the opportunity arises (older Eguas, for example).

- **Inspired by:** Protorohippus; Hippopotamus; Ardennes Heavy Horse Breed.
- **Habitat:** Throughout the four kingdoms; wild ones live on the plains around Forway City.
- **Tastes like:** Bland, old-tasting beef, with a texture like a tougher top sirloin, since only old Eguas are eaten.
- **Food:** Mainly about 20 kg of grass, hay or Carbey a day.
- **Intelligence:** Horse.
- **Reproduction:** Females give birth once a year.
- **Other:** Gallop (~30 km/h); 5km @ ~10% Load (Max Load of ~90 kilos); Canter (~20 km/h); 15km @ ~10% Load (Max Load of ~90 kilos); 5km @ ~20% Load (Max Load of ~180 kilos); Trot (~10 km/h); 35km @ ~10% Load (Max Load of ~90 kilos); 15km @ ~20% Load (Max Load of ~180 kilos); Gait (~7 km/h); 65km @ ~10% Load (Max Load of ~90 kilos); 35km @ ~20% Load (Max Load of ~180 kilos).
- **Interface:** Hair on legs.
- **Skills:** Water Walk; Dirt Repel; Increase/decrease size of legs; Eject their privates or intestines through their asshole (very specific, but that's how they got away from certain predators).

#### Economic value

- **Egua Meat**: Meat cut by a butcher. Relatively cheap, since it comes from older Eguas that no longer carry cargo or people.

# Legunia

Birds native to the deserts, where they spend most of their lives; they come down to the lower plains for the breeding season.

- **Inspired by:** Flamingo with Winga mix.
- **Habitat:** Lower plains.
- **Tastes like:** Chicken meat near the bone, fattier and stronger.

- **Intelligence:** Cockatoo.
- **Reproduction:** Every year; ~3 eggs laid, of which only a few reach adulthood.
- **Interface:** Feet & beak.
- **Skills:** High Jump; Super Speed.

# Winga/Pink Winga

Big flying birds that eat Gyut berries, and their meat tastes sweet. They settle high in the trees to rest and breed, and may attack humans who come too close, depending on past encounters. They're famous for flying fast and high, which makes them hard to hunt. Tamed, they make loyal companions, and since they recognize people's faces and places, they make a great courier service. Pink Wingas, which some call Tolerideme Wingas, are incredibly hard to find, since they camouflage themselves really well.

- **Inspired by:** Macaw.
- **Habitat:** Throughout the Lower and Buhraies Plains, with a larger population in Wingarest.
- **Tastes like:** Honey turkey breast, but not as tender.
- **Food:** Primarily Gyut Berries.
- **Intelligence:** Parrot.
- **Reproduction:** 3 or 4 Winga couples get together for a swing party, then build a shared nest to care for the eggs, which hatch in less than a month. About 20 Winga eggs are laid every year.
- **Interface:** Head feathers.
- **Skills:** Sense Lifeforms; Generate Air Shield; Louder-than-usual shriek; strengthening the beak and claws to diamond level, so they cut through anything.

#### Economic value

- **Winga Meat**: Meat cut by a butcher.

  ![][image5]

- **Winga Jerky**: Jerky prepared by a butcher.

  ![][image6]

- **Winga Feathers**: Feathers, prepared by a butcher for artisans to work on.
- **Winga Egg**: A treat for many, thanks to its sweet taste.

- **Winga Dust**: Usually ground, using other beaks and claws to do it. They must have been strengthened by the Winga before they were removed, and the effect lasts for years before it starts to wear off. The coating can be as strong as diamond until the effect wears off.

# Wooly

Created by a Buhraies who fell in love with a Lishie and had to prove himself, with something that would help the Lishies, to be accepted into the mountains. A hybrid of cow and sheep DNA that only thrives at high altitudes, where it's colder and drier. Its milk and wool are a staple of Gandew's economy, and so is its meat, from older females, or males without the best genes or health. When dying or giving birth, it lets out a massive scream that can be heard for kilometers.

- **Inspired by:** Cow & Sheep.
- **Habitat:** Nowadays they only live in the Gandew Kingdom, and in the wild in the high mountains that border the desert (Old Fuhrair), at about 4 to 6 km. They would suffocate in their own fur at altitudes < 4km, or the humidity would spoil their fur. They were meant for high altitudes.
- **Tastes like:** Beef with a slight note of mutton.
- **Food:** About 18 kg of Domar Carbey a day, plus about 2 kg of Gyut berries for richer milk; about 20 kg of Carbey a day for meat production.
- **Intelligence:** Cow.
- **Reproduction:** One to three Woolies a year.
- **Interface:** Horns.
- **Skills:** Reverberate Voice; Create Light.

#### Economic value

- **Wooly Meat**: Meat cut by a butcher.

- **Wooly Hide/Fur/Pelt**: Hide/fur, prepared by a butcher for artisans to work on.
- **Wooly Horns**: Horns, prepared by a butcher for artisans to work on.
- **Wooly Milk**: Females fed only Carbey produce about 8 liters of milk a day, tasting somewhere between cow's and sheep's milk. Mostly available only in Gandew.

# Sanbi

The Sanbi Kingdom is named after this fish, its main food source and economy. It's famous for taking all of a normal human's strength to reel in: even muscular fishermen catch at most 2 adults a day, if they're lucky, unless they tap into nari gels and the like. More in the novel The True Sanbi King.

- **Inspired by:** Kushlukia.
- **Habitat:** Mainly in the ocean to the east of the Sanbi Kingdom.
- **Tastes like:** Tuna.
- **Food:** Gulps down smaller fish.
- **Intelligence:** Tuna/Shark.
- **Reproduction:** Tens of Sanbi fish a year.
- **Interface:** Hair on the head and chin.
- **Skills:** Sense Surroundings; Communicate in radio waves; Emit strong radio waves that cause migraines in most people; Inflate/expand their lips, then rupture them out of their bodies as fake prey. Usually as an escape maneuver; They can do the same with their tails and grow them extra long to evade beasts/hunters.

# Sayna

Looks like a fox, but is as rocky as a horned lizard. Very friendly with humans, and a good addition to an adventurer's party. It makes a cute sound: "Pohw-pohhhhwww!"

- **Inspired by:** Fennec Fox; Horned Lizard.
- **Habitat:** Desert.
- **Tastes like:** Earthy and sandy, like fox meat, with a minty taste similar to lamb, but not as tough as you'd imagine. A delicacy, eaten only by some people, since most think they're too cute to eat. Sundeath worships them.
- **Food:** Insects and small rodents.
- **Intelligence:** Dog, great for a companion. Golden Retriever. Goofy.
- **Reproduction:** Every fall, 2 or 3 Saynas are born.
- **Interface:** Eyes/Ears.
- **Skills:** Distant Hearing; Cloak detection; Blood Shield (self-defense only).

# Wozen

Night hunters, known for snacking on humans when given the chance. They run fast and are skilled in close combat with their claws. They tend to be defensive, except at night, when they come out to hunt.

- **Inspired by:** Vulpes Vulpes.
- **Habitat:** Mostly in the dense forests around Blood City and the Freeish Kingdom.
- **Tastes like:** Gamy and tough.
- **Food:** Loves eating Bagarisa, and snacks on humans given the chance.

# Bagarisa

A pig- and deer-like mammal whose meat isn't too gamy: a bit earthy, with hints of mushroom. Famous for shrugging off arrows, since an instant kill means shooting both of its hearts: one in the chest, and another in the gut, which has its own pathways that can also power the hind legs. Its musk smells like shrimp brine.

- **Inspired by:** Babirusa.
- **Habitat:** Throughout the lower region.
- **Tastes like:** Deer with notes of olive oil.
- **Food:** Omnivore: mostly grass, but likes to snack on Sorenos once in a while.
- **Intelligence:** Wild Boar.
- **Reproduction:** Females give birth to 2 or 3 babies every 3 months.
- **Interface:** Hair on Back/Tusks on fronts.
- **Skills:** Blood Heal; Flaming Tusks (balls of flame created between the tusks); a gust of hot air, built up from the tail and shot through the hair crest, which combined with the flame creates a flamethrower effect.

#### Economic value

# Fanchaki

A Buhraies subjugation experiment: the Buhraies took a few chakis (chickens) and transformed them into fanchakis. As usual, both escaped containment, but the chakis went extinct, eaten up by the fauna and the Lishies. Fanchakis breed fast (like rats), so they survived and spread like wildfire, even with the Lishies hunting them by the thousands.

- **Inspired by:** Chicken.
- **Habitat:** Most common in the dense forests along the coast and around Freeish and Blood.
- **Tastes like:** Fatty chicken, very tender.
- **Food:** Mainly seeds and insects.
- **Intelligence:** Chicken.
- **Reproduction:** Lays one egg every day; fertilized eggs hatch in about 21 days.
- **Interface:** Wattle.
- **Skills:** Speedy Reaction; Fake Body.

#### Economic value

- **Fanchaki Meat**: Meat cut by a butcher. The kingdoms' favorite meat, for how good it tastes for the price.

  ![][image38]

- **Fanchaki Jerky**: Jerky prepared by a butcher.

  ![][image39]

- **Fanchaki Feathers**: Feathers, prepared by a butcher for artisans to work on.
- **Fanchaki Egg**: A common food.

  ![][image40]

- **Tamed Fanchaki**: Some keep them as pets, but they're mostly livestock. Some use them as bait for predators, especially for their Soul Energy abilities.

# Soreno

Small mammals that eat insects and grain. They prefer insects, so in small numbers they're great pest control; in large numbers, they become pests themselves. Hunters and Pearl adventurers are constantly hired to keep them in check.

- **Inspired by:** Rats & Shrews.
- **Habitat:** Throughout the four kingdoms and their cities, and in the wild, mostly in open fields.
- **Tastes like:** Bitter, and smells like urine. The meat is given to wild animals or used as fertilizer, though some Pearl adventurers still eat it, because it's free meat.
- **Food:** Insects and grain.
- **Intelligence:** Rats.
- **Reproduction:** About 10 are born every few months.
- **Interface:** Tail/Ears/Mouth.
- **Skills:** Cloak (temperature and vision); Hearing; Long-distance communication; Imitating animal voices; Shooting themselves in any direction with a quick motion.

#### Economic value

- **Hide/Fur**: Hide/fur, prepared by a butcher for artisans to work on; incredibly flexible to work with.
- **Tamed Adult Soreno**: They take a while to train properly, but they can relay conversations over long distances without other humans hearing, because of their high pitch. A secret trick of the Hidden Sorenos gang.

# Yuyta

A classic case of convergent evolution.

### Yuyta meat

###

- **Inspired by:** Gopher/Gerbil; Hops around like a gerbil.
- **Habitat:** Mostly the forests of the Sanbi region and up the coast.
- **Tastes like:** Chicken, but hard, only good for stew, and it doesn't freeze well. It smells clean and mild, almost like fresh tuna without the ocean smell: more like faint wet grass, like being downwind of someone cutting grass.
- **Food:** Grass.
- **Intelligence:** Like a rabbit or so. Tends to be in groups. Tends to be defensive/run away. When you get close to it, it tends to hiss. If attacked, it screams like a dying human.
- **Reproduction:** Every few months.
- **Interface:** Jumping Feet; Microwave detection (whiskers); Can spot objects in the walls/dirt. It’s like an infrared camera of sorts.
