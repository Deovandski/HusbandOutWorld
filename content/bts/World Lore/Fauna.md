# Cinika

A small cyan butterfly that spreads around every Spring. Known for attacking Wozefaries/Sorefaries in herds and paralyzing them to lick their sweat. One of the most popular butterflies around, as it gives adventurers and commoners a sense if there is any bad weather approaching when they create swarms and flee from the bad weather.

- **Inspired by:** Butterfly.
- **Habitat:** Lower plains.
- **Tastes like:** Hairy flowers, earthy-like.
- **Food:** Flower nectar.
- **Intelligence:** Butterfly.
- **Reproduction:** Every Spring.
- **Interface:** Antenna.
- **Skills:** Detect Pressure; Detect Humidity; Detect Temperature; Paralyze.

#### Economic value

- **Cinika Butterflies**: Fresh kill from not too far off, stored in a wagon with ice, ready for butcher shops or to be shipped around.

# Culuim

These worms consumed soil infused with Walking Heart, and over time, the constant feeding and forced usage altered their soul.

- **Inspired by:** Worms (average).
- **Habitat:** Ponds. Someone dumped walking hearts in a pond near Freish kingdom, and the worms ate it, and it altered their soul.
- **Tastes like:** Dirt. Very earthy.
- **Food:** Ground.
- **Intelligence:** Worm.
- **Reproduction:** By themselves or with other Culuim.
- **Interface:** Walking Heart.
- **Skills:** The carapace can flip between Walking Heart modes at will; It activates the Walking heart by inflating it with blood. It doesn’t seem capable of doing it for an extended period.

#### Economic value

- **Medallions**: Used for some nari gels, but also chopped up and grilled. It is part of the cuisine in the Freeish Region.

- **Cuisine**: See Freeish Kingdom.

# Egua

Heavy horse with Hippopotamus characteristics to its head. They were herbivores who did not mind being around other species, so they quickly became domesticated. They love to be petted and can understand basic instructions. Hurting them physically or emotionally will cause them to grow violently. They naturally can walk on water due to their Soul Energy interface. The basis of transportation across the Shared Delta. Humans respect their friendliness to the point that they often have human burials for this species. Some do eat their meat, but most of these people eat when the opportunity arises (e.g. older Eguas.)

- **Inspired by:** Protorohippus; Hippopotamus; Ardennes Heavy Horse Breed.
- **Habitat:** Throughout the four kingdoms, wild specimens can be found in the plains where Forway city is located.
- **Tastes like:** Bland, old-tasting beef. The texture is like that of tougher top sirloin, especially since only old Eguas are used.
- **Food:** Primarily ~20 Kg of Grass, Hay or Carbey.
- **Intelligence:** Horse.
- **Reproduction:** Female Egua gives birth once a year.
- **Other:** Gallop (~30 km/h); 5km @ ~10% Load (Max Load of ~90 kilos); Canter (~20 km/h); 15km @ ~10% Load (Max Load of ~90 kilos); 5km @ ~20% Load (Max Load of ~180 kilos); Trot (~10 km/h); 35km @ ~10% Load (Max Load of ~90 kilos); 15km @ ~20% Load (Max Load of ~180 kilos); Gait (~7 km/h); 65km @ ~10% Load (Max Load of ~90 kilos); 35km @ ~20% Load (Max Load of ~180 kilos).
- **Interface:** Hair on legs.
- **Skills:** Water Walk; Dirt Repel; Increase/decrease size of legs; Eject privates or intestines through assholes (very specific, but that’s how they got away from certain predators.).

#### Economic value

- **Egua Meat**: Butcher cuts meat. Relatively cheap meat due to the source being older eguas who no longer transport cargo/people.

- **Egua Jerky**: Butcher prepared jerky.

  ![][image3]

# Winga/Pink Winga

Big flying birds that eat Gyut berries. Their meat tastes sweet. They settle high up in the trees to rest/procreate. Will attack humans if they come too close, depending on past interactions. Are famous for being able to fly fast and high, which makes hunting not as easy. When tamed, they make loyal companions. They recognize People’s faces and places, so they make a great message courier service. Pink Wingas are also referred to as Tolerideme Wingas by some. They are incredibly hard to find as they camouflage themselves really well.

- **Inspired by:** Macaw.
- **Habitat:** Present throughout the Lower & Buhraies Plains. Larger population on Wingarest.
- **Tastes like:** Honey Turkey breast, but not as tender.
- **Food:** Primarily Gyut Berries.
- **Intelligence:** Parrot.
- **Reproduction:** ~3 or 4 pairs of Winga couples get together for a swing party, then they all create a shared nest to take care of the eggs. It takes less than one month for the eggs to hatch; Every year ~20 Winga eggs are laid.
- **Interface:** Head feathers.
- **Skills:** Sense Lifeforms; Generate Air Shield; Loud than usual Shriek; Making the beak/claws cut through anything by strengthening it to diamond level.

#### Economic value

- **Winga Meat**: Butcher cuts meat.

  ![][image5]

- **Winga Jerky**: Butcher prepared jerky.

  ![][image6]

- **Winga Feathers**: Butcher prepared feathers for artisans to work on.
- **Winga Egg**: A treat for many people due to its sweet taste.

# Wooly

Created by a Buhraies that fell in love with a Lishies, and had to prove himself with something that would help the Lishies in order to be accepted into the mountains. A hybrid between cow & sheep DNA  that only thrives in higher altitudes where it’s colder and drier. A staple of Gandew’s economy is its milk and wool. Also, for its meat for older Woolys Females or Male Woolys who don’t have the best genes or health. When dying/giving birth, it hurls out a massive scream that can be heard for kilometers.

- **Inspired by:** Cow & Sheep.
- **Habitat:** Nowadays, they only exist within the Gandew Kingdom and out in the wild in the high up mountains that separate the desert (Old Fuhrair) ~4 km-~6 km. They would suffocate in their own fur at altitudes < 4km, or the humidity would spoil their fur. They were meant for high altitudes.
- **Tastes like:** Cow with a slight sheep flavor note.
- **Food:** Can eat ~18kg Domar Carbey per day + ~2kg of Gyut Berries for richer tasting milk. ~20kg Carbey per day for meat production.
- **Intelligence:** Cow.
- **Reproduction:** One-Three Woolies per year.
- **Interface:** Horns.
- **Skills:** Reverberate Voice; Create Light.

- **Wooly Milk**: Females produce ~8 Liters of Milk a day by feeding just Carbey. Taste is on the spectrum between cow and sheep milk. Mostly only available on Gandew.

Famous for being resistant to bow attacks, as you need to shoot both of its hearts if you are going for an instant kill. One in the chest. Another in the gut as the gut has its special pathways that can also power the hind legs. Musk that smells like a shrimp brine.

# Fanchaki

Buhraies' subjugation experiment. Buhairs got a few chakis (chickens) and transformed them into fanchakis. As usual, both of them fled their containment, but chakis grew extinct as the fauna/Lishies ate it all up. Fanchakis are quick to breed (like rats) so they survived and spread around like wildfire, even though Lishies were hunting them in the thousands.

- **Inspired by:** Chicken.
- **Habitat:** More common around the dense forests throughout the coastline and the dense forests around freeish and Blood.
- **Tastes like:** Fat Chicken. Very tender.
- **Food:** Primarily seeds and insects.
- **Intelligence:** Chicken.
- **Reproduction:** Lays one egg everyday; Fertilized eggs will hatch in ~21 days.
- **Interface:** Wattle.
- **Skills:** Speedy Reaction; Fake Body.

#### Economic value

- **Fanchaki Meat**: Butcher cut meat. Favorite meat among the kingdoms for its good taste for the price.

# Soreno

Small mammals that feed on insects and grain. They prefer insects, so in small numbers, they are great pest control. In large numbers, they become a pest themselves. Hunters/pear adventurers are constantly hired to keep them in check.

- **Inspired by:** Rats & Shrews.
- **Habitat:** Throughout the four kingdoms and related cities. Also found throughout the wild, but more in open fields.
- **Tastes like:** Their meat tastes bitter and smells like urine. Their meat is given off to wild animals or put in the ground as fertilizer. Some pearl adventurers still eat it because it is free meat.
- **Food:** Insects & grains.
- **Intelligence:** Rats.
- **Reproduction:** Every few months ~10 are born.
- **Interface:** Tail/Ears/Mouth.
- **Skills:** Cloak (temperature/vision); Hearing; Long distance communication; Imitate animal voices; Shoot themselves in any direction with quick motion.

#### Economic value

- **Hide/Fur**: Butcher prepared hide/fur for artisans to work on. Incredibly flexible to work with.
- **Tamed Adult Soreno**: It takes a while for them to be trained properly, but they are able to relay conversation over long distances without other humans hearing them due to their high pitched nature. A hidden trick of the Hidden Sorenos gang.

# Yuyta

A classic case of convergent evolution.

### Yuyta meat

![][image41]

###

- **Inspired by:** Gopher/Gerbil; Hops around like a gerbil.
- **Habitat:** Mostly forests in the Sanbi Region and up the coast.
- **Tastes like:** Chicken. Hard meat, only good for stew. Doesn’t freeze well. The smell was clean, mild, almost like fresh tuna, but lacking that distinct oceanic smell. Rather, it took a faint wet grass smell like being downwind from someone cutting grass.
- **Food:** Grass.
- **Intelligence:** Like a rabbit or so. Tends to be in groups. Tends to be defensive/run away. When you get close to it, it tends to hiss. If attacked, it screams like a dying human.
- **Reproduction:** Every few months.
- **Interface:** Jumping Feet; Microwave detection (whiskers); Can spot objects in the walls/dirt. It’s like an infrared camera of sorts.
