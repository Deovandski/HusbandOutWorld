# Documentation Notation

- **Races:** species with intelligence near or above humans'.
- **Beasts:** mildly intelligent species, or highly dangerous beasts.
- **Fauna:** low-intelligence species.

Elves and the other Tolkien races don't exist in Vraki. Growth, lifespan, soul recharge per day and tier averages per race are in the [Soul Energy spreadsheet](Legacy/Behind%20The%20Scenes/Spreadsheets/Formulas%20-%20Soul%20Energy.xlsx) (Race Soul Avg).

# Finaies

The primordial race (see the World Timeline). Inspired by trees.

- **Habitat:** above Hifuna.
- **Food:** tree sap.
- **Nature:** emotionally charged, somewhat childish, territorial and long-lived.
- **Language:** an old version of Vraki, like really old English, hard to understand.
- **Reproduction:** pollination between females, by rubbing on one another.
- **Interface:** the Finaies Interface. Like the Darfaries interface, but weaker and simpler; it works like a nerve spreading through their body, letting them sense their surroundings and make up for their poor eyesight.
- **Skills:** elemental control.

# Fuhraies

An ancient race (see the World Timeline). Inspired by snow elves and trees.

- **Habitat:** snowy mountains.
- **Food:** soul energy from the air.
- **Nature:** highly intelligent, risen above the common issues between mortals. Their bodies run on literal cold fluids with antifreeze.
- **Language:** Vraki.
- **Reproduction:** pollination between females, by rubbing on one another.
- **Interface:** the Fuhraies Interface. Like a nerve, closer to the Darfaries interface than the Finaies one, and more "animal" in nature. They could use their bodies to send out waves to sense their surroundings, and even to talk to each other over distances.
- **Skills:** elemental control.

# Lishies

An ancient race (see the World Timeline), focused on battling and hunting. They couldn't use the Finaies interface and never developed their own. Dying in battle was the death they preferred. Extremely horny: think Mokina levels. Inspired by orcs and trees.

- **Habitat:** the lower regions.
- **Food:** meat.
- **Nature:** brutes; everything was solved through violence.
- **Language:** Vraki.
- **Reproduction:** about 6 months' gestation, 1 to 3 babies. Lishies + humans = nothing.
- **Interface and skills:** none.

# Buhraies

An ancient race (see the World Timeline), split off from the Finaies. After gaining a taste for blood, they grew ever more powerful in exchange for their physical strength, but their lust for power and curiosity made them incredibly smug and greedy. Inspired by vampires and trees.

- **Habitat:** the Buhraies Plains, an island where Endlessfog is now.
- **Food:** blood and bone marrow.
- **Language:** Vraki.
- **Reproduction:** about 14 months' gestation, 1 baby. Buhraies + humans = a baby that dies within a few months of birth.
- **Interface:** the Finaies Interface and the Buhraies Interface.
- **Skills:** a powerful soul energy interface, with extensive experimentation. Because they focused so much on controlling the soul itself, some gained control over their own soul: they could shape-shift, reattach their soul to another living body, and even talk to someone else through a kind of quantum entanglement, on a soul level. It was very tricky, though, and they had an incredibly hard time teaching and spreading these skills, because each soul is unique.

# Darfaries

A new race (see the World Timeline), from Lishies + Buhraies. They can be born with a wide range of hair and eye colors; females tend to have lighter hues and males darker ones. Both men and women are tall and muscular.

- **Habitat:** mostly the Gandew Kingdom, but some live elsewhere, since not every Darfaries agrees with the Gandew rules.
- **Food:** omnivores.
- **Intelligence:** 1.5 times humans' in every faculty.
- **Language:** Vraki.
- **Reproduction:** about 15 months' gestation, 1 to 3 babies. Two Darfaries may have a Wozefaries instead. With a Wozefaries, a Sorefaries is born. With a human, the baby is born human-like but with the Darfaries' soul energy capabilities, sometimes with energy closer to a human's: whether they have the Darfaries interface shows which way their genes and magic lean.
- **Interface:** the Darfaries Interface.

#### Darfaries veins

The whole species shows the marks of controlled flight: soul-energy organs in the form of veins across the upper body. They aren't actually veins, but nerves, and the skin there is more sensitive than normal. Darfaries, Wozefaries and Sorefaries all have them; Darfaries have the largest.

- The veins burn with a base white flame that converts soul energy directly, and show white fire when casting spells. When dampening a fall or flying, a black flame appears on them. The color can differ for someone who developed their own interface, or really messed with their own Darfaries interface and somehow didn't die in the dangerous process. The black flame adjusts its strength on its own if the person thinks of it like a jetpack, or a spaceship's monopropellant thruster. Their feet dangle, so they steer with their whole arms.

  ![][image1]

- Darfaries are too big for the interface to do much, so they can't fly, but it isn't nothing: if they're not too heavy, it softens a fall a bit, so they take about 80% of the fall damage instead of 100%.
- They can feel any leftover soul energy signature as a tingling, by smell, by taste, or in their sexual organs.
- They can neutralize any kind of soul energy by putting their saliva on it.
- With the most prominent veins, Darfaries finely sense soul energy disturbances around them, plus a rough feel for humidity, wind and air pressure. As they say, if a Darfaries tells you to buy an umbrella, you'd better buy one!

From Vol 1, Chapter II: "Its red bumpiness was warmer than the rest of her hand and had a certain toughness to it, but as his finger pressed slightly on it, it gave way to its squishiness."

# Wozefaries

A new race (see the World Timeline), from Lishies + Buhraies. Several recessive genes mean that, mixed with other species, some features may show while others don't. They can be born with a wide range of hair and eye colors; females tend to have lighter hues and males darker ones. Both men and women are small and youthful-looking. Darfaries consider them lesser beings. Their sweat smells and tastes mildly sweet, a natural, somewhat girly sweetness.

- **Habitat:** mainly Flatland City, but all over the kingdoms when opportunity or curiosity strikes.
- **Food:** omnivores.
- **Intelligence:** similar to humans, but slightly more emotional.
- **Language:** Vraki.
- **Reproduction:** about 7 months' gestation, 1 to 2 babies. Wozefaries + Darfaries = Sorefaries. Wozefaries + Wozefaries = Wozefaries, with a chance of Sorefaries. Wozefaries + human = a hybrid, born human-like but with soul energy capabilities closer to a Wozefaries', including their soul energy senses; whether they have the Darfaries interface shows which way their genes and magic lean.
- **Interface:** the Darfaries Interface.
- **Skills:** the Darfaries veins (see Darfaries), with the same black flame, senses and saliva. They glide longer than Darfaries and can dampen a fall from up to ten stories.

# Sorefaries

A new race (see the World Timeline), from Lishies + Buhraies. Genetically, they're the result of different genes conflicting with each other, and Darfaries consider them an abomination. Several recessive genes mean that, mixed with other species, some features may show while others don't. They can be born with a wide range of hair and eye colors; females tend to have lighter hues and males darker ones. They're small, childlike, and capable of flight. Their sweat smells floral.

What looks like a tattoo isn't one: Sorefaries have Darfaries veins, in the soul itself, like Wozefaries and Darfaries. Like Darfaries, they show a black flame on them when they fly (as in the Vol 1 illustration). They fly using the interface itself as lift, with their feet floating free to help with their center of mass.

- **Habitat:** Flatland City, and all over the kingdoms thanks to adventurers.
- **Food:** omnivores.
- **Intelligence:** like a 5-year-old's. Energetic by nature and highly emotional; they can control some of their emotional outbursts, but not all. Great companions if trained in Flatland City, and their keen hearing makes them great for adventurers and hunters.
- **Language:** Vraki.
- **Reproduction:** sterile, because of a conflict between some recessive genes.
- **Interface:** the Darfaries Interface.
- **Skills:** the Darfaries veins (see Darfaries), with the same flames, senses and saliva. Sorefaries can control their altitude at will.

# Humans (Naked Bruntos)

A new race (see the World Timeline): Homo sapiens.

- **Habitat:** most places across the kingdoms. By far the most numerous species.
- **Food:** omnivores.
- **Intelligence:** the same as in our world.
- **Language:** Vraki.
- **Reproduction:** about 9 months' gestation, 1 to 5 kids per birth.
- **Interface:** the Darfaries Interface.
- **Skills:** none.

# Cruros

See the World Timeline and Cruro. Crocus became their pets. They make weird throat sounds that other Cruros and Crocus seem to understand, mostly to send others to attack or to stop. Cruros aren't citizens of the Four Kingdoms by default, until they join the Half-Cruro guild. Inspired by the [Crurotarsi](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crurotarsi).

- **Habitat:** most live among themselves in Cruro; a few go out exploring.
- **Food:** carnivores.
- **Intelligence:** most are about as smart as a dog and act mostly on instinct. The few intelligent ones communicate through drawings, and some become tribe leaders. A few highly intelligent ones go out to explore and mingle with humans, and understand concepts like money, law and work. Most Cruros aren't deaf: the smarter ones hear Vraki just fine but struggle to enunciate anything, so they scribble it down (a group of Cruros writing in their journals and showing each other, in Vol 2) or use head movements. Some people communicate with them through different noises that stand for different things, depending on how much friendship and history they share. Guardedness is a universal trait.
- **Language:** Vraki, through writing. Otherwise none, though some grunts work among themselves as generic cues (happy, sad).
- **Reproduction:** every year, a female lays a dozen or so eggs.
- **Interface:** the Cruros Interface, inherited from the Crocus (see Fauna › Crocus), but using their hair: it lifts into the air like static and waves as soul fire envelops it, rushing through their body into the target, or through their own body to physically tweak them. They still have the invisibility skill, but most can't hold it for more than a few minutes, unlike the Crocus.
- **Skills:** they detect soul energy as an itchy back.
