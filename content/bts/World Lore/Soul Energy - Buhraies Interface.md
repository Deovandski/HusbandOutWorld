# Buhraies Interface

The Buhraies developed this interface as the blood they drank mutated their bodies, and because they wanted more power. Its logograms are the guardrails: cast without them, and you are altering the soul itself.

Soul energy shapes the spell using logograms drawn from the knowledge shared by the souls of the people nearby.

#### Interface shells

When a spell is cast, five rings appear around the hand holding it. Each shell, on its own, holds a limited number of soul energy tiers:

| Shell | Tiers |
|---|---|
| I (smallest, closest to the hand) | 2 |
| II | 8 |
| III | 18 |
| IV | 32 |
| V (largest, farthest from the hand) | 50 |

Nobody has ever maxed it out to know what would happen. The current theory is that going past the theoretical maximum would make the whole thing explode, possibly in a White Hole event.

#### Casting a spell

In short: choose the spell, put energy into it, and release it. A spell has two parts:

- **Target:** what the spell acts on. With no target, the soul energy falls back on the caster's own idea of how it should apply itself, and a miscast explodes in soul flame in the caster's hand.
- **Verb:** what you want to do. You have to think it clearly; a lack of concentration means a miscast, an explosion, or even blowback.

1. **Draw the spell.** Cut the target logogram into your skin with something sharp (see Language › Buhraies Interface Language), or just cut two empty squares, which has to be done with a knife.

   ![][image1]

2. **Start the spell.** Clench the fingers of the hand holding the spell and the soul energy. The Buhraies shells appear as you do.

   ![][image2]

3. **Infuse soul energy.** The casting hand takes in energy when the caster "eats" with it. Touching the middle and ring fingers lights up the Buhraies veins in that hand with soul flame, and when the other hand "eats", the flames are absorbed. A spell is only stable at a soul energy tier, so energy always fills up to the next tier (after a partial charge, it adds the difference).

   ![][image3]

4. **Apply the energy.** Touch what you want to apply it to with your palm and straighten your fingers, or release the spell as it is for a ranged attack. Cock your arm and time it right for extra range. The caster's body feels the weight of the spell, like firing a gun.

   ![][image4]

# Soul Spectrum

An analysis of the soul. Research with it can find soul harmony, and the Buhraies used it to selectively breed people and enhance their power.

![][image5]

# Soul VERB

Alters the target's soul DNA according to the caster's desires.

![][image6]

**Tiers:** the higher the tier, the more it increases the soul qualities of the summon, with the increase split across several skills. Only tiers above X had a noticeable effect, and only once the soul reconnected to new flesh, if it worked.

# {BodyPart} VERB

Alters the target's body DNA according to the caster's desires.

![][image7]

**Tiers:** the mass affected follows the General Power Effects: 0.1 kg at Tier I, doubling with every tier (51.2 kg at Tier X). Because it changes the genetic information, not the soul, the change stays even after Body Restore, as long as the genetic information isn't lost.
