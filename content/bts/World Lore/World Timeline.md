# Finaies

#### The Evolution Time (unknown to ~10,000 B.G.)

- **The Unknown Period (unknown to ~100,000 B.G.):** what is known about the Finaies today came about in this period: the ability to move around, sexual reproduction, Soul Energy abilities and so on. They lived north of Hifuna, on the planet's northern continent.
- **The Landscaping Period (~100,000 to ~50,000 B.G.):** self-aware and able to move around, they spread south, conquered the entire continent and shaped the land to look like the north.
- **The Revolution Period (~50,000 to ~10,000 B.G.):** as different groups were exposed to different environments on the southern continent, the land they lived on shaped them into distinct specialized groups. Three new races formed from them: the Fuhraies, the Lishies and the Buhraies. They spread quickly and began clashing with one another over territory.

#### The Devolution Time (~10,000 B.G. to today)

- **The Cleansing Period (~10,000 to ~5,215 B.G.):** the Finaies back in the north became aware of a disturbance in the natural order of the world, or at least what they deemed natural. They sent scouts, then armies, but to no avail: their offspring's specialization triumphed over their generalist nature.
- **The Isolation Period (~5,215 to ~3,000 B.G.):**
  - ~5,215 B.G.: given the extent of their losses, they decided to sacrifice themselves to create a barrier between the northern and southern continents, now known as Hifuna. They spent most of their army creating a curtain of radioactive meteors.
  - Later on, they made a pact with the Flingas to watch over the south and plan a comeback.
  - They tried several times to invade by sea, but every attempt was massacred. ~3,150 B.G.: the Great Sea War ended in a massive loss for them, but they at least managed to destroy part of the Buhraies Plains East Dip.
- **The Hifuna Conquer Period (~3,000 to ~2,500 B.G.):**
  - They spent so long planning another comeback that, in the end, they couldn't fix their own mess: Hifuna. They mounted the largest army they could muster, but suffered massive losses crossing it.
  - ~2,650 B.G.: a secret assault went through the desert, but the desert killed off most of the army, and what was left was easily massacred when it reached the Fuhraies. (Legacy says the Buhraies mounted it; see the Q&A.)
- **The Replanning Period (~2,500 B.G. to today):** they've grown so conservative that they're still planning, or rather, watching what happens in the south and discussing it among themselves as if it were a TV show.

# Fuhraies

#### Cold Growth Time (~20,000 to ~5,215 B.G.)

- **Up the Mountain Period (~20,000 to ~10,000 B.G.):** the Finaies near the snowy mountains acclimated to the colder regions at higher altitudes and, over time, moved farther toward the peaks. As they did, their bodies changed: their biological rhythm slowed, and they grew calmer, almost as if they were depressed.
- **Gridlock Period (~10,000 to ~5,215 B.G.):** the Fuhraies now had no trouble fending the Finaies off from their homes, but they were stuck in a deadlock, since they couldn't leave the cold regions anymore: anything above 0 ºC was unbearable for them. ~5,215 B.G.: Hifuna was created, and with it, peace came to the Fuhraies.

#### Cold Defense Time (~5,215 to ~2,100 B.G.)

- **Windless Snow Period (~5,215 to ~3,500 B.G.):** a period of peace, with plentiful resources and unoccupied snowy land as far as the eye could see, since the other two races had plenty of land of their own to grow into.
- **Blue Snow Period (~3,500 to ~3,000 B.G.):**
  - The Buhraies decided to test everything they knew about subjugation on the Fuhraies, but it didn't work, and they fought. Or rather, the Fuhraies kept defending themselves from the Buhraies' weak attacks: being fleshy, the Buhraies weren't used to such cold weather and altitude. Negotiations never worked.
  - ~3,150 B.G.: the Finaies invaded by sea, but the Fuhraies were too far away to be affected, as the Buhraies and Lishies killed every invader.
- **Red Snow Period (~3,000 to ~2,100 B.G.):**
  - Since their battle with the Buhraies was a stalemate, the Lishies began fighting, or rather bullying, the Fuhraies too. That also ended in a stalemate, and negotiations never went anywhere either.
  - Sick of the skirmishes, some Fuhraies left Old Fuhrair for the biggest mountain to the north and founded Fuhrair.
  - ~2,650 B.G.: a secret assault through the desert was mostly wiped out by the desert itself, so the Fuhraies easily defended themselves. (Legacy says the Buhraies mounted it; see the Q&A.)
  - The endless skirmishes slowed and lost their intensity. The Fuhraies assumed they had finally won.

#### Cold Fall Time (~2,100 to ~500 B.G.)

- **The Calm Period (~2,100 to ~600 B.G.):** the skirmishes between the Fuhraies and the Buhraies and Lishies finally ended, ushering in a period of calm. They shored up their defenses as much as they could, but over time their battle fangs dulled.
- **The Naked Bruntos Period (~600 to ~500 B.G.):** seeing the rage of the Naked Bruntos (humans), and how quickly the other two races were falling, the rest of their civilization fled into Fuhrair and once again beefed up their defenses as much as they could, mostly to hide from the Naked Bruntos.
- **The Pact (~500 B.G.):** seeing how voracious the humans' attacks were, the Fuhraies agreed to a pact with the Buhraies, and from then on their story is intertwined with the Buhraies' (see The Last Breath Time under Buhraies).

# Lishies

#### Toughening Up Time (~15,000 to ~4,500 B.G.)

- **Muscle Development Period (~15,000 to ~10,000 B.G.):** the Finaies in the deep forest developed a taste for meat, and their bodies changed slightly, then a lot, as they became excellent hunters. The females preferred beefy, fleshy men, so they steered evolution toward losing the Finaies interface altogether. The Lishies became a new species.
- **Eat the Land Period (~10,000 to ~4,500 B.G.):**
  - They developed a tribal, territorial nature, which forced them to spread out quickly to avoid too much internal conflict. In doing so, they clashed with the Buhraies so often that they got to know the Buhraies' blood Soul Energy and found ways to fight it.
  - ~6,000 to ~5,000 B.G.: the Buhraies created their plains and separated themselves. The Lishies kept trying to reach them for payback, but without a Soul Energy interface, they couldn't.
  - ~5,215 B.G.: the Finaies retreated.
  - The Lishies spread through the forest, especially toward the southwest, where food was abundant. Where there was enough food and strong tribal leadership, their population grew denser and even tamer.

#### Clashing Time (~4,500 B.G. to ~0 A.G.)

- **Resisting Subjugation Period (~4,500 to ~3,500 B.G.):**
  - The Lishies clashed with the Buhraies again and discovered how to use Buhraies bodies as a Soul Energy interface to fly up onto the Buhraies Plains, since the plains were far too high to climb, and carving a path always led to a skirmish that took too long.
  - They kept up constant skirmishes and raids to collect more bodies for an all-out war. ~3,575 B.G.: the Lishies launched a war against the Buhraies, and both sides were decimated as the battle raged on.
- **Overtaking Subjugation Period (~3,500 to ~2,100 B.G.):**
  - The Lishies realized the Buhraies were trying to subjugate the Fuhraies, so they tried to kill the Fuhraies off first to prevent it. They seemed stuck in a fruitless battle when they suddenly realized they had lost Endlessfog Island.
  - ~3,150 B.G.: the Finaies launched a massive strike from the sea, but the Lishies were less affected than the Buhraies, since most Lishies lived down south.
  - ~2,650 B.G.: the Buhraies were terraforming Endlessfog right under their noses while the Lishies were busy preparing to fight off the Finaies, in case they were a powerful menace this time.
  - Not as dumb as the Buhraies thought, the Lishies began playing 4D chess: they faked battles with the Buhraies while planning to take over the island.
  - ~2,100 B.G.: the Lishies found a way to take the island back and began eating the Bruntos as a peculiar food source. But the fog meant they couldn't eat them all, so once they had had their fill, the island was mostly abandoned.
- **Interfighting Period (~2,100 to ~1,100 B.G.):**
  - With the Buhraies and Fuhraies on the defensive, the Lishies fought for power among themselves, to the point where several wars broke out between tribes.
  - Some tried to figure out why the Buhraies were on the defensive, but found nothing useful. Some Lishies even began to forget the Buhraies and Fuhraies existed.
  - ~1,650 B.G.: Naked Bruntos started to appear, but the Lishies treated them like any other animal: something to hunt for dinner. They tasted great, too, so the Lishies began herding humans and fattening them up like cattle. Unaware of how intelligent humans were, the Lishies let them grow and prosper under their noses, as often as the humans planned their escape.
- **Wild Bruntos Extermination Period (~1,100 to ~900 B.G.):** several Lishin tribes set out to eat every wild Brunto in sight, as they realized how many of these "animals" were roaming around. It seemed too late to find every single one, but they were making steady progress, until humans fought back in ways other than physical…
- **The Path of No Return Period (~900 to ~500 B.G.):**
  - ~855 B.G.: Lishin and other tribes attacked the Buhraies, thinking it was the perfect time, but that was false information from the humans.
  - ~805 B.G.: a massive civil war broke out in Aldemeri, which burned for days, all instigated by humans. Some residents less inclined toward war fled to other cities (or to Flatland) and tried to stop another civil war. Several Lishies fled into what is now Flatland and made a pact with the Buhraies after a battle that thinned both sides.
  - The Lishies were steadily losing ground to the Naked Bruntos' invasion south and east of the Buhraies Plains.
  - ~525 B.G.: a massive war broke out between Ganyrami and Lishin, also provoked by humans after they failed to start another civil war. Lishin fell, and humans picked over the remains.
- **Fall From Grace Period (~500 B.G. to ~0 A.G.):** humans worked their way through countless tribes until Ganyrami, the biggest city of the ancient species, was the species' last stronghold. Amid chaos, infighting and disorganization, humans killed off the entire city: the historic Fall of Ganyrami. The Lishin blood lives on in The Last Breath Time (under Buhraies), and some Lishies might have survived in the southwestern woodland.

# Buhraies

#### Rise of the Flesh Time (~15,000 to ~5,000 B.G.)

- **Blood Feast Period (~15,000 to ~10,000 B.G.):** the Finaies near what is now Tolerideme began feasting on the blood and bone marrow of fauna and beasts. The variety of blood triggered a mutation in their bodies: the birth of the Buhraies.
- **Blood & Soul Energy Period (~10,000 to ~6,000 B.G.):** the Buhraies developed their own blood interface. Their hunger for more power led them to clash with the Finaies to the north and the Lishies to the south at the same time. The constant battling made them flesh out their abilities further, while the Lishies grew resistant to them, ending in a stalemate.
- **Landscaping Period (~6,000 to ~5,000 B.G.):**
  - With the Lishies' overwhelming numbers looming, the Buhraies raised a large section of the plains off the ground, grinding Finaies bodies into the earth, to create a safe zone from the Lishies: the Buhraies Plains, a cliff about 2 km high on average that doesn't erode.
  - Fost stones formed during the process, from Soul Energy interfering deep within the land. The Buhraies used them as a light source; the Lishies eventually learned about them, but never used them widely, since fire pits were easier.
  - More earth came from where the Lukewarm Buhraies Lake is now. They built an underground structure that pipes its water to Wingarest.
  - ~5,215 B.G.: the Finaies, having failed to bring down the Buhraies Plains, retreated and created Hifuna.

#### Blood Research Time (~5,000 to ~2,100 B.G.)

- **First Subjugation Period (~5,000 to ~4,500 B.G.):** with the Finaies out of the picture and the Lishies out of reach, their society flourished. Having modified Wingas into Big Wingas and Yabuca trees into Wood People and bent them to their will, they researched how to subjugate the other two races. Wingarest became the home of the Big Wingas. The Wood People were created by altering the souls of Yabuca trees, then confined to small forests across the Buhraies Plains, where they produced food and lumber in a controlled way. Their goal: subjugate and experiment on the Finaies in the north, and fix and terraform Hifuna.
- **Lishies Subjugation Period (~4,500 to ~3,500 B.G.):** they tried to subjugate the Lishies, but not only did it fail, it led the Lishies to discover how to get up onto the Buhraies Plains. The Lishies were hard to fight there, and the peaceful life on the plains was over: the Buhraies researched how to keep the Lishies at bay while skirmishing nearly every week. ~3,575 B.G.: the Lishies launched a war against the Buhraies, and both sides were decimated as the battle raged on.
- **Fuhraies Subjugation Period (~3,500 to ~3,000 B.G.):**
  - Frustrated that their subjugation efforts had gone nowhere, the Buhraies began targeting the Fuhraies, but that only led to fighting. The Lishies attacked as well, since they didn't know what power the Buhraies were after to turn the tide. (See the Q&A.)
  - ~3,150 B.G.: the Finaies launched their biggest strike from the sea. The Buhraies Plains East Dip lost the most land. It was repaired, but by a different generation from the one that built it, so their ratios and practices didn't match the original ones.
- **Fake Battle Period (~3,000 to ~2,100 B.G.):**
  - Realizing the Lishies were focused on the Fuhraies, the Buhraies kept faking an endless skirmish with the Fuhraies to keep the Lishies away from their true plot: terraforming what is now Endlessfog and experimenting with summoning species. Bruntos came about then.
  - ~2,650 B.G.: the Buhraies didn't care about the failed Finaies invasion of the Fuhraies.
  - ~2,450 B.G.: the Buhraies discovered the Walking Heart stones, irradiated rocks found in Hifuna.
  - ~2,100 B.G.: they finally lost the island to the Lishies, who had figured out how to deal with the fog, as the Buhraies were no longer as focused on their research there.

#### Naked Bruntos Time (~2,100 to ~855 B.G.)

- **Bloodsport Period (~2,100 to ~1,500 B.G.):**
  - Licking their wounds (mostly emotional) after losing Endlessfog Island to Lishies they thought too dumb to plan anything, they stopped all battles and focused on research.
  - That's when the Naked Bruntos (humans) came about. While testing their war skills, the Buhraies discovered a hunting sport, and especially how tasty human blood was when the humans were frightened and running for their lives. Some also found it tasty when Naked Bruntos were extremely lustful for their tiny beauty. This led to tons of research, and to mixing Buhraies blood into humans.
  - In this period, they discovered the power of souls and Walking Heart stones and created an endless army with them. One of the humans brought back to life was really good at mineralogy, so his knowledge was extracted, and with it, English names for minerals stuck in the Vraki language: gold, iron, magnesium and so on.
  - They noticed humans spreading like rats, but didn't care: at least the researchers who mattered truly believed humans were powerless.
  - ~1,650 B.G.: Naked Bruntos made the Buhraies Plains East Dip crumble, and several Wood People fell and slowly began to spread; some became unchained. The Buhraies didn't care: it meant more trouble for the Lishies, and a chance to see how things would work themselves out, since Naked Bruntos couldn't be smart or powerful enough to do any harm in return.
- **Bahyum Castle Period (~1,500 to ~855 B.G.):**
  - They built their biggest research station ever, Bahyum, and tried to create an automated army factory there, but struggled to control it: it always had a life of its own.
  - ~855 B.G.: a coordinated attack from the Lishies and the Naked Bruntos, plus a sudden turn for the worse inside Bahyum Castle (sabotage), put the Buhraies in a pinch. Too stubborn to fight together, they began losing ground to the Naked Bruntos and the Lishies.
  - ~855 B.G.: the Buhraies activated their last defense by unchaining the Wood People (removing the Fost stone from the top of each one), but the Wood People mindlessly attacked everyone and everything. Over the years they multiplied rapidly, killing countless humans as they spread, until humans figured out how to use Fost stones to their advantage and built villages surrounded by Wood People.
  - The Buhraies realized the Wood People and humans had basically taken over the land. They used the last of their strength to cast a spell at the moon itself to make all living things visible, since humans hid, bundled together, at night, but the spell wasn't strong enough and failed.
  - Completely drained of Soul Energy, the Buhraies gave up and fled west, to where Flatland is now. Some Lishies came to the same place, and a war between them left both sides with measly numbers, until a ceasefire. With so few left, the Buhraies made a cooperation pact with the Lishies: they would suck the Lishies' blood to mount an assault to retake their plains. It never worked, since they were always on the defensive for lack of numbers.

#### The Last Breath Time (~855 B.G. to ~380 A.G.)

- **New Home Period (~855 to ~500 B.G.):**
  - The Buhraies found themselves in what is now Flatland. Resources were scarce, so the Lishies didn't care for it, but more Lishies arrived as humans toppled their cities, especially after the fall of Aldemeri (~805 B.G.). They interbred, and the first Darfaries, Wozefaries and Sorefaries were born.
  - ~500 B.G.: the remnants of the Buhraies and Lishies made a pact with the Fuhraies. The Naked Bruntos' attacks on what is now Flatland kept growing exponentially. The humans couldn't take the land, but the Buhraies drained most of their Soul Energy, and many Lishies died from having all their blood sucked out just to keep fending off the humans. Mortified by the humans' strength, the Fuhraies didn't think twice about going as far west as they could when the Buhraies proposed building a large mountain.
- **Mountain Pact Period (~500 B.G. to ~250 A.G.):**
  - The mountain pact was made, along with a new gesture to go with it, the Mountain Greeting: hands up, touching noses (if you can), staring into each other's eyes, and shaking bellies together.
  - ~495 B.G.: they all fled into the desert to build a mountain to protect themselves from what they called a plague of Naked Bruntos.
  - The remnants of the Buhraies, Lishies and Fuhraies now shared a single mountain and interbred, so more and more Darfaries, Wozefaries and Sorefaries were born, and Vraki became the official language. (The Fuhraies couldn't interbreed with the others, since all the other species are fleshy.) The Buhraies developed the Darfaries interface in this period, trying to control the Darfaries as foot soldiers.
  - ~97 A.G.: they fended off an assault from the Naked Bruntos.
- **Expulsion Period (~250 to ~270 A.G.):** ~250 A.G.: the remaining Buhraies attempted a coup, but it failed. The Lishies tried too, but there were so few of them that the Darfaries easily defended themselves. The Darfaries wiped out every other species on the mountain. Some fled farther west, but all likely met their end.
- **Sundeath Period (~270 to ~380 A.G.):** a group of ancient species gathered and created a second mountain, now known as Sundeath. They summoned Naked Bruntos again, but the humans soon rebelled and, with the help of other humans who taught them Vraki and about the world, wiped out the ancient species.

# Darfaries

#### Transformation Time (~200 B.G. to ~270 A.G.)

- **Lishies & Buhraies Shift Period (~805 B.G. to ~0 A.G.):** the children of these species began to crossbreed, but they couldn't breed back with either parent species: these mixed children could only breed with one another, which sped up the rapid growth of the three new species: the Darfaries, the Wozefaries and the Sorefaries.
- **Population Expansion Period (~0 to ~250 A.G.):** the Darfaries and Wozefaries kept growing until they were the largest populations on the mountain. Conflicts between species, especially between the new and the old, rose for lack of space.
- **Mountain Overtake Period (~250 to ~270 A.G.):** the Darfaries killed everyone, their history included. But Wozefaries and Sorefaries kept being born: their blood was tainted forever, and some Darfaries grew uneasy about killing their own babies for the sake of purity. That's when the first king, Gandew, established the Goodbye Cliff, from which every Wozefaries and Sorefaries was sent flying toward Flatland. Anyone who didn't accept it had to leave.

#### Gandew Time (~270 A.G. to today)

- **Isolated Period (~270 to ~570 A.G.):** Gandew kept out of human affairs while making use of the Goodbye Cliff. Flatland City came to be in this period. ~375 A.G.: the Gandew Common Duel Rights & Law was established, and it evolved over the years.
- **Greedy Humans Period (~570 to ~680 A.G.):** Tolerideme, winning its war against Sanbi, marched on Gandew in 570 A.G., and a great war between the three kingdoms began. The Darfaries were now as flexible as humans in warfare, so it wasn't easy, and war after war mostly ended in stalemate (see The Greed Period under Humans).
- **The Prosperity Treaty Period (~680 A.G. to today):** the Darfaries, Wozefaries and Sorefaries became equal to humans under the law, but kept some traditions, which was allowed, since each kingdom could have its own laws as long as they obeyed the Prosperity Treaty (see The Prosperity Treaty Period under Humans).

# Wozefaries & Sorefaries

#### Transformation Time (~200 B.G. to ~270 A.G.)

The same as the Darfaries': born from the crossbreeding of Lishies and Buhraies on the mountain, then sent flying off the Goodbye Cliff toward Flatland once the Darfaries took over (see Transformation Time under Darfaries).

#### Prosperity Time (~270 A.G. to today)

- **Human Period (~270 to ~570 A.G.):**
  - A group of bandits venturing near the desert mountain hole found Wozefaries and Sorefaries dying from falls they couldn't control, predator attacks or starvation. The bandit leader at the time decided to take care of them, to increase his number of Soul Energy–capable people: a move way ahead of his time, since most people would have killed anything that looked like an ancient species.
  - With a constant stream of new babies, and the older ones growing up, the bandit leader became the leader of the biggest village in the oasis: Flatland. He poured all his resources into building the village, hoping to use the Wozefaries and Sorefaries as slaves in his mines. Wozefaries and Sorefaries slowly spread through the kingdoms, though many people were racist toward them and most were slaves. Some began changing public opinion by using their abilities and Soul Energy to help.
  - As the bandit leader grew old, his children fought for power, along with some old gang members who wanted more for their work. A gang war broke out until most of them were dead. It ended when the remaining members split the riches among themselves and disbanded the bandit gang, by then the size of a militia. Some stayed behind for a more normal life; others traveled far and wide in search of fortune.
  - Over time, Flatland grew into the center of the oasis, with many economic activities, such as training Sorefaries as hunting companions.
- **Greedy Humans Period (~570 to ~680 A.G.):** many Wozefaries and Sorefaries spread throughout the kingdoms. Some knew how to grease the wheels to get a good life; others ended up as slaves (see The Greed Period under Humans).
- **The Prosperity Treaty Period (680 A.G. to today):** the Darfaries, Wozefaries and Sorefaries became equal to humans under the law (see The Prosperity Treaty Period under Humans).

# Humans

#### Before Ganyrami Time (~2,100 B.G. to 0 A.G.)

- **Summoning Period (~2,100 to ~1,900 B.G.):**
  - The Buhraies brought several humans to the islands of the Buhraies Plains and taught them Vraki and other research-related skills. They stayed on the middle islands, along with other scientific studies. Buhraies blood was mixed into human blood, letting humans cast spells, some more than others.
  - Most were hunted, and some disappeared, never to be seen again. Some fell through the cracks and lived hidden in the forests, away from the Buhraies' fangs, until they could escape. They camouflaged themselves with tree leaves and dirt, and stole food from the Buhraies' homes as they spread like rats. The Buhraies knew about it, but did nothing: they were more curious than anything.
- **Tribal Period (~1,900 to ~1,500 B.G.):**
  - Wingas became their friends and let humans communicate with each other, while Big Wingas were usually dangerous and territorial.
  - They looked for ways off the Buhraies Plains and eventually found the Buhraies Plains East Dip, which was growing unstable. ~1,650 B.G.: they provoked a massive landslide and used it to escape in droves.
  - Tribes formed around what they still remembered of themselves on the Buhraies Plains, and in the lower plains too, as villages spread. The Lishies began hunting humans.
- **Lishin Cattle Period (~1,500 to ~1,000 B.G.):** the Lishies bred and fattened humans by the thousands for food, and with so many humans, some were always escaping. The human population exploded all over the lowlands. A small group escaped south and founded what is now Endlessfog and Triangle City.
- **Lishin Food Period (~1,000 to ~900 B.G.):**
  - The Lishies hunted and ate humans so voraciously that humans felt they were going extinct. They reached for anything that could turn the tide, and in 939 B.G. they found it: Walking Heart stones, discovered by stealing information from the Buhraies (see the book Strong Walking Heart in [Books of Vraki](/lore/in-world-books/)).
  - Once a person's heart is replaced with a stone, they can only feel the emotion set in it. Most who tried it died, going berserk or taking their own lives, among other reasons. The few who could live with a stone became legends. The stone also seeps into the person's soul, corrupting it and causing many symptoms, including being impossible to revive.
- **The Hazing of Aldemeri Period (~900 to ~805 B.G.):** humans struck back at the Lishies by pitting them against one another. They managed to provoke a war between Lishin and the Buhraies, all while killing Buhraies and sabotaging Bahyum Castle. With the power of the stones, humans worked directly, and indirectly from the shadows, to bring Aldemeri down. ~855 B.G.: the Buhraies unchained the Wood People, which wiped out most humans on the plains, but didn't slow human activity in the southern plains. ~805 B.G.: Aldemeri fell.
- **The Hazing of Lishin Period (~805 to ~525 B.G.):** with Aldemeri out of the picture, humans focused on Lishin, the hub for attacking any other civilization. They failed to trigger another civil war, but managed to start an all-out war between Ganyrami and Lishin, then took over the remnants with a war of their own. ~525 B.G.: Lishin fell.
- **The Hazing of Ganyrami Period (~525 to 0 B.G.):**
  - ~495 B.G.: humans took over Flatland as the ancient species fled into the desert.
  - ~438 B.G.: Nari stones were discovered, helping the fight as a Soul Energy replacement.
  - ~338 B.G.: nari gels were discovered, though mostly used to poison enemies rather than to heal.
  - ~0 B.G.: Ganyrami fell. The lower plains and the Buhraies Plains were now under human control.

#### After Ganyrami Time (0 to 939 A.G.)

- **The Indulgence Period (0 to 120 A.G.):**
  - With Ganyrami fallen, and the desert treacherous and offering few resources, humans slowed their attacks and shifted toward daily life. For a brief time, humans were so spread out that conflicts were rare, and the use of Walking Heart stones faded as times grew peaceful.
  - Some human settlements grew into villages, then cities. As the population grew, bandits showed up, so the Sanbi and Tolerideme Kingdoms formed around the mighty adventurers who protected them (not the same as Forway adventurers); at this point, both were cities rather than villages. Roads began to connect these places, including one of the first roads up the Buhraies Plains South Dip into Tolerideme.
  - Rumors of ancient species look-alikes began to float around. At the time, the Gandew Kingdom killed any human who wandered too close to the mountain. ~97 A.G.: humans mounted an assault, but the mountain was so well protected that they gave up.
  - The kingdoms began competing with each other.
- **The Greed Period (120 to 680 A.G.):**
  - The sons and daughters of those adventurers were born into lives of luxury and serenity, so they grew greedy for even more, and killing over it was no big deal. Wars between the two kingdoms began; Walking Heart stones came back into use, especially in the armies, and there was carnage everywhere.
  - ~150 A.G.: Whiteland was discovered.
  - 270 to 380 A.G.: humans helped free Sundeath from the ancient species, and it became a refuge from the skirmishes between the human kingdoms. Wozefaries and Sorefaries slowly spread through the kingdoms, though many people were racist toward them and most were slaves; some began changing public opinion by using their abilities and Soul Energy to help.
  - ~420 A.G.: Newland was discovered.
  - ~470 A.G.: a past Sanbi King decided to build a city at the Waterfall, then a small village, as a war measure against Tolerideme: flooding them, which somewhat worked.
  - ~490 A.G.: Old Fuhrair became a mining town.
  - 570 A.G.: the Kingdom of Tolerideme was winning its war against Sanbi, so it marched on Gandew, and a great war between the three kingdoms began. Flatland became a resting spot for soldiers, especially for exchanging information. The Darfaries were now as flexible as humans in warfare, so it wasn't easy; wars kept coming one after another, mostly ending in stalemates.
  - ~570 A.G.: Endlessfog and Triangle City grew with all the people fleeing the wars, and so did what is now the Freeish Kingdom. Blood City grew out of a village full of hunters, and Deltacross grew from the constant flow of people traveling toward Freeish.
  - 572 A.G.: Tolerideme blew up the road up the Buhraies Plains South Dip after losing too many men in the war against Sanbi.
  - 575 A.G.: on a dark, cloudy night, a few people saw someone in robe-like clothing walking drunkenly around Tastyleaf, and a guard saw them head toward the swamp. The guard warned it was dangerous, but the person replied, "I have a literal awesome fucking idea," and kept walking. The Cruros showed up soon after.
  - 640 A.G.: an incredibly smart man named Freeish Paulin felt compelled to change things, so he began building a secret base in what is now Forway City to overthrow the kingdoms.
  - 648 A.G.: Paulin was betrayed and sold to the Gandew Kingdom to be assassinated. But as the King of Gandew learned what Paulin was trying to do, he came to agree with most of it, overthrowing the current kingdoms included, mainly because he realized that otherwise everyone would end up dead and nothing would be solved. He still had to have Paulin killed to save face with the people, but Paulin had given him all his connections, so the king began building a new base in what is now the Freeish Kingdom. He entrusted one of his closest allies (and mistresses) with leading the organization, and made her Courtroom Scientist under the guise of "better war preparations".
  - 650 A.G.: the courtroom scientists of Gandew (Asa the Powerful), Tolerideme (Adrian from North Tolerideme) and Sanbi (Bijura Moacir) met at the hidden base to plan the overthrow. They slowly conditioned the people to accept their ideas and stirred up revolts ("the greed of the heroes was controlling the people"), until the kingdoms lost their grip on their people and could no longer wage war on each other. Then the rebellion truly began, and it toppled all three kingdoms. The King of Gandew was executed in front of everyone, calm and relieved despite it all (some people don't believe he was that kind and selfless). The King of Tolerideme was starved to death. The King of Sanbi was publicly spanked in humiliation, then dropped alive into the ocean.
- **The Prosperity Treaty Period (680 A.G. to today):**
  - The plan succeeded, and the courtroom scientists became the new kings. They wrote the Prosperity Treaty and Forway law so that no more wars over greed would break out.
  - They created a common currency, formalized a calendar starting from the year Ganyrami fell (Adrian helped drive both the calendar and the units of measurement), and built other tools to put everyone on the same page and make trade easier.
  - Embassies were founded soon after, and all ruins and major cities became Forway property. Sundeath tried to stay out of it all, but gave in under the sheer pressure from Forway; in exchange, it has Pharaohs as city leaders.
