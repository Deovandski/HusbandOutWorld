# Harry Sanbi

In his previous life, Harry was Desmond. In 978 A.G., in another universe, Desmond died of a heart attack after drinking too much coffee, eating too many tacos and working long hours. In 1039 A.G., his decaying soul was bound back to flesh in this universe, and he woke up with no idea where he was…

Publicly, people know he came out of one of the castle towers after the "silent" explosion, but nobody saw him being brought into the castle. He's the "missing piece" for understanding what [Gabiro](#gabiro-sanbi-royal) was up to. The official story is that his worth lies in being the Sanbi Eye, and Sanbi blamed Gabiro for the soul mess, but rumors persist.

- **Race:** mostly human, with some Bagarisa.
- **Birthday:** 1039 A.G. Paulin 14th (he counts it as his birthday, since he doesn't know his real one).
- **Attracted to:** Wozefaries girls: kawaii, dependent.
- **Myers-Briggs:** ENFJ.
- **Intelligence:** normal, but he lacks street smarts and any common knowledge of the new world he woke up in. Not very detail-oriented; somewhat innocent, and confused at first. Easy to take advantage of.
- **Habits:** furrows his brow; scratches his beard or belly; joins conversations and asks questions. He doesn't depend on Nari stones as much, thanks to his high recharge rate, but he can't use the Darfaries interface. In Vol 4 he shows, for the first time, an interface of his own that turns Soul Energy into fire from the tips of his middle and ring fingers.
- **Speech:** lots of "could", "would", "maybe", "hmm" and "ahh"; tries not to be pushy; forgets names, so he tends not to use them.
- **Strengths:** adaptable; amicable; caring; curious; funny; sincere; ecchi, and easily impressed by beauty.
- **Flaws:** dependent; insecure; claustrophobic; cowardly, especially around things he has never seen before, but he can be egged on to change his mind.
- **Likes and dislikes:** he enjoys the food, especially as he develops a taste for this new world's dishes. Loves experimenting in the kitchen and discovering new things. Afraid of danger.
- **Interface:** Bagarisa. He can't use the Darfaries interface, since he has no Finaies or Buhraies blood.

# Magi Blood Blue-Flower-Red-Bone

Magi grew up with loving parents and would likely have become a historian, but the sudden disappearance of her father, [Fernan](#fernan-blood-blue-flower-red-bone), forced her to become an adventurer to pay off guild debts. She wants to find him, or at least his bow. She inherited her father's hunting talent, but she has narcolepsy and is rather distrustful of people. She always carries hunting gear in her bag, especially traps and camouflage for hiding.

- **Race:** human, with Wozefaries (a higher percentage than usual).
- **Birthday:** 1020 A.G. Asa 3rd.
- **Attracted to:** cute, dependable, handsome men taller than her, slender with some muscle.
- **Myers-Briggs:** INTJ.
- **Intelligence:** above average; she knows a lot about history, fauna and flora. She strives to be independent, after all.
- **Habits:** avoids physical contact and keeps space between herself and others; looks coldly into people's eyes as she tries to hide her emotions; knows how to defend herself; cares about people.
- **Speech:** short and sweet. She strips the emotions out and leaves the cold, hard facts; doesn't use interjections, and says "you", "he" and "she" far more than "I". She pries for information and listens; her questions are like arrows into a Bagarisa's heart. Her "hmm, hmm" murmurs can go either way.
- **Strengths:** independent; loyal; clever; educated; skillful; cares about people, and believes that once someone is punished fairly, things are settled; knows what she wants and stays focused on her goals; secretive (which also has a bad side, since it makes her cold).
- **Flaws:** distrustful, putting up a barrier with people she doesn't know; immature, especially emotionally; secretive; cold, for the same reasons; hangry, especially after a bad night's sleep.
- **Narcolepsy (type 2):**
  - Excessive daytime sleepiness: sleep attacks when she isn't actively hunting or forcing herself to focus on something; otherwise, an average sleepiness that comes and goes depending on the day and whether she napped.
  - Automatic behaviors: she sometimes falls asleep while eating without even realizing it.
  - Hallucinations and vivid dreams when waking up or falling asleep.
- **Likes and dislikes:** likes fruits and vegetables in general; loves Domar baking and Sanbi fish; not as fond of meat. Likes pink, and feeling cozy and protected under blankets. Hates the cold, and people who pry into her life.
- **Interface:** Darfaries.

# Fernan Blood Blue-Flower-Red-Bone

Born and raised in Freeish, in the Blood group, Fernan learned to hunt from a young age, and the mix of talent and early practice made him one of the most powerful bow hunters around. He was an Aquamarine adventurer when he met his future wife, [Akemi](#akemi-blood-blue-flower-red-bone) (her entry has more of their story), and later became a Sapphire adventurer.

He died on an expedition, killed by his Black Sapphire colleagues on Fuhrair: he had found a torture prison where a handful of Fuhraies had been kept for what felt like an eternity, while a group of Black Sapphires tried to figure out how to prolong life. Fernan couldn't accept what they were doing to them, so they killed him and stashed his body. Officially, he died on a dangerous mission in Hifuna: someone of similar build wore his armor and carried his weapon to Tastyleaf to hide the trail, which made searching for him far more dangerous. His wife lives in Blood City with Magi's little brother, [Jonem](#jonem-blood-blue-flower-red-bone).

- **Race:** human.
- **Birthday:** 994 A.G. Adrian 6th.
- **Attracted to:** Wozefaries with long hair.
- **Myers-Briggs:** ESTP.
- **Intelligence:** street smarts, but he couldn't fully see when people were plotting against him, since he tended to trust people. Not much knowledge beyond that.
- **Habits:** relaxed posture, friendly in general; not touchy, but not distant either. Looks people in the eye with sincerity.
- **Speech:** talkative; he liked to keep the conversation going and make others feel better, turned things into action items, and made sure others were okay.
- **Strengths:** adventurous; caring; charming; compassionate; genuine; romantic; warm; trusting; brave; selfless.
- **Flaws:** disobedient at times, especially when he thought someone's plan needed changing on the fly; nosy; dependent; pessimistic.
- **Likes and dislikes:** loved jerky, and tiny girls. Loved tasting new dishes and drinks, and being at the center of a crowd, talking with people and building relationships.
- **Interface:** none; he couldn't use the Darfaries interface, since he had no Finaies or Buhraies blood.

# Hiromi High-Gandew Tough-Hammer

Born and raised in the Gandew Kingdom as the First Prince of [Nooru](#nooru-high-gandew-tough-hammer) and [Satomi](#satomi-high-gandew-tough-hammer). He read books and asked his mother about the world, but she brushed him off, busy with her blacksmithing. So he went back to the books, and the more he read, the more he wanted to see things with his own eyes. People from other places told him stories that captivated him to no end, and he decided to go exploring; his mother tried to stop him, but it was too late. Like his father, he had an adventurous spirit, and people rarely recognized him as a First Prince. He made many wonderful memories exploring, and loved telling other adventurers about them in taverns.

On 1038 A.G. Paulin 98th (Sunnulow), after many adventures, he got way too drunk at The Dead Wozen bar and decided to ask a man about his awesome, voluptuous hair. He put a drunken hand on the man's shoulder, congratulated him and asked for tips. That "man" was [Yacamim](#yacamim-sanbi-royal), who was pissed beyond belief when he blurted out that he'd thought she was a man, and immediately tried to punch him. He ducked with his amazing reflexes, which set off a massive bar brawl. Hiromi won fair and square, and as he went to pick her up, they fell in love with each other's awesomeness. He'll become the next King of Sanbi, since Yacamim will be queen, though neither of them wants it.

- **Race:** Darfaries.
- **Birthday:** 994 A.G. Asa 44th.
- **Attracted to:** strong women in general.
- **Myers-Briggs:** ESFP.
- **Intelligence:** focused on adventuring, but he knows a bit more about the world than most. Doesn't care for politics.
- **Habits:** handsy; friendly; threatening in looks, but not in action.
- **Speech:** speaks in the first person, and his stories always revolve around himself as the main character; a deep, somewhat growly voice.
- **Strengths:** adventurous; empathetic; jovial; dynamic; relaxed and friendly with strangers; a teacher, especially about Darfaries and Gandew things; protective of what he holds dear.
- **Flaws:** flies off the handle when what he holds dear is disrespected; opinionated; slightly disobedient; adventurous; rambly.
- **Likes and dislikes:** exploring; a good listener; alcohol; Yacamim, despite her rudeness. Dislikes people who get in his way or mess with what he holds dear.
- **Interface:** Darfaries.

# Morika Tolerideme

Born and raised in the Tolerideme Kingdom, to a lower-class family. He always had trouble fattening up, so he became the butt of jokes, even though by other standards he's very handsome. He fled Tolerideme and everyone around him, and stumbled through the world until he ended up in Sweetlake. Much of what he tried never quite worked out because of his personality, and since he couldn't form long-term friendships, people lost trust in him over time.

He once wore a Sweet Sting costume to gather Black Honey, and it felt so empowering that he saved for about a year and a half to have a unique, chitin-looking armor made, which makes up for his slow reflexes and hides his face. People are trying to figure out who this crazy warrior is, and nobody knows it's Morika: in the armor, he becomes another persona, [The Stinger](#the-stinger).

He's very subservient to [Neferteri](#neferteri-sanbi-royal) and models himself on her: he keeps no logbook, holding his notes and reports in his head, rehearsed to give the Queen just the right amount of information without wasting her time. He doesn't play mind games and isn't the experimental type: when he acts, he knows the outcome from practice. Very autistic, he's stuck doing things a certain way (killing with the spike of his Stinger helmet, for example). Even in disguise, he gives himself away by how instantly ready to fight and how aware of his surroundings he is.

- **Race:** human.
- **Birthday:** 1014 A.G. Asa 24th.
- **Attracted to:** a decisive woman around his age, as tall as him or taller, but not a Darfaries.
- **Myers-Briggs:** ESTP in the Stinger armor; ISTJ without it.
- **Intelligence:** some street smarts; no formal education. A plan follower.
- **In the Stinger armor:**
  - **Habits:** aggressive, imposing posture; eye contact (through the armor).
  - **Speech:** confrontational and provocative; straight to the point.
  - **Strengths:** ambitious; brave; capable (sometimes less so); powerful; quick-witted, takes the lead; dogmatic; spontaneous, though a bit too much when his energy runs high; serious.
  - **Flaws:** dull; tense; boring; blunt; crazy (rarely).
- **Without the armor:**
  - **Habits:** avoidant; quiet.
  - **Speech:** avoidant; beats around the bush, if he speaks at all. Very respectful, to the point of sounding robotic; serious, like a leader. Never cracks a joke, and doesn't take charge of the conversation.
  - **Strengths:** serious; reserved; meticulous; shy; tidy; dogmatic; orderly.
  - **Flaws:** mute (completely); dull; tense; boring; slight OCD issues; jealous; avoids fights.
- **Likes and dislikes:** fish and light-tasting food; not really fond of jerky; loves bread. Cleans his armor after taking it off.
- **Interface:** Darfaries.

# Takeo Forway Tea-Drinkers

[Francisco](#francisco-forway-tea-drinkers)'s only son. Passersby take him for a Darfaries after one glance at his height and thick, protruding teeth, but Takeo is technically human, since he lacks the Darfaries veins, and he's conflicted about whether that's true. He's known for being too reserved and polite with everyone, often going as far as leaving parties over any small conflict rather than hashing it out. At the same time, he seems far too eager to use his finger clipper to extract information from anyone he deems suspicious. Some adventurers suspect he eats more Hifuna Yabucas than he should.

- **Race:** human, though he looks like a Darfaries.
- **Birthday:** 969 A.G. Asa 23rd.
- **Attracted to:** maybe a human girl as big as him; he wants to find someone in a situation like his.
- **Myers-Briggs:** ISFP.
- **Intelligence:** not the most intelligent, or rather logical, so he trusts his gut a bit too much. That's why he believes eating Hifuna Yabuca can make him more powerful, even though he knows its effects.
- **Habits:** prone to violence when it comes to holding someone until they spill everything they know.
- **Speech:** straightforward but polite ("We finished the quest. We celebrated. We all spoke our minds. Now it's time for me to say goodbye."), unless he suspects you. Never curses.
- **Strengths:** likes to get to the bottom of everything, leaving no stone unturned; detail-oriented; a sense of justice (his version of it: if he suspects you of something, he'll torture the information out of you); trusts his gut, sometimes too much, making on-the-fly decisions that go against the plan he just laid out.
- **Flaws:** narcissistic; looks calm, but his emotions drive him to figure things out without caring about others; careless of other people's feelings even while polite: his politeness is cold rather than sincere.
- **Likes and dislikes:** hates lies; hates suspicion.
- **Interface:** Darfaries.

# July Forway Tea-Drinkers

July fled Freeish and found her way to Forway, where she became the youngest member ever accepted into the Tea-Drinkers. She left because her parents, back in the village where she grew up, didn't take kindly to "him" being her: they thought it was an excuse to get out of farming, and didn't believe she was serious. Her close-quarters combat skills are unmatched in the guild, but there's a good chance her charisma, and making men feel good, helped her get in: she sucked off pretty much every man in the guild who wanted to be sucked.

You can fully trust her as a party member, but don't pry into her past or try to feed her any nari gel: she literally killed someone for daring to squirt Nari Refresher into her mouth. She did warn him to stop, but he didn't.

- **Race:** human.
- **Birthday:** 1013 A.G. Adrian 85th.
- **Attracted to:** she's voracious. Born male, she keeps using nari gels to maintain her cute, feminine appearance.
- **Myers-Briggs:** ENFP. If a perceived "fake" outer shell now matches the "true" inner self, is the outer shell actually fake, or simply a representation of the truth?
- **Intelligence:** good street smarts, and a very capable adventurer. Her emotions can get out of control if someone pushes the wrong buttons, but at least she warns you before exploding. Her pet, Mugi, is extremely cuddly and rather smart: like a cat, but more cooperative, and it understands basic actions.
- **Habits:** touchy, and she goes for the balls not long after a chat. She likes to milk them.
- **Speech:** not very direct in her words, but her body language is very direct.
- **Strengths:** persuasive; charming; beautiful; girly; trustworthy; energetic.
- **Flaws:** pushy; secretive; easily bored, especially with men; aggressive when someone intrudes on her memories or boundaries.
- **Likes and dislikes:** having fun with men; dating; exploring. She doesn't like [Takeo](#takeo-forway-tea-drinkers), since he found out she was born male.
- **Interface:** Darfaries.

# Akemi Blood Blue-Flower-Red-Bone

[Magi](#magi-blood-blue-flower-red-bone)'s mother. Her parents were from Flatland, but later ended up in Blood City, where she was born. She grew up in Flatland City, overhearing adventure stories in the bar where she worked and wanted to become an adventurer, but on her first quest she realized she didn't have the physique, or the will, for it (she also hated the outdoors). Then a perfect example of an adventurer, in her eyes, came up and asked if she was okay. She explained, and he patted her shoulder and suggested she become a historian: all the history, with barely any danger. She agreed, and as a young adult she found her way back to Blood City. Some people are just "destined" for a place: no matter what happens, it keeps grabbing them back.

To her surprise, that same adventurer, [Fernan](#fernan-blood-blue-flower-red-bone), passed by her again one day and asked how she was doing. She said she'd followed his advice, and he giddily asked if he could give her another piece of advice: go on a date with him! They grew closer, and Magi and her little brother were born. Akemi knows a fair amount of history, but her real interest lies in the occult and forbidden knowledge. Her clothes show some wear, since she's saving every ball she can for her son's specialty school.

- **Race:** Wozefaries.
- **Birthday:** 993 A.G. Asa 64th (she had Magi at 26).
- **Attracted to:** humans: charming, jovial and dependent, who care about her more than anyone else.
- **Myers-Briggs:** INTJ.
- **Intelligence:** above average, especially in history and the occult.
- **Habits:** smiles; friendly, but reserved with physical contact; some of [Neferteri](#neferteri-sanbi-royal)'s elegance, but warmer and more sincere. Her eyes wander as her brain works out the bigger picture.
- **Speech:** takes long pauses, preferring to think a sentence through before saying it; avoids stating the obvious or making things awkward. A simple word from her implies a lot.
- **Strengths:** a bookworm, versed in the kingdom's history; disciplined; clever; subtle; articulate, a wordsmith; understanding; curious about the occult, especially the Finaies.
- **Flaws:** cowardly; dogmatic; calculating; self-indulgent; somewhat fragile; overcritical at times, since she overanalyzes some situations.
- **Likes and dislikes:** loves books and tea, and slowly munches on Waterfall finger cakes whenever she can. Hates being out in the wild, especially near or in forests, because of her past.
- **Interface:** Darfaries.
