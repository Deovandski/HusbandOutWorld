# Distance Cost Tier

Goods are sorted into four tiers, which set how much it costs to ship them (see [Economy](/lore/economy/)).

- **Tier I (Cheap Goods):** natural resources; paper and vellum.
- **Tier II (Common Goods):** expensive mineral resources (Nari stones, sapphires, aquamarines, amethysts); Black Honey; sigil rings; lower-quality armor and weapons; inks and dyes.
- **Tier III (Expensive Goods):** rare mineral resources (gold, titanium); higher-quality armor and weapons.
- **Tier IV (Nobility Goods):** custom armor and weapons; household items, or any item of extremely high craft or quality.

# Fost Stone

A stone found in the sediment of the Buhraies lake, on the Buhraies Plains, and all the way from Waterfall City to the Sanbi Kingdom. Closer to the ocean, the riverbed lights up where the rocks get too close to salt water. Fauna, and beasts especially, seem to avoid places where it lies underwater.

- **Light source:** it needs water and salt to work. There's no wick, flame or smoke; the hissing a lit fost stone makes is the water and salt reacting with the stone, which gives off light and steam.
- **No switch:** there's no "turning off the lights". To put one out, you pour out the water to stop the reaction.
- **Where there's none:** people burn animal-fat torches, though not usually inside homes. Gandew and Sundeath use more torches than anywhere else, because they're far from where fost stones are mined.
- **No candles:** there are no candles in Vraki. Wax exists, but it's for sealing letters (like the Winga mail Forway provides).
- **Keeps monsters at bay.**

# Walking Heart

Black, fist-sized stones that form in the irradiated zones of Hifuna, with glowing colors inside that tell what powers they will likely grant. Whoever replaces their heart with one becomes undead, if they survive the binding. Forway has banned them. See [Walking Heart Interface](/lore/walking-heart/).

# Bone Wood

A mixture of bone, iron, ground Nari and Greenbread fruit core. Less flexible than most wood, but it lasts much longer, and mixed with other materials, it can be made more flexible for armor and weapons.

- **Used for:** construction and furniture; armor; weapons.

# Wood & Wood People Wood

Wood People wood is very durable, with a dark red color, and gives off a strong, woody musk when soaked (see [Wood People](/lore/beasts/#wood-people)).

- **Used for:** construction and furniture; decorating armor and weapons.

# Soul Wood

The wood of the Soul Tree, a lone tree with metallic, shiny bark that grows around Blood City and Freeish (see [Soul Tree](/lore/flora/#soul-tree)). It's often used with nari stones, sigils and containers that hold Soul Energy, to keep the Soul Energy from leaking. Its seeds give caffeine, and ground, they're basically the world's coffee.

# Black Wood

The wood of the Black Sapphire Tree, a rare lone tree found between Flatland and Forway that looks dead and soaks up Soul Energy from its surroundings (see [Black Sapphire Tree](/lore/flora/#black-sapphire-tree)). Dark and dense, it's used with nari stones, sigils and Soul Energy containers to keep the Soul Energy from leaking. A single small or medium tree can cost a whole sapphire.

# Winga Dust

Winga beaks and claws, ground up with other beaks and claws. They all have to be strengthened by a Winga before they're removed, and the effect lasts for years before it starts to wear off (see [Winga](/lore/fauna/#winga-pink-winga)). Used to make tools that "cut through anything", since the dust makes them as hard as diamond: usually a coating is applied, then replaced every few years.

- **Used for:** weapons; tools.

# Weapons

What the materials alone cost, shipping included, in Pearls. The range runs from the cheapest region to the most expensive; crafting costs come on top.

- **Dagger:** iron ~293–329; steel ~432–468; Bone Wood ~122–255; chitin ~79–223.
- **Sword:** iron ~683–767; steel ~1,008–1,092; Bone Wood ~285–596; chitin ~185–521.
- **Longsword:** iron ~1,464–1,644; steel ~2,160–2,340; Bone Wood ~611–1,277; chitin ~396–1,116.
- **Bow:** iron ~586–658; steel ~864–936; Bone Wood ~244–511; chitin ~158–446.

Iron and steel cost the same everywhere except Tolerideme, where they're a little pricier. Bone Wood is cheapest in Freeish and Chitin in Gandew. See the [Economy spreadsheet](Legacy/Behind%20The%20Scenes/Spreadsheets/Formulas%20-%20Economy.xlsx) (Weapon & Household Price Calculator).

# Fernan’s Bow

A custom-made weapon of chitin and finely crafted Bone Wood, with Winga beaks and claws for close-quarters combat (CQC). It works as a bow, but also as a melee weapon if needed.

When [Fernan](/lore/characters/#fernan-blood-blue-flower-red-bone) disappeared, so did his bow: his daughter [Magi](/lore/characters/#magi-blood-blue-flower-red-bone) wants to find him, or at least his bow. Wilson Big-Gyut once tried to steal it and got punched for it, so he recreated it as closely as he could from memory.

# Morika’s Armor

A special chitin armor designed after the [Sweet Sting](/lore/beasts/#sweet-sting), used for CQC and impaling. [Morika](/lore/characters/#morika-tolerideme) once wore a Sweet Sting costume to gather Black Honey and liked how empowering it felt, so he saved for about a year and a half to have it made. It makes up for his slow reflexes and hides his face. He becomes another persona in it, [The Stinger](/lore/characters/#the-stinger), and kills with the spike of his Stinger helmet.

# Kitchen/Dining Table

- **Ice Preserver** (a fridge-freezer): made of wood and clay (~1 Amethyst), or iron, wood and clay (~1 Aquamarine).
- **Picker:** a two-pronged fork on one end and a spoon on the other. Each person commonly has their own and takes it with them; some decorate theirs or have their name carved on it to make it unique. Wood ~5 Pearls; Bone Wood ~10 Pearls; chitin ~17 Pearls; iron ~25 Pearls; steel ~1 Amethyst; gold ~1 Aquamarine.
- **Beer Mug:** getting drunk requires the proper tools. Wood ~15 Pearls; Bone Wood ~20 Pearls; chitin ~28 Pearls; iron ~35 Pearls; steel ~3 Amethysts; gold ~5 Aquamarines.
- **Wine/Mead Mug:** gotta be fancy. Wood ~13 Pearls; Bone Wood ~16 Pearls; chitin ~23 Pearls; iron ~29 Pearls; steel ~2 Amethysts; gold ~3 Aquamarines.
- **Bread Plate/Bowl:** made of bread (~0–2 Pearls). It soaks up the juices of your food, and is given to the poor afterward.
