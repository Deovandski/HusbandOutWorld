# Soul Energy

Soul energy is a mysterious energy that can shape reality according to the spell applied to it. Organized into a soul, it has no dimensions and belongs to no universe; unorganized, it pops in and out of existence.

#### Body, genes, soul and memories

- **The body** is the physical form, built from genes.
- **Genes** are the soul made physical: the sum of what the soul remembers.
- **The soul** is a group of virtual particles (quantum foam) that merged to form a being. When it isn't linked to a body, it is where consciousness and soul energy live.
- **Memories** are stored in the soul. The stronger the emotion behind a memory, the longer it lasts, though the body can still alter memories by interfering with thought. Together, a soul's memories make up its genetic information.

The author thinks of it in programming terms: the soul is the database, genes are the class, and the body is an object built from that class.

Soul energy itself is also quantum foam, stored inside the soul. When two beings procreate, their bodies release some of their energy, which prompts nearby quantum foam to coalesce into a new being. That is why a baby's emotional character seems random.

#### Lifespan and aging

Lifespan depends on how strongly the flesh is connected to the soul, and on how many of those connections there are (their density). Aging is those connections weakening or thinning out. No scientific breakthrough has found a way to renew them and extend life. Maybe someone has cracked it, but they aren't sharing.

Every time someone loses a limb and regrows it, the density of their soul connections drops, shortening their potential lifespan. Reattaching the lost limb before healing avoids that extra aging.

Soul strength follows a bell curve. Early adulthood is the most resilient, which makes revival then virtually guaranteed. Infants and the elderly are far less resilient. Nobody knows exactly why, but theories point to memories, to the soul infused by the parents (for infants), and to how fulfilling a life was (for the elderly).

#### Death, revival and soul death

- When someone dies (the heart stops, the brain stops…), the soul stays connected to the flesh through its genetic material, as long as some remains. With no genetic material left, the soul is completely unlinked.
- The dead experience a dream-like state, usually their strongest memories on a loop, or their strongest desire.
- As the body ages and its genetic material decays, so does the soul's connection to the living flesh. If the genetic material is worn down, revival may fail: reviving forces the soul to reconnect to the flesh, and weakened strands (a body almost fully decomposed, or someone very young or very old) can't take that stress.
- A failed revival doesn't mean the soul is gone. It is just too weak to reconnect to the flesh, and it keeps decaying.
- A disconnected soul slowly decays back into quantum foam. This is **soul death** (the technical term is *detachment*). The more soul energy capacity and the stronger the memories someone had at death, the longer their soul takes to dissipate fully.
- There is no such thing as "nari decay".

That a soul lingers after a failed revival is partly why some people are so keen on Soul Research despite its dangers, and why everyone frowns on it. There hasn't been much progress in forcing a soul to bind to flesh again, or in "refueling" it.

#### Growing your soul energy

Soul energy expansion means training your soul to store more energy. A newborn (or a summoned being) starts with a soul whose size is a portion of its parents'. Like any exercise it is tiring and slow to show results, but those who keep at it can store more energy and become more resilient against detachment.

Still, grabbing a nari stick to power a spell is far easier than putting your own energy into it. So, coincidentally, the more affluent tend to have less soul energy: they can just throw a couple of balls at someone else to deal with it.

#### Recharging

Soul energy recharges through emotions and eating, more or less quickly depending on how intense someone's emotions are and on their own ability. Eating a living being pulls some of its soul energy into the one eating it.

#### Measuring and sensing

- **Your badge** shows how much soul energy you have right now: press your thumb on the base of its two thermometer-like gauges, and a red light rises along their scale (1 to 5 on the thin gauge, 6 to 10 on the thick one, spaced logarithmically). Touching the badge doesn't drain any soul energy.
- **Sensing spells** depends on the species and the interface. Darfaries, Wozefaries and Sorefaries can sense soul energy with their own bodies, through their Darfaries veins.

#### Tiers

Spells come in tiers, from Tier I to Tier XVII, and each tier doubles the one before it: twice the power, twice as long (one hour at Tier I, two at Tier II, and so on), and twice the cost, in soul energy or in nari stones (one stone at Tier I, two at Tier II, four at Tier III…). Moving up to the next tier takes 20 casts at the current one.

# Interfaces

There are many ways to interface with soul energy. Some give their users great control over it; others give very little.

The Darfaries Interface and nari gels are examples, but other races, fauna and even beasts have interfaces of their own, and more often than not those are part of their bodies.

How soul energy turns into a spell depends on the interface:

- **Darfaries Interface:** logograms, applied to the palm or through a sigil ring, tell the soul energy what to do. See Darfaries Interface.
- **Nari gels:** the gel is eaten, drunk or injected, and its effect depends on what it's made of and its tier. See Nari Gels.
- **Buhraies, Finaies and Walking Heart interfaces:** each has rules of its own. See their pages.
