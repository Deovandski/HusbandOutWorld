# Blood

Rich fauna and flora drew hunters (back before adventurers were a thing) to spend most of their lives around here. A single fire pit and a nearby hut slowly grew into a cluster of stone fireplaces with a city around them. The fire pits are constantly fed carcasses and other unusable garbage to smelt iron and other minerals and for blacksmithing, and since the city grew out of community spirit, they're public and publicly maintained.

#### Politics

Retired hunters gather around the stone fireplaces every day to help run the city. With no representative, governor or king, Blood doesn't have a strong voice at the Forway meetings. Forway tried to push a government on them and got a backlash (animal guts poured on their Blood embassy), so it switched to a slow takeover through the embassy quest system, signing people up as adventurers instead. With time, they'll change the culture.

**Threats:** beasts, and Forway probing around.

#### Society and culture

Mostly humans, with a handful of Darfaries, a few Wozefaries and tons of Sorefaries, plus a handful of adventurers with Bruntos or Sorefaries.

Hunting totems made of bone, stone, Fost stone and wood are placed and maintained around town, and people eat bone meal to keep their souls strong. They're rather tribal, but welcoming to people who don't look like authority; always ready to fight and compete to be the best, while keeping their hunting secrets within their guilds over generations. Some families actively avoid all Forway embassy services.

#### Food

- **Bagarisa Nose with Cheese:** chewy, but delicious! Hunters created it to use every bit of the meat, and now it's a peculiar dish. 15 Pearls a nose.

  ![][image1]

- **Bloody Blood Sausage:** Blood City's famous Bagarisa-and-blood sausage, in a wavy pattern.

  ![][image2]

- **Blood Sausage:** animal meat and seasoning, in a wavy-pattern sausage.

  ![][image3]

- **Bone Meal:** animal bone ground into meal, for crafting, construction or as a meal aid.

  ![][image4]

Prices are in the [Economy spreadsheet](Legacy/Behind%20The%20Scenes/Spreadsheets/Formulas%20-%20Economy.xlsx).

#### Daily life

- **Clothing:** warm-weather hunting clothes.
- **Education:** Forway created a fundamental school for children to "expand their knowledge", but a chunk of the population "wood-schools" their children instead of paying for it.
- **Crime and law:** a variant of Forway Law. Minor punishments are paid by replacement; for a major one, the criminal becomes prey for the hunters.
- **Local embassy quests:** Sweetlake assistance, Woodland exploration, Nari Caves assistance.
- **Economy:** vellum from animal skin; butchers working game, bone and skin; bone armor and swords; bone meal and items such as totems; Black Mead; nari.
- **Marriage and names:** like the Freeish Kingdom, but a marriage proposal is made with the game they're proudest of instead of fruit.

#### Guilds

- **Blue-Flower-Red-Bone (#7):** created when Akemi and Fernan married.
- **Sweetlake Blood-Tribe (#8):** once one of the richest blood-tribes, now the poorest, about to die out with its last member. People call him a lunatic and avoid the place at all costs. He's researching shapeshifters, since that might reveal what happened to his guild after they all disappeared; he was left behind because he felt lazy that day.

#### Points of interest

- **Quarries (#1 to #4):** a group of hunter brothers always tried to outdo each other in popularity. One built a quarry to win favor building Blood City, the others followed suit, and now Blood City is a popular source of stone.
- **Fireplaces:** the stone fireplaces the city grew around.

  ![][image5]

- **Nari Extraction Coordination Office (#5):** a long name for a building that investigates and prosecutes anyone who harms the nari worms.
- **Animal Part Extraction Facility (#6):** since some animal parts vary in quality and are growing rarer, Forway invested in a facility where the best specimens with desirable parts are kept freshly dead and harvested with Body Heal.

#### Festivals

- **The Great Hunter/Huntress competition:** every Asa, a two-week competition for the strongest hunters: tree cutting, hunting, skinning, wrestling, survival, honey gathering, etc.
- **Bone festival:** every Asa, new totems are added around the city. People leave empty beer cups at the base of the totems they like most; the favorites are placed around town, and the losers are burned.
- **Drunken Sweet Sting Festival:** every Adrian, the top 10 of the Great Hunter/Huntress competition dress in special Sweet Sting costumes and bar-hop around Blood and Sweetlake, drinking Black Mead. Other adventurers follow along celebrating, and bars put on their best show and goods to lure the famous adventurers in.

**Burial:** the dead are shoved into the furnace: in their mindset, people are no different from animals.

**Visual references:** like the Freeish Kingdom, but with hunting totems instead of trees with Trunk People.

# Cruro

Other races rarely visit, out of fear of the Cruros, who act on instinct.

#### Politics

A tribe leader's word is final. To become tribe leader, you fight the current one; when another Cruro wins, all the previous leader's infants are killed and eaten. Highly intelligent Cruros don't take part in the tribal fights.

**Threats:** the dumb ones, or their pets, attack passersby once in a while, and if the population grows too large, adventurers could invade to thin it out. Some Sanbi citizens still hold a grudge against the Cruros for taking away the easy job of collecting pearls.

#### Society and culture

Mostly Crocus and dumb Cruros, a few intelligent ones, and rarely a highly intelligent one who stays. No religion. Tribal: a young Cruro's rite of passage is to kill one Wood Person, bring the body back to build a hut, and tame a Crocus.

#### Food

- **Sun-Rotted Animals:** a delicacy among Cruros, and perhaps some humans. Traded for other food, or free if the Tribe Leader gives it to you.

#### Daily life

- **Clothing:** none.
- **Education:** learning from the elders. Highly intelligent Cruros learn more through the Half-Cruro guild in Sanbi or Tastyleaf.
- **Crime and law:** it seems like a free-for-all, but they manage to survive in a rather brutal, tribal way of life.
- **Economy:** they don't use balls, but they can barter, since they aren't citizens of the four kingdoms (highly intelligent Cruros are the exception). They trade the bodies of their dead and their pets, pearls from the swamp, and corals for making lime, for Wooly beef jerky and other food.
- **Marriage:** the tribe leader is the only one who can mate with all the Crocus in the region.
- **Names:** they don't have names. The highly intelligent ones get one from the Sanbi or Tastyleaf embassy, through the Half-Cruro guild.

#### Points of interest: the Cruro tribes

According to the smartest Cruros, quite a few tribes divide the territory:

- **#2:** rather peaceful.
- **#3, #4, #5 and #6:** the oldest, in a constant power struggle.
- **#7 and #8:** have some sort of alliance.
- **#9:** a newer tribe that works well with the Half-Cruros.
- **#1 and #10:** too busy dealing with the Wood People to care what happens around them.

**Burial:** they trade their dead for food.

**Visual reference:** Brazil's Pantanal.

# Deltacross

Two villages across the lake offer transport, and the constant flow of people through them gave the city its economic lifeline. They see themselves as villages of the Freeish Kingdom, but Forway made them a city of their own to shrink the kingdom; through backdoor negotiations, they stay part of the Freeish Kingdom anyway.

#### Politics

On the surface, like the other cities; on the down low, like the villages of the Freeish Kingdom.

**Threats:** Forway stamping on their way of life for trying to be part of the Freeish Kingdom.

#### Society and culture

Mostly humans, some Wozefaries and a few other species, with a significant number of richer, older humans. Like Freeish, no overarching religion, just a random mix from all the people. A melting pot like Forway City, but not as cold, since fewer people live there.

#### Food

- **Drink Floaters:** drinks served in Cocona shells, which you let float off into the stream when you're done. 10 to 30 Pearls.

#### Daily life

- **Clothing, marriage and names:** like the Freeish Kingdom and Forway City.
- **Education:** follows Forway.
- **Crime and law:** follows Forway Law. Minor punishments are paid by replacement; for a major one, the criminal is sent to Freeish.
- **Local embassy quests:** nothing extra.
- **Economy:** helping people cross the delta; resorts and bars.

#### Guilds

- **Calm Water Resort Guild (#4):** adventurers who are getting old and have no money left join this guild, spending their retirement at the Calm Water Resort in exchange for helping protect it against threats.

#### Points of interest

- **Crossing Buildings (#1):** large buildings where eguas and their carriages board a bigger boat, pulled by six eguas. Because of the water, the boat pulls about 10 tons: 15 full wagons per crossing, back and forth. 15 Pearls per wagon per crossing, 2 Pearls per person. The eguas pulling the boat are all dressed in uniform, a tradition from the village's early days.

  ![][image10]

- **The Floating Egua Hotel (#2):** one of the oldest hotels, with entertainment for all tastes.
- **The Sunken Egua Hotel (#3):** another famous hotel.
- **Calm Water Resort (#4):** home of the Calm Water Resort Guild. Wealthier residents who believe in Gandew's spiritual powers often come to live here in their later years, especially those who think they might be haunted by the spirits of people they screwed over.
- **Delta Bar (#5):** a famous open-air bar with no seats, out in the calm waters; beer and food are served from covered stands. Open only in the summer.

  ![][image11]

#### Festivals

- **Egua Uniform Contest:** every Asa and Adrian, a contest to design new uniforms for the eguas; they must be waterproof and suit the weather. The winner's name goes on a board displayed at the crossing buildings.
- **Drink in the Delta contest:** on the last day, Delta Bar holds a massive contest to see who can drink the most without leaving their spot. Peeing in the water isn't frowned upon; it's encouraged, to spare your bladder.
- **Old Bone:** every Paulin, the Calm Water Resort hosts a festival for the old people living there, with tons of music, fight choreography, bards' tales, etc.

**Burial:** no widely followed ritual. Some throw the bodies into the delta so they're carried out to sea; others follow the customs of where they came from.

# Endlessfog

As humans settled on this island, the Bruntos began interacting with them and grew close. Humans made great-tasting food and shelter for them, and the Bruntos brought raw ingredients, helped with farming, and helped them hide from the Lishies. Living here for years, people noticed the weather is unusually warm and humid all year long, with rain every day at Sunnu; it's certainly not for everyone. Humans and Bruntos developed a taste for Lonllows, but humans couldn't farm them on a large scale until the Bruntos taught them to plant fish in the ground before putting in the Lonllow branches. With that, they could be farmed all year long.

#### Politics

A decentralized government among the villages, led by guilds that work independently but cooperate rather well. Forway counts it as a single city, and the guilds choose a smart, good-natured representative with a strong bond with a Brunto.

**Threats:** a Brunto overpopulation, especially if they start a war against humans.

#### Society and culture

Mostly Bruntos, with humans coordinating with them. People are often paler than usual, from less sun. They're nature-minded: at some point they even started burying people in the land and planting banana trees on top. Colder to foreigners, and outright violent with anyone who harms them or the Bruntos.

#### Food

- **Lonllow Breadcake:** a delicious, not-too-sweet breadcake, common on Endlessfog. About 9 Pearls for a loaf the size of two fists.

  ![][image15]

#### Daily life

- **Clothing:** between Sanbi and Tolerideme, for colder weather.
- **Education:** follows Forway, but also teaches how to cooperate, especially with the Bruntos.
- **Crime and law:** like the Sanbi Kingdom.
- **Local embassy quests:** Newland exploration.
- **Economy:** exports Lonllows all year long; the harvest guilds' cooperation keeps a constant supply flowing through the kingdoms.
- **Marriage:** made official by the village leader. Then the couple (who must buy or build a house first) goes to the House of Bruntos to bond with one and bring it home.
- **Names:** like the Sanbi Kingdom.

#### Guilds

- **Asa, Paulin, Moacir and Adrian Lonllow Harvest (#2 to #5):** each guild coordinates the harvest in its own month of the year.

#### Points of interest

- **House of Bruntos (#1):** a house that unbonded Bruntos have all to themselves, where humans and Bruntos come to bond. If a bond forms, the Brunto moves into the human's home. Adventurers can do the same, and if they bond with a Brunto, they can take it along as a companion for free (a decision made to boost tourism, though it rarely happens). Inside stands a 20-meter wooden statue of the Bruntos who taught them to plant Lonllows and hide from the Lishies.

#### Festivals

- **Lonllows Cuisine:** a big fest in Paulin, where people from all over make food and drinks with Lonllows. The winner takes home a massive stack of Lonllows.
- **Long & Yellow Sanbi Celebration:** every Adrian, a grand festival celebrates the first contact between humans and Bruntos. Lonllows are baked with Sanbi, cheese and a tiny bit of Tastyleaf.
- **White Roundies Arrival:** every winter the White Roundies arrive, and people party with them, since they behave like dogs but are the size of a human. People also try to trade them food for things they find in the deep sea: sometimes they get lucky and get gold, sometimes a half-eaten fish.

**Burial:** buried naked in the fields. Their clothes are washed in seawater and returned to their guild or relatives.

# Flatland

#### Politics

A few dominant guilds, tied to the leftover bandits and their children, basically control Flatland, so they pick the embassy representative. They put the most charming Wozefaries from their guilds out front as a facade for their suspicious activities. They're very strict with anyone who makes false accusations or speaks badly of the royal guilds, and find ways to punish them (real-world comparison: Thailand's royal family).

**Threats:** the occasional drunk, pervert or bandit, or all three in one person; and adventurers investigating something a Flatland guild didn't hide well enough.

#### Society and culture

Mostly Wozefaries, with some Sorefaries, a few humans, and some humans from Sundeath. Lots of tourists, leaning heavily male. Their motto: "Flatland is here to satisfy you." Very open-minded and warm, but step out of line or pry into their private matters, and they'll kill you to protect their town and its people. Reserved about private matters, but very accommodating about everything else: good listeners, touchy and handsy. Boots and sandals stay in one spot inside the home (real-world comparison: Thailand).

#### Food

- **Boiling Ramen:** ramen (from Tolerideme) cooked on the hottest geyser, with seasonings found only here, served with meat, wild game or fish. 7 to 30 Pearls a bowl.

#### Daily life

- **Clothing:** closer to the Freeish Kingdom, mixed with Forway.
- **Education:** follows Forway.
- **Crime and law:** follows Forway Law. Minor punishments are paid by replacing the goods; for a major one, the criminal is thrown into the Skin Peeler.
- **Local embassy quests:** Fuhrair assistance.
- **Economy:** a spring town for travelers and a rest stop before the Gandew Kingdom; adventurers hire Sorefaries here. Mines Amethysts for Forway City, and mines and processes Fost stones.
- **Marriage:** an invitation to buy or build a house together.
- **Names:** like the Gandew Kingdom and Forway.

#### Guilds

- **Sand Sea (#6):** known for capturing Wozefaries and Sorefaries in the desert.
- **Hot Skin:** one of the earliest guilds, famous for running the OutBalls and InBalls resorts. It's common to see carriages packed with men stop right in front of the guild and everyone go straight in for fun times: inside, the women working there move about, and you can pick one to "spend time with".

#### Points of interest

- **OutBalls Spring Resort:** a male-focused spring resort around the biggest hot spring. Legend says the Hot Skin guild's children got together and renamed the resort's banner overnight. The guild was furious, but it attracted customers, and the kids' punishment was going to every house handing out ball-shaped soap.
- **InBalls Spring Resort:** like OutBalls, but female-focused.
- **Flatland Darfaries Market (#1):** the city's center, where many products to and from the Gandew Kingdom are found.
- **Skin Peeler Geyser (near #1):** an incredibly hot geyser in a scalding pond. People stand around it in winter to warm up, and being near its steam is said to be rejuvenating.

  ![][image18]

- **Life 'n' Oasis Resort (#2):** a large resort for rich people, owned and run by one of the bandit guilds, which is rumored to still operate underground.
- **Abandoned Dock (#3):** fishing isn't considered viable on the massive lake, even though Fihi is its specialty: the Fihi there developed a taste for humans and figured out how to sink them into the water. Usually adventurers fish there, or people cast from the shore; most people catch their Fihi on the Buhraies Plains instead.
- **Sorefaries Line (#4):** a really fast zip line from the mountain down to the base of the Skin Peeler Geyser.
- **Grand Miner (#5):** the region's biggest mining operation, specializing in Amethysts for minting.

#### Festivals

- **Hot in the Cold:** every Asa, a food festival around the Skin Peeler Geyser.
- **Second Life:** every Moacir, a festival across the whole town celebrating every Wozefaries and Sorefaries who has landed since the last one.
- **Companion Meetup:** every Adrian, a massive meetup where Sorefaries sit at tables and adventurers come over to talk about themselves. If the adventurers and a Sorefaries form a bond, the adventurers pay the city a fee to take them as a companion.

**Burial:** bodies are often buried in the desert; Wozefaries and Sorefaries grew used to being buried at the same spot where they landed.

# Forway

A city bristling with research, academia and rich people. Forway's style is gothic, like the Addams Family (metal bathtubs, black with red corners), and it's also known for its bureaucracy. Rich people live inside the city, where a badge is required; poor people live around it, where no badge is needed.

#### Politics

The central command for all the kingdoms and cities. Security is tightened to show the Forway leaders' power, especially at the time of the Prosperity Treaty meeting (Section #7).

**Threats:** anyone trying to overthrow the current balance of power, or planting false information between leaders to provoke arguments.

#### Society and culture

Mixed, in people and beliefs, though money is becoming the religion. A big city: people don't talk to strangers much, everyone minds their own business, and people are commonly called by their profession, especially by those who can't be bothered to remember names. A nari gel frenzy for style is common, so you see people of every color, texture, clothing and body type. The craziest body mods are for the rich; everyone else tends to change their hair, in droves, or small body parts that don't affect their work.

#### Food

- **Wooly Filet Mignon wrapped in smoked Bagarisa bacon:** one of the most expensive dishes around, served with mashed fried Cadioca and Domar barley. 1 Amethyst a meal; usually the first entrée at expensive taverns.

#### Daily life

- **Clothing:** a gap between rich and poor, mixing styles from every other kingdom.
- **Education:** a standard dress code and teaching (see Education).
- **Crime and law:** Forway Law. If the person can pay for the crime, they walk away; if not, they go to prison.
- **Guards:** Forway guards trust their crossbows with their lives. Their shiny armor and generous Forway funding make them very visible and somewhat slow, but they always travel in groups, so picking a fight isn't advised, unless you know how to exploit their superiority complex, common among these guards: let them think they have the upper hand, then hit back with a surprise counterattack at the last second and make them bleed out. Some have baited them into traps, too, but that's harder when they have a Sorefaries or another companion watching their surroundings. Either way, once you're fighting them, beware of their revival capabilities, unless you take away their hearty and expensive supply of Black Honey, often kept in their leg pockets.
- **Local embassy quests:** embassy checking and verification.
- **Economy:** trade and tourism.
- **Marriage:** mixed. Some marry by their own customs, going to the palace to speak with their kingdom or city representative. A growing trend is contract marriage between money-hungry people.
- **Names:** mixed.

#### Guilds

- **White Bell (#3):** famous for a single courtesan who rose in life after a career pleasuring her guests, and now controls multiple brothels. She has a big, well-known homestead where some "parties" happen.

#### Points of interest

- **Forway Palace:** at the city center; entering requires a badge. The lower floors are the embassy, the upper floors are restricted to Sapphire adventurers and kingdom and city officials, and underground is research and experimentation. They cross-check names against books and other people if they suspect someone.
- **Forway and You:** the biggest journal, issued weekly on Freeday, with news, fashion and more.
- **Emelia's Flowery Paradise:** in Forway's southwest district, with a bakery nearby.
- **Broken Knuckle:** the tavern where Honati worked.
- **Specialized Schools (#E types):** see Education.
- **The Wood People Picker (#1):** Yosuke Forway's restaurant, outside the gates.
- **End Lake (a.k.a. the Shitter):** a man-made lake south of the city center where Forway's sewage is dumped, since there's too much to handle with Soul Energy alone.
- **Lonida Stadium (#2):** a privately owned stadium where some big events happen.

![][image19]![][image20]

#### Festivals

- **Adventurer recognition:** every Paulin, to recognize adventurers and promote the best (most loyal) ones.
- **Fashion Show:** at the start of every month, for artists to show off their work.
- **All the way festival:** every Moacir, each city and kingdom builds a movable monument and parades it through Forway amid the festivities, to promote cultural integration in such a mixed city.

  ![][image21]

**Burial:** depends heavily on the culture. If the person has no money, the body is incinerated.

# Old Fuhrair

The largest mining town, built among the ruins of Old Fuhrair. Some leftover buildings remain, and Fuhraies traps can still be uncovered.

#### Politics

Everyone is out for themselves. **Threats:** cold snaps, and themselves.

#### Society and culture

Mixed, with more Darfaries than humans, and beliefs leaning toward the individualist side. Gambling is everywhere: people are trying to make as much money as possible.

#### Food

- **Old Kick in the Ass:** a mix of a bunch of different shit, just to clear out stock. Guaranteed to taste funky, and to kick your ass.

#### Daily life

- **Clothing:** heavy, Gandew style.
- **Education:** none to speak of, since the jobs are basically mining stuff out of the ground.
- **Crime and law:** sorted out among the people. When things get hairy, Sapphire adventurers are sent in to stop it, and Amethysts are sent regularly to help the guards rotating through the city.
- **Economy:** mining.
- **Marriage, names and burial:** mixed, leaning toward Gandew's ways.

#### Guilds

- **Big Rock (#2):** one of the most famous guilds, where a single person found a large Sapphire and spent it all on pussy and beer.

#### Points of interest

- **Falling Rock Tavern (#1):** a famous tavern where people fresh from underground go to get lit!
- **Mining Entry Points (#3):** the mining happens beneath the city, with many ways in. Most are public; a few are privately held by guilds.

# Ganyrami

Since the fall of Ganyrami, some humans have made the ruins their home. Some bandits live in the refuge too, but they seem to live in symbiosis with the locals. That made Forway want to place an embassy here to change the vibe, but it was, and still is, pretty tough.

#### Politics

Somewhat detached from everyone. The Freeish leaders have some shady dealings with people here, so they're left alone, and Forway is finding it hard to intervene.

**Threats:** the bandits taking over if the symbiosis breaks, or if they leech too much; Forway deciding to take firm action.

#### Society and culture

All kinds of people and beliefs, including some shady characters. The past is the past, and nobody talks about it: you deal with your own shit. Badges are rarely used, since they mean showing your true self, and contracts are mostly made outside the normal contract system, so you have to enforce them yourself if needed.

#### Food

- **Food Paste:** Ganyrami invented food pastes and still makes the best. They come in all kinds of flavors and let people travel long distances on the cheap, especially with a large batch for the road. Some bandits use them to avoid certain places, or to scout new spots to hit.

  ![][image25]

#### Daily life

- **Clothing:** simple things, for the dense, muggy forest.
- **Education:** barely any.
- **Crime and law:** unreported. People sort things out themselves instead of relying on the embassy or the guards, and those who do rely on the guards tend to get into bigger trouble anyway.
- **Local embassy quests:** discovery and infiltration quests to find bandits, given only to adventurers they trust a whole lot.
- **Guilds:** no Forway-recognized guild is based here; Forway is trying to change that, without much luck.
- **Economy:** some local goods from the forest, plus an unnatural boom (likely from all the stolen shit): you can find all kinds of things, from all kinds of places.
- **Marriage:** no commonly accepted official way, and guilds rarely form, since people don't talk about their pasts.
- **Names:** all kinds; people usually give themselves new names.

#### Points of interest

- **Central Hut:** one of the biggest Lishies huts ever seen. It stood the test of time and somehow survived the fall of Ganyrami (mostly because the human who conquered it took the throne for himself). There's no leader, but people take turns keeping the hut's fire lit and having someone there to act as leader, since Forway demanded someone answer for the city. The leader is basically whoever feels like keeping the fire going. The catch: Forway requires that person's badge, and they always say they don't have one. It infuriates the Forway representatives, but they don't want to overstep and cause unrest.
- **Buna's Fishery:** an old place that somehow has never been robbed, likely because its owners have deep connections to shady stuff, with hints (past rumors) of what they did to people who tried.

**Festivals:** none. The culture is too uncoordinated for anything that brings everyone together.

**Burial:** depends on whether anyone knew what the person wanted; otherwise, burial in the forest, away from prying eyes, but expect to be stripped of all valuables.

# Sundeath

#### Politics

A royal family line commands everything. The pharaoh's word is absolute; pharaohs wear a gold crown with jewels, and the one in command decides who becomes the next pharaoh when they grow too old.

**Threats:** the desert, and Forway deciding to take over completely.

#### Society and culture

Mostly darker-skinned people, with some Darfaries, Wozefaries and Sorefaries, and adventurers.

![][image28]![][image29]

Statues of the land's beasts and fauna stand around, with eyes of pure Fost stone and bodies of mud. The Sayna is one of the most powerful humanoids. Most people wear simple, plain clothing, and richer people wear gold accessories. They're shy by nature, especially with foreigners, very clean, and drenched in perfume. Pharaohs can only marry their brothers or sisters. Boots and sandals are left outside the home.

#### Food

- **Black Honey Aged Wooly Cheese:** a specialty made by putting Wooly cheese into some of Sweetlake's Black Honey vaults and letting it rest for about 3 months.

  ![][image30]

- **Purplebread with Sandworm:** a sweet and nutty combination of fruit and meat on a stick. About 15 Pearls.

  ![][image31]

#### Daily life

- **Clothing:** Egyptian and medieval themed.
- **Education:** like Forway, with more about fauna and beasts.
- **Crime and law:** similar to Forway Law, but with the presumption of guilt until proven innocent. Many people avoid the city because of this law, and how easily locals abuse it over trivial matters.
- **Local embassy quests:** exploring the west and the desert.
- **Economy:** farming and selling desert goods. It's something of an adventurer town, with hotels, healers, etc.
- **Marriage:** a man and a woman can marry with the pharaoh's approval; richer couples create a guild.
- **Names:** Egyptian.

#### Guilds

- **Pharaohs (#2):** the most powerful guild in Sundeath.

#### Points of interest

- **Pathway:** two massive tunnels running from the entrance almost to the other end of the mountain. Non-citizens may only use these pathways; every road off them is closed to non-citizens, since local guilds own them.
- **Trial Center (#1):** people are often judged and executed in front of everyone, and their remains thrown into the desert.

#### Festivals

- **Protector Celebration:** every Forrday, a celebration of one of the protectors (humanoid animals, beasts or fauna).

**Burial:** mummies placed at great depths, for people who are worthy, or who paid large sums. Anyone with a shady or dark past is most likely thrown out into the desert, so they don't taint the others' "after-living" space.

# Sweetlake

The kingdoms' main western city, the frontier for exploring the villages out west and unknown lands.

#### Politics, society and culture

Similar to Blood, but with more adventurers living around here as they seek out new lands. **Threats:** beasts, and disorganization from within.

#### Food

- **Sweetlake Bottled Water:** created by a snake-oil salesman to get money out of rich people. 30 Pearls a liter.

  ![][image35]

- **Black Mead:** mead made from Black Honey.

  ![][image36]

#### Daily life

- **Clothing:** similar to Blood.
- **Education:** none provided, for now; some people move to Blood for schooling.
- **Crime and law:** follows Forway Law, but with no embassy, people sort things out among themselves, since guards rarely come by.
- **Local embassy quests:** like Blood.
- **Economy:** Black Honey, mead, and being the western front for exploration.

# Tastyleaf

One of the first tribes to become a city after Aldemeri fell, though its one-of-a-kind economy keeps it distant from everything. Humans found a giant tree in a hidden garden whose leaves make most things incredibly tasty; the story diverges on whether humans killed the garden's owners or found it abandoned. The tree also lights up when it rains, and helps keep Hifuna's beasts away from the settlement.

#### Politics

The tree is massive, so at first everyone shared it. But as the city grew, so did the number of people who wanted to own and profit from it, and people from different guilds banded together into new guilds to seize control of the tree. Forway kind of turns a blind eye, as long as the tree isn't harmed.

**Threats:** the Cruros eat a few people a year; sea travel is difficult; and beasts that stray south run into people traveling around.

#### Society and culture

Mostly humans, plus a significant number of Wozefaries, from back when they were slaves. People worship the tree for its natural protection against the beasts. Similar to Sanbi, but warier of strangers; they constantly argue over the tree's grounds, but will act if anyone dares to harm it.

#### Food

- **Baked Crocus with Tastyleaf:** Crocus meat seasoned with Tastyleaf.

#### Daily life

- **Clothing:** similar to Sanbi, but made from Croro leather.
- **Education:** Forway provides basic education free to citizens who sign a contract to stay on Tastyleaf for the rest of their lives, to give the tree some protection, at least until someone figures out how to clone it.
- **Crime and law:** like Forway, but for major cases, the criminal is forced to go out and fight beasts; if they survive, their punishment is absolved.
- **Local embassy quests:** Newland exploration, Crocus extermination, Cruro reconnaissance, checking on Hifuna.
- **Economy:** revolves around the Tastyleaf tree in the fall, when the major shipments come and go. The rest of the year it trades with the Cruros and serves as a town for the adventurers working the area.
- **Marriage:** like Sanbi, but with a heart made of perfect leaves.
- **Names:** like the Sanbi Kingdom.

#### Guilds

- **East Ground (#1), South Ground (#2), West Ground (#3) and North Ground (#4):** each controls the leaves that fall on its side of the tree.

#### Points of interest

- **Tastyleaf Tree:** see Tastyleaf under Flora.

- **Tastyleaf Docking (#5):** the main way in and out of Tastyleaf; few dare to go through the Cruro tribes instead.
- **WhiteLeaf Hotel/Tavern (#6):** one of the area's first hotels. It expanded, added a tavern, and set itself apart with Wozefaries and humans dancing around wearing nothing but preserved white leaves.

#### Festivals

- **Picking Festival:** every fall, when the WhiteLeaf tree begins to shed its leaves, people from the four guilds compete to gather the most leaves, using wind Soul Energy to move them.

# Triangle

When humans fled the Lishies' hungry mouths, they came across land where natural rocks formed triangles. They began building in triangular shapes, and since the triangle is such a strong natural structure, the buildings survived the harsh storms from the sea. Certain people took the idea and created a community to make sure everyone followed the triangle way of doing things. When tons of people fled to the city during the Greed Period, the community forced "The Triangle Way" on everyone: it calmed and organized the people, while letting three guilds control essentially everything. They control everyone so tightly that even people's poop schedules are synced: there are bathrooms everywhere, and everyone goes at the same time.

#### Politics

A triangle on the forehead marks the three royal guilds, which control everything on the island.

**Threats:** other places, if they discover the truth; tsunamis.

#### Society and culture

Only humans may live in the city; any other species must wear a triangle amulet for the length of their stay. The triangle gave them new life, protecting them from the elements; it started with people, and has become something above and beyond people: a thing, a concept. Similar to Sanbi, but not as warm and friendly if you don't believe in the power of the triangle, and not exactly friendly to other species either; pets are frowned upon. They worship triangles, especially the original triangle, kept in the Triangle Cathedral. People come to touch it for good luck and health, and some come from elsewhere hoping it will fix problems caused by their DNA, since Soul Energy can't seem to.

#### Food

- **Quick Cake:** a Tenderthrough and Lonllow cake that tastes amazing, but passes through you incredibly fast. About 5 Pearls for a big slice.

  ![][image42]

#### Daily life

- **Clothing:** like the Sanbi Kingdom, but with triangle cuts in their shirts, and everything else revolving around the triangle craziness.
- **Education:** based on Forway, but with an emphasis on Triangle teachings, to pull people into the cult from early on.
- **Crime and law:** like the Sanbi Kingdom.
- **Economy:** Tenderthrough, and serving as the trade bridge between Freeish and Sanbi at the south end of the region.
- **Marriage:** in the Triangle Cathedral, with both people's guilds present.
- **Names:** like the Sanbi Kingdom.

#### Guilds

- **Ronaldo Lazyeye (#1), Purple-Magina (#2) and Angry-Jin (#3):** the mansions of the three guilds controlling the island; their colors are yellow, purple and red.

#### Points of interest

- **Triangle Central:** the central building behind Triangle City.
- **Cleansing Fountain:** where people go for public enemas: they touch their butt to the fountain, and it cleans them out.

#### Festivals

- **Triangle Fest:** every Moacir, a crowd plays the triangle to the same rhythm around the cathedral, hoping to boost the triangle's power to keep enemies at bay.
- **White Roundies Arrival:** every winter the White Roundies arrive, and people elsewhere party with them, since they behave like dogs but are the size of a human, and try to trade them food for things they find in the deep sea: sometimes gold, sometimes a half-eaten fish. Triangle citizens tend to keep them at a distance.

**Burial:** like Sanbi, but the stones here are triangle-shaped.

**Visual references:** houses with a medieval mix of sharp angles, plus a concrete-and-glass look.

# Waterfall

Over time, smart people saw the potential of using the waterfall to grind grain, and the city flourished. With the Prosperity Treaty, it became the center for producing fish meal (from Sanbi's dried fish) and flour (from Tolerideme's Domar wheat), and for tourists who come to see its beauty.

#### Politics

"Those who control the water mills control the city." They're kind of lazy about the controlling part, though, and unwilling to deal with anything that doesn't affect them directly, so Forway City usually has to bring in someone to run the embassy, which also runs the city. Some citizens still treat Sanbi citizens better than Tolerideme's, since Toleridemians are seen as lazy for relying on Soul Energy for everything. Part of it is that Tolerideme is always fair on pay and straight in negotiation, which leaves the more Sanbian, flexible Waterfall in a tough spot.

**Threats:** not really.

#### Society and culture

Mostly humans, with a decent number of fat people, since Tolerideme is nearby and fresh-baked bread is cheap. Darfaries are around too, also slightly overweight; not many Wozefaries or Sorefaries, because of the colder climate. Beliefs mix respect for the "Sea" god with reverence for the "Three Smart Ladies". They keep to themselves but aren't unwelcoming to strangers, except Tolerideme citizens, who get the "you wait in line longer" treatment. They're on the lazy side, with so much job stability; they shit on Tolerideme citizens, but apparently don't see their own laziness.

#### Food

- **Finger Cake:** after someone lost a finger in the cogs, they saw a cool cake possibility and created dense finger cakes, great for adventuring. Famous flavors include Domar, Gyut Domar, Lonllow Domar, Purplebread Domar, Fishmeal with Tastyleaf Domar, Black Honey Domar, Rakuja Gyut Domar, Cadioca and Tastyleaf Cadioca. About 7 Pearls each, about the size of a fist; a refillable leather pack of 6 costs 68 Pearls, and a refill alone 42. From Vol 2: "From the same crochet bag, she dug around, pulled out a rectangular thatch box, and slid it open, revealing a yellowish color with red dots along the width and girth of a finger inside."

  ![][image45]

#### Daily life

- **Clothing:** similar to Tolerideme.
- **Education:** like Forway.
- **Crime and law:** like the Sanbi Kingdom.
- **Economy:** grinding Domar wheat (by boat from Tolerideme) into flour and dried fish (by boat from the Sanbi Kingdom) into fish meal, plus the goods passing through the elevators.
- **Marriage:** like the Tolerideme Kingdom; declaring your love during an elevator descent is rather common.
- **Names:** like the Tolerideme and Sanbi Kingdoms.

#### Guilds

- **Elevator:** controls the elevators.
- **Finger Cake Maker:** controls Waterfall's famous finger cakes.

#### Points of interest

- **Water Mills:** several mills harness the waterfall to grind all kinds of things. A few are owned by the same guild, which makes it rather powerful.
- **Cliff Walk (10,764 steps):** carved into the stone long ago by former Sanbi Kingdom citizens; each step is 20 cm. With the great elevators built, it's now mostly used as a punishment, or by people who want to avoid the elevators, for money, law or weight-loss reasons (and who prefer not to die, or suffer by other means).
- **Elevators:** guided up and down by cables along the side of the wooden structure. They move at about 10 km/h (about 12 minutes up or down, with speeding up and slowing down). Maximum load: 5,120 kg. 20 Pearls a person (double if really fat, or a Darfaries; Sorefaries ride free), or 1 Pearl per 3 kg of cargo. Locks at the top and bottom keep the elevator from floating away when it has no cargo.

  ![][image46]

- **Rainy Waterfalls:** when the Buhraies Plains were created, a double waterfall appeared, splitting the land between them into a spot the Wood People couldn't reach. The waterfalls are so big and tall they create clouds over the whole region.
- **Elevator Guild (#1):** where the people who own and maintain the elevators gather.
- **Above the Clouds Hotel (#2):** a rich hotel with spectacular views of the clouds.
- **Longing for Sun Hotel (#3):** one of the cheapest hotels around: no windows, just a rough painting of the sun where the windows used to be.
- **Drunk Step Bar (#4):** a bar so old it once had another name, but so many people came here to get loaded before climbing the massive stairs that it got renamed.
- **Halfway Inn (#5):** a cheap inn and restaurant with a couples' discount for taking the stairs together.
- **Finger Cake Factory (#6):** a large factory making all kinds of finger cakes.

#### Festivals

- **Waterfall Grand Race:** every summer, a race to see who reaches Sanbi fastest after jumping off the docks at the foot of the waterfall and swimming downstream. Soul Energy of any kind and intensity is allowed, and so is any clothing; you can't harm others, and your body is yours to enhance as you like. The record holder is a female Darfaries, at 13.5 hours.
- **Waterfall Jump Race:** every summer, a competition to see who drops down the waterfall fastest and lands safely in the water below.

**Burial:** some follow Sanbi's practices, others Tolerideme's.

**Visual references:** like Sanbi, but hillier, mixed with Tolerideme's Wood People building structures.

# Wingarest

The village elders keep things afloat, since Forway doesn't seem to care much. **Threats:** not really, apart from the Wood People.

Mostly humans, some Darfaries, and some adventurers who settled here for Winga hunting. No real beliefs, just respect for their food source. They have Tolerideme's laziness: everything is chill and calm, and everyone is sleepy all the time, without a care in the world.

#### Food

- **Pickled Winga Eggs:** Wingarest's famous food: fresh Winga eggs pickled with special seasonings. About 1 Pearl an egg pickled for half a year, about 2 Pearls for a year.
