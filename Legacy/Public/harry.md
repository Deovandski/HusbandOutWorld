# Harry

![](../Behind%20The%20Scenes/Light%20Novel/Arc%20%231%20-%20Dubious%20Contract/Vol1/2_1.png)

  
  

From an unfulfilled, stagnate life that ended in a massive heart attack, Harry awoke in a new world after an apparently failed scientific experiment to summon the perfect husband for Princess Maiara Sanbi Royal.

What will he do now with the new lease on life given to him? Does he have a choice in it?

## 1039 A.G Paulin 14th, Gannday.

![](../Behind%20The%20Scenes/Illustrations/Harry/Harry%20%231.png)

The day Harry was summoned into the new world, awakening in a tower with blood everywhere and a lingering stench of Bagarisa musk.

## 1039 A.G Paulin 15th, Forrday.

![](../Behind%20The%20Scenes/Illustrations/Harry/Harry%20%232.png)

The day he was propelled into a battle with Princess Maiara. Perhaps all she wanted at the end of the day was a punching bag.

## 1039 A.G Paulin 17th, Tollday.

![](../Behind%20The%20Scenes/Illustrations/Harry/Harry%20%233%20and%20%234.png)

Harry awoke from his coma after dying at Maiara's hand and being forced to revive out of his stored energy rather than using black honey.
