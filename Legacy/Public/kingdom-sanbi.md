# Kingdom - Sanbi

## Sanbi Kingdom

![](../Behind%20The%20Scenes/Illustrations/Emblems/Sanbi%20Kingdom.png)

  
  

Do you like the smell of fish and humid, salty air? Do you prefer frugality and a sense of connection between people over material possessions? Yes? Then this is the place to be for you! Be amazed by the infinite ways they can prepare Sanbi fish; most dishes are tasty, but some can be too experimental and require an adventurous spirit to gulp it down.

The Sanbi Kingdom is one of the coolest and friendliest places to be. I mean, even the King himself will talk to you if you approach him on the streets. Sanbi shit! If he sees you having a good time, he will most likely drop whatever he's doing to hang out. Do visit the bars when night falls, as there is always stuff to do and fun people to drink with! Get ready to make friends and memories for a lifetime! Even the local Cruros will share a beer with you, and if you happen to drink all of your balls away, a helpful stranger might have a place for you to crash and sober up.

Just be sure not to go too wild and leave off East towards Aldemeri or Cruro, or you might never come back—unless you can defend yourself.

Just be okay with their extra handsy and straightforward culture if you want to chummy up to them. They are known for living and enjoying the present. As such, they don't really have many festivals; they prefer to celebrate life every day.

![](../Behind%20The%20Scenes/Illustrations/World%20Map/Sanbi%20City%20Map.png)

  
  

The Sanbi Kingdom is also the capital name—which surely never confused anyone! As it grew from the harbor area and around the Sanbi Castle, its shape became like an inverted triangle. Over time as the kingdom grew, the monarchy department built roads to connect the capital to the surrounding villages like Sanbi West and Sanbi East. When the time came to construct the main highway between Sanbi and Forway, the southern point was chosen, including to house the Forway embassy.

## Guards

![](../Behind%20The%20Scenes/Illustrations/Guards/Sanbi%20Guards.png)

Sanbi Guards are flexible guards who can use knives, Nari gels, and Nari bombs to control a nuisance quickly or to get the fuck away if it’s too much for them to handle. Their large bows, combined with nari gels, allow them to have more varied battling techniques; they can be sneakier, have increased muscle mass, or even possess arrows poisoned with debilitating nari gels. There is a reason their training often emphasizes the “everything is a weapon” mentality, and so should you if you feel like disturbing the peace of their kingdom.

## Names

Sanbi names are often the most unique. Unlike other kingdoms, you can change it as much as you like. So much so, that the Sanbi Royals have a special subsidy contract with Forway to lower the cost of badge remakes for their citizens. Meanwhile, Forway officials are still dealing with the aneurysm-inducing problem of keeping track of previous names tied to the soul signature without ballooning costs.

Name characteristics are often akin to Brazilian Indians' names or Indian names as well. Sanbians like cool distinct names, especially one that speaks to your soul. Be it that kinda name that makes you want to scream out loud in the middle of the street on a rainy stormy night, or the one that you proudly have to repeat over and over because nobody can fucking understand how to write or say it.
