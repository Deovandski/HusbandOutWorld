# FAQ

**What is A Husband Out of This World about?**

It’s a dark fantasy isekai story centered on Harry, a stranger whose soul gets misbound during a botched summoning experiment—ending up reborn in a high‑magic world with no memory

**What genre blend does the series fall into?**

It’s a dark fantasy ecchi isekai, mixing magical ritual, romantic entanglements, psychological twist, sensual undertones and heavy world‑building

**What tone and themes can I expect?**

Expect a gritty, emotionally charged journey full of political intrigue, ritual violence, sensual undertones, and a blend of science‑styled magic and soul metaphysics .

**Who is the main character?**

You follow Harry, caught between surviving a soul experiment gone wrong and navigating unexpected entanglements with royalty and magic .

**How does Harry end up in this new world?**

After perishing from our mortal plane, his detached soul began to soul die. At some point, two Sanbi scientists devised a summoning ritual intended to create the perfect body in an alternate reality. However, it went awry, and the botched ritual captured Harry’s soul and brought him a torn identity.

**Is the story complete or ongoing?**

The webcomic prologue is completed and freely available, thanks to Kickstarter support. The final light novel volume (Vol 4) is in progress.

**What format is it available in?**

The story exists both as a light novel (with full‑page color art in each sub‑chapter) and a webcomic, featuring prologue/chapter structure and stylized charisma

**Where can I read or buy it?**

Read the webcomic for free (hosted on the official site, Webtoon, and Tapas). The light novel—like Vol 1: Failed Experiment—is available as an e‑book or paperback via the store

**Who are the creative team behind it?**

Written by Deovandski Skibinski Junior and illustrated by Mitsukiven (Vols 1 & 2) and Feiourm (Vols 3, 4 & webcomic), the creative team blends sharp world‑building with striking visuals
