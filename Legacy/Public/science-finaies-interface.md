# Science - Finaies Interface

## Finaies Interface

![](../Behind%20The%20Scenes/Illustrations/Soul%20Energy%20Interfaces/Interface%20Logograms%20-%20Finaies.png)

The primordial interface of what we know as the Darfaries interface nowadays. Some say it’s the start of all interfaces as well, but that never left the ranting books of scientists as proof of that has been quite elusive.

Why is the interface symbology so small and specific? The consensus believes that it is related to the nature-focused and simplistic minds of Finaies.

## Casting

![](../Behind%20The%20Scenes/Illustrations/Races/Finaies.png)

Unlike complex casting rituals of specific interfaces such as Darfaries and Buhraies, their interface resembles animals like Bagarisa. The casting is done with a simple, singular hand movement matching the desired output, and voila! The spell comes out swiftly and aggressively out of their stubbed hands.
