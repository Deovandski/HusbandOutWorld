# Afterwords

It feels surreal how much this project pushed me to grow and challenge myself! If you are curious, you can see my evolution throughout the project by reading the Light Novel Afterwords:

## Vol 1 Afterword - 2022

Hi! Nice to meet you! I’m Deo! Hope you had fun reading!

It will be weird to say this, but I’m actually not big into reading—I know, right? What a weird thing to say coming from me, speaking inside your head as you read the afterword of my first book.

Well, but here we are! Somehow! Funny story actually: it all started right before sunrise on October 15, 2019, when I had an extremely vivid dream of me being isekai-ed. The dream was so vivid I could smell, touch, and even taste things, but I startled awake right at the climax plot point! That sucked, but as I woke up, I suddenly felt this extreme urge, as if an outside force steered my thoughts and actions, to sit down and start writing as if my life depended on it.

But of course, things would never go that easy—not long after, I met a huge roadblock with one of the characters as I literally couldn’t visualize her. Her name was Magi, and even with a backstory and personality written for her, I simply reached a point where I thought I had to give up. However, I’m known for being a persistent asshole, so I decided to approach the problem creatively. That’s when I went to Freelancer.com and posted a contest titled “I need an anime illustrator to bring one of my characters to life.” If you are curious about my mental state at that point in time, then you can still look it up—needless to say, that conquest led me to meet Mitsukiven, an awesome illustrator that I worked with ever since that fateful day to bring countless characters, and world lore to life! She was a pillar in allowing my imagination to grow stronger, the world to feel more vivid, and the characters to become more real!

I also would like to thank my friends and family! My parents were always there to lend support, including Fluffy (the family doggo) as well! The cat I adopted while writing this book didn’t help me as much, possibly because her tsundere behavior is too strong, hence her proper name of Maddog. She had to return to the shelter, but then I found a little precious furball called Peeta! He’s so cuddly! I’m probably spoiling him too much, but I’m not even ashamed to say that. Anyway, this was an opportunity cost for sure, but also something that I really wanted to do. Thank you all who supported me, even while I was dealing with the worst parts of my GAD. Also, special thanks to the thirty some beta readers, and Bookbaby.com who helped me prepare for my first book launch!

Last, there are least twelve books planned (correlating to the first three story arcs). That is why I am teasing you with all this world lore, as the plan is to have Harry, and you, grow up in this world together! 

I am planning to release one per year, at least for now, since I also work full-time developing software, plus I need time to go fishing, gaming, meditate, and hit up the gym! But if enough of you out there have fun reading this book, then I will certainly consider making this opportunity my top priority.

For more information, visit the website below! I made available a glossary of sorts for those of you who really want to learn a bit more about the world, but do not want to wait for the next book. The Glossary there will be kept mostly up to date with what Harry knows, but you could likely infer some spoilers if read too much into it… just saying ;)

https://www.ahusbandoutofthisworld.com/

Regards,

Deo (デオ)

  
  

## Vol 2 Afterword - 2023

Thank you! I hope you enjoyed the second book! I think it’s better and just slightly less meandering than book one! Going through the hurdles of making something, publishing, advertising, and seeing people enjoying and giving feedback gave Mitsuken and me more perspectives and a deeper appreciation of the effort made by others in the entertainment space. These two books are the culmination of thousands of hours between us, after all. I’m glad I’m still working with her after all these years! Also, a shout-out to my parents and friends who supported me through this journey! Especially since everyone had to deal with me during one of my worst life chapters struggling with GAD. Emphasis on had as I haven’t had panic attacks or had the need to take medicine in more than six months already. Life is good! And can’t forget Peeta! He loves to yell at my ear, but he’s a cute smol boye with long whiskers, so it’s all forgiven.

I guess since I might still have some people’s attention, let me drop a hot take on my fellow Japanese artists: I love isekai, but I fucking hate that nearly every one of them has good vs. evil or overall heavy religious elements, or the same goddam species like elves, dwarves, etc. Sometimes even the same-looking cities too! Also, shouting out actions/spell names alongside striking similarly magical systems with that bland-looking circular-ish magical symbols is tiring—I mean, why not go ahead and create an asekai (同世界) category for all these anime akin to samey-tasting junk food? Because all these asekai stores are tarnishing the definition of what an isekai (異世界) is actually supposed to be about! At least, that’s what I think. I mean, I know companies want to milk this genre while it’s hot, but people can only take so much junk food before they get outright sick of it.

Aiight! Let me step off my high egua for a second here. I am not shitting on them because I love junk food like any other obese gun-loving American, but I don’t want to eat that daily! My LDL is already high enough as is! I can only handle so many asekai animes in a row before I lose faith in the genre! I want more options, for fuck’s sake! Well, I guess that’s another reason I decided to throw my hat in the ring with this series after that fateful day, October 15, 2019, when I woke up from the extremely vivid dream I had.

So let’s get back to the important stuff! It makes me feel like I know what I am doing now that I stepped my toes into the entertainment industry, but not really. My goal is to watch this series as an anime adaptation one day. However, I see lots of impediments that might prevent that from becoming a reality, with the strongest being my lack of penetration in Eastern marketplaces like BookWalker because I am self-publishing. Another big one is the lack of public interest, since at the end of the day, I am writing what I think is a pretty atypical isekai story, while the series name is kinda generic as fuck—maybe I dug myself a hole on that, but that name just popped into my head early on during R&D, and it stuck ever since. I spent months trying to come up with a better title, but somehow this name just kept coming back because it would make a whole lot of sense when Harry’s story eventually wrapped up.

I gotta be realistic about this project as a whole too. It’s demanding time, money, and mental capacity as this whole thing requires a lot more than just writing, such as project management, accounting, and marketing. I for sure have the passion for pushing through the first arc, about four or five books, in order to give this series a survival chance in the endless sea of light and web novels. However, once that’s done, I will sit down and decide on its future. If there are enough people that care for it, then I will keep trucking along. Also, I had quite a few people inquire about manga or audiobook versions of this light novel, and to those people, I say the thought already crossed my mind, but that requires more mental capacity than I can offer at the moment.

I for sure would love to see the whole thing come alive, but that doesn’t only depend on me. It would be really appreciated if you could follow the book on social media and leave a review on Amazon, Wattpad, or whatever other media we publish in. If you can, spread the word too! More people having fun with the book means Mitsuken and I can focus more time on it! It’s a win-win!

Regards, 

Deo (デオ)

Vol 4 Afterword - 2027

Close to ten years—that’s how long it took to this exact moment! These ramblings you read at the end of Vol 4. 

I am not the same man who, on a fateful day in October 2019, had a vivid dream and started writing the first draft of this story because my intuition told me to! I remember how life-or-death the sensation felt, and now, looking back, I understand why: I was deeply unfulfilled. Sorry to the people who saw me struggle and couldn’t help back then; I didn't know either. Only now, at 34, I understand I’m AuDHD as fuck! You can’t expect others to fix your shit! You're the only one who can look inward and figure it out. Therapy can help only if the therapist does not enable you, but rather challenges your arse, and you’re willing to listen and process against your inner reality.

Amongst all the things I learned recently, I now see how much energy I poured into people who were just energy vampires. It was also interesting seeing firsthand how someone can be double your age but still be mentally an immature, spoiled child, unable to have an adult conversation, especially people who hide behind spiritual bypassing. There’s nothing I can do about my past innocent self, but I can use the present to continually push myself forward, and that included shedding away my social influencer side, for I had learned a lot about it; I had already achieved the main goal of facing that attention-seeking, external-validation shadow side within me, to the point that it was no longer fun or worthwhile continuing because it kept me stuck in hypervigilance mode and I was no longer deriving any new lessons as to how to sell my book series or my artist side.

People who know me have heard my core mantras, and you can probably guess they're fueled by AuDHD in one way or another:

* “Either full commit or eat shit!”
* “I don’t let it happen! I make it happen!”
* “I’m growing, and I will keep growing until the day I die!”
* “Silence is the poison of the soul instead of communication, assuming you still have faith in the other person!”

And now I have two new ones that I'm integrating into my soul:

* “Never wrestle with pigs. You both get dirty, and the pig likes it!”
* “I’m not dying in the waiting room of someone else’s stagnant life!”
* “Action or inaction, it’s all seed. Mistakes and lessons feed the next harvest. You eat what you grow, even when it tastes like shit.”

To the people in my inner circle who helped each other grow, and with whom I've developed a trusted bond: you are awesome folks; I will always be there for you, a ping away. To my awesome mother and father, I love you two! To my two illustrators, you became the sisters I never had! To my friends who supported me throughout, even just to shitpost on the story, or the ones that lent a hand with the logistics, thank you for the laughs! To my kitty Peeta, you're the goodest of boys!

To everyone I crossed paths with, especially at cons, from artists to directors and publishers: Thank you for letting me be part of the life experience as an artist! It was hella fun! And fulfilling too! Being a guest on Anime Fargo 2024, being deeply embedded in Con Quest 2026, and even participating in the premiere of Book That Goes Wrong were things my inner child never thought I would get to. Now it’s a treasured memory and part of my journey!

—Since we are on this topic of newfound memories, to all the women I shared a bed with since my AuDHD unmasking, thank you for indulging my shadow side while I was on a rebound spree due to being mentally abused and starved emotionally and physically! For the way my brain works, that was necessary —alongside a lot of self-reflection—to re-stabilize myself and grow, although I do confess the pendulum swung hard, and I tested my very limits by going into the deepest, lustful desires of my ADHD driven soul, even jumping without hesitation into experiencing six women at once, flooding every sense into purely carnal surreality, continuously trying to please more than being pleased.

This same energy pushed me to go skydiving as well, a dream I'd had forever, and I was going to do it when I finished the series… So I did.

Now that I no longer feel in survival mode, my original goal is to settle down with a woman I can deeply bond with, cut all contact with girls I was emotionally or physically tangled with, and raise a supportive family together. It's resurfacing once again, but I’m no longer as innocent, and I wonder if I will ever find a woman I truly feel safe creating a life with and raising them into this wild, weird world, as I’ve learned that I push people away romantically when I smell ambiguity, and I tend to feel more dissonance with someone than love the more they struggle with their feelings—I’m writing this to other neurodivergent people as I do believe this is something we struggle a lot with—trust.

If this series somehow explodes, I will circle back and create the Omnibus with double the illustrations and hire the same ghostwriter who did Vol 4—it’s obvious, so I am not going to sit here and mentally masturbate people by saying I wrote Vol 4 because I clearly didn’t if you compare with my Vol 1-3 writing. I mean, I never was a good writer to begin with; my strength was always being a Creative Director because it’s not that different from being a Solution Architect! Still, why use a ghostwriter on Vol 4? Fair question, and it’s simple: I wanted to experiment and analyze the results!

And no. I didn't use AI to write or come up with the story; Vol 1 was published back on June 30, 2022! I did use Claude much later on to cross-check against the world lore and to help create the West March D&D Campaign, which you should totally check out! And no, we never used AI to generate the illustrations either.

Anyways, here I go again, babbling on too much, usual! Some things really don’t change, but at least I am aware of it! Fuck it! For all I know, this is goodbye unless you see me out in the wild or fate somehow drags me back to write more of this wild story! Until that happens, or I just die, I leave you with my favorite Hávamál quote:

Deyr fé

Deyja frændr

Deyr sjalfr it sama
