# Vol 4 Draft 2: World Lore Q&A

The ghostwriter's Draft 2 of Vol 4 (*BAL247, Part 2 of 2*, reviewed by Deovandski from 2026-11-16 to 11-18) got some things wrong about the world. Deovandski left 236 review comments. Many of them explain how the world actually works, and until now that only lived in his head. This file writes it down and uses it to strengthen the World Lore (GitHub issue #107).

Each entry has:

- **Question:** what the draft got wrong, asked as a question about the world.
- **Draft 2 said:** a line or two of the ghostwriter's passage that the comment was attached to.
- **Deovandski's comments:** copied word for word from the review, with their comment numbers. Line breaks inside a comment are shown as a space or " / ".
- **Answer:** the facts from those comments, in his words, cleaned up. Nothing added.
- **Already in the World Lore:** what the docs said before this file, with the file name.
- **Proposal:** the exact lines the AI suggests adding, and where. Anything that conflicts with the docs is asked as a question instead of being decided.
- **Approval notes:** Deovandski's decision. Nothing goes into the World Lore docs until this is filled in. Once it is, the lines that went in are listed under **Applied**.

Comments that are only about the writing (tone, pacing, matching the illustrations, who says which line) aren't in this file. It only covers facts about the world and its people.

Every World Lore line added from this file ends with "(See World Lore Q&A › Vol 4 Draft 2, Q#.)" so it can be traced back here.

## Q1. How do fost lamps work? Do they have a wick, smoke, or a way to turn them off?

- #73: *There's no smoke. Just steam. She coughed from the funky smell from this place*
- #29: *There's no "turning off the lights" in this world. See previous mention on how Fost lamps works. Also, it's daytime. You can have him shut off the blue window curtains*

```

```
